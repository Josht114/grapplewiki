The gi adds cloth grips and collar chokes; no-gi is faster, more slippery and leans more on wrestling and leg locks.

## Key points

1. Gi: collar chokes, lapel guards, slower and more grip-heavy.
2. No-gi: underhooks, wrist control, front headlocks, heel hooks.
3. Many techniques work in both with small grip changes.

## Notes

Gi-only: [[Bow and arrow]], [[Cross collar choke]], [[Bottom lapel guard (worm)]]. No-gi favourites: [[Darce choke]], [[Heel hook]].
