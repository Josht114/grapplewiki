---
Base is how stable you are against being moved; posture is a straight spine with the head up. Losing either gets you swept or submitted.

## Key points

1. Wide, low base when on top.
2. Keep your head above your hips in guard.
3. Avoid posting hands where they can be trapped.
4. Re-establish posture before you attack.
