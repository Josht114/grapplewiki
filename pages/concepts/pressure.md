Putting your weight where the opponent has to carry it, so they tire and can't move their hips.

## Key points

1. Weight through your hips or chest, not your hands.
2. Pin the hips and the head: that stops most movement.
3. Stay on your toes so your weight goes into them, not the mat.
4. Pressure first, then pass or submit.

## Notes

Pressure passes include [[Smash pass]], [[Over under pass]] and [[Double under pass]].
