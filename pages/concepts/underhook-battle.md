---
name: Underhook battle
order: 50
---
Fighting to get your arm under the opponent's arm. The person with the underhook usually wins the position.

## Key points

1. Pummel: swim your arm inside theirs.
2. Use the underhook to lift and get to your side or knees.
3. Deny their underhook with a crossface or whizzer.
4. Matters most in half guard and standing.
