name: Grip fighting
order: 10
---
The battle for the grips you want and against the grips they want. Whoever controls the grips controls the exchange.

## Key points

1. Break grips early, before they are set.
2. Grip with a purpose: each grip should lead to a technique.
3. Two-on-one any grip you need to break.
4. In no-gi, wrists, collar ties, underhooks and overhooks replace cloth grips.
