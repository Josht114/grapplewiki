---
Using bones (forearms, shins, straight arms) instead of muscles to hold space between you and the opponent.

## Key points

1. Bone-to-body: forearm on the neck, hand on the hip.
2. Frame first, then move your hips away from the frame.
3. A frame is for making space, not for pushing.
4. Rebuild frames as soon as one collapses.

## Notes

Key for [[Side control escape (frame & shrimp)]] and guard retention (see the Defense section of passes like the [[Knee cut pass]]).
