Common BJJ terms used across this wiki.

## Notes

Base — stability against being moved. See [[Base and posture]].
Crossface — shoulder pressed across the opponent's face to turn their head away.
Frame — a bone structure that holds space. See [[Frames]].
Hook — a foot or leg wrapped behind the opponent's leg or inside their thigh.
Knee line — the line of the knee; whoever controls it controls leg entanglements.
Pass — getting past the opponent's legs to a pin.
Post — a hand or foot put on the mat to stop being swept.
Seatbelt — over-under grip from behind. See [[Seatbelt back take]].
Shrimp — hip escape, moving your hips away on your side.
Sweep — reversing from bottom guard to top.
Underhook — your arm under the opponent's armpit. See [[Underhook battle]].
Whizzer — an overhook used to stop an underhook.
