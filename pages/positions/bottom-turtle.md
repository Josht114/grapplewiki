You are on hands and knees, often after avoiding a pass. Keep elbows to knees, protect the neck, and recover guard or go for a single leg.

## Key points

1. Elbows to knees: no gaps for hooks or arms.
2. Chin tucked and collar covered.
3. Don't stay still: sit out, roll or recover guard.
4. Grab their wrist or a leg when they reach.

## Principles

### Goal
- Don't stay here: recover guard or come up to your feet before they get hooks in.

### Base and posture
- Elbows glued to your knees, chin tucked, hips low.
- Weight forward on your knees and elbows, not on your hands.
- Keep moving: a still turtle gets broken down.

### Hands and arms
- Hands near your neck or guarding your wrists; don't post a hand far out on the mat.
- Grab and control their hand on your far wrist or hip.
- Gi: guard your collar from the clock choke and collar grips.

### Frames
- Elbows to knees is the frame: it blocks the hooks and the seatbelt.

### Priorities
1. Elbows to knees, neck protected.
2. Get out: sit out, granby roll to guard, or come up into a single leg or stand.
3. If hooks start to go in, block them with your elbows and turn toward guard.

### Common mistakes
- **Reaching out or posting a hand:** they take the arm or the seatbelt.
- **Staying still:** they break you down and get hooks in.
- **Head high and elbows wide:** gives the clock choke and the back.
