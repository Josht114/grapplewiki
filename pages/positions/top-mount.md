Sitting on the opponent's torso with knees on the mat. One of the most dominant positions, with chokes and arm attacks available.

## Key points

1. Low mount: grapevine their legs and keep your chest heavy when they bridge.
2. High mount: walk your knees up into their armpits to set up arm attacks.
3. Post your hands wide when they bridge, then re-settle.
4. When they push on your chest, climb higher or attack the arm.
