---
name: Top half guard (deep half)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-half-guard-deep
---
Opponent is under your hips, hugging your trapped leg. Your base is in danger, so stay wide and heavy and free the leg.

## Key points

1. Widen the free leg for base.
2. Keep your weight back over your hips, not forward.
3. Use a whizzer on their far arm.
4. Back-step or push their head away to free the leg.
