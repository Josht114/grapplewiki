---
name: Top half guard (deep half)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-half-guard-deep
---
Opponent is under your hips, hugging your trapped leg. Your base is in danger, so stay wide and heavy and free the leg.

## Key points

1. Widen the free leg for base.
2. Keep your weight back over your hips, not forward.
3. Use a whizzer on their far arm.
4. Back-step or push their head away to free the leg.

## Principles

### Goal
- Keep your base and free your leg before they sweep you.

### Base and posture
- Free leg wide and posted for base.
- Weight back over your hips, not forward over their shoulders.
- Keep your hips low; they need you light to lift you.

### Hands and arms
- Whizzer (overhook) their far arm and drive it down.
- Other hand pushes their head or shoulder away from your hips.
- Gi: grip the belt or the back of the pants to pin them under you.

### Priorities
1. Widen your base and sit your weight back.
2. Whizzer the far arm.
3. Push their head away, or back-step, to free the trapped leg.
4. Pass to side control once the leg is out.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaning forward:** they waiter sweep you.
- **Narrow base:** easy to tip over.
- **Ignoring the whizzer:** they take your back.
