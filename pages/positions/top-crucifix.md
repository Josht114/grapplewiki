---
Both of the opponent's arms are trapped, one between your legs and one by your arm, usually from turtle. Their neck is unprotected.

## Key points

1. From top turtle, hook their near arm with your legs.
2. Roll or slide to their side, keeping the arm trapped.
3. Control their other arm with your own arm.
4. Attack the neck or extend the trapped arm.
