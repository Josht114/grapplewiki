---
name: Top side control (kesa gatame)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-side-control-kesa
---
Seated beside the opponent, hip to their ribs, arm around their neck and their near arm trapped. A judo-style pin with arm locks and chokes.

## Key points

1. Sit hip-to-hip against their ribs, facing their head.
2. Wrap their head with your near arm.
3. Trap their near arm under your armpit.
4. Spread your legs wide for base and keep your head low.

## Principles

### Goal
- Pin them with your weight and the head wrap, then attack the trapped arm or the neck.

### Base and posture
- Sit hip to hip against their ribs, facing their head.
- Legs spread wide (one forward, one back) for base against the bridge.
- Head low, close to theirs.

### Hands and arms
- Near arm wraps their head.
- Their near arm trapped under your armpit, gripped at the wrist or sleeve.
- Gi: grab your own pants or their collar behind their head.

### Priorities
1. Settle the head wrap and the trapped arm.
2. Base wide against the bridge.
3. Attack the trapped arm (armlock) or the neck.
4. Switch to side control if they turn in.

### Common mistakes
- **Narrow base:** they bridge and roll you.
- **Head high:** they push you off.
- **Loose trapped arm:** they pull it free and take your back.
