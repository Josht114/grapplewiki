---
name: Top side control (kesa gatame)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-side-control-kesa
---
Seated beside the opponent, hip to their ribs, arm around their neck and their near arm trapped. A judo-style pin with arm locks and chokes.

## Key points

1. Sit hip-to-hip against their ribs, facing their head.
2. Wrap their head with your near arm.
3. Trap their near arm under your armpit.
4. Spread your legs wide for base and keep your head low.
