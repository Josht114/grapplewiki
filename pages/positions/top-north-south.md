Chest-to-chest with your head toward the opponent's hips. Strong pressure pin with chokes and kimuras.

## Key points

1. Chest to chest, your hips low and back.
2. Keep their arms away from their head (or trap one for the kimura).
3. Stay on your toes so your weight goes into them.
4. Walk around to side control as they turn.
