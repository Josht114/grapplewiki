Chest-to-chest with your head toward the opponent's hips. Strong pressure pin with chokes and kimuras.

## Key points

1. Chest to chest, your hips low and back.
2. Keep their arms away from their head (or trap one for the kimura).
3. Stay on your toes so your weight goes into them.
4. Walk around to side control as they turn.

## Principles

### Goal
- Hold them flat with your chest and attack the north-south choke or a kimura.

### Base and posture
- Chest on their chest, hips low, legs sprawled back or knees wide.
- Keep your hips away from their arms and knees.
- Move around the head to side control rather than letting them turn.

### Hands and arms
- Arms under their armpits or around their arms to kill the frames.
- Gi: belt or pants grips at the hips.
- For the choke, your arm wraps the neck and your chest drives down on it.

### Priorities
1. Pin them flat and kill the elbow frames.
2. Attack the north-south choke or a kimura.
3. Follow to side control when they turn or frame.

### Common mistakes
- **Hips high:** they get their knees in.
- **Too far forward:** they bridge and roll you over their head.
- **Letting their elbows get to your hips:** they make space and turn in.
