---
name: Bottom lasso guard
side: bottom
counterpart: top-lasso
---
Your leg wraps around the opponent's arm with a sleeve grip. A strong control guard for sweeps and stalling the pass.

## Principles

### Goal
- Control their arm and posture with the lasso, then sweep or stall the pass.

### Base and posture
- On your back or hip, turned toward the lasso side.
- Lasso leg wraps around the outside of their arm, shin across their biceps and foot near their armpit or hip.

### Hands and arms
- Same-side hand grips the sleeve of the lassoed arm, close to the wrist.
- Other hand on the other sleeve, collar or ankle.
- Gi: sleeve grips are what make the lasso strong. No-gi: a wrist grip and the leg wrap do less, so pair it with an ankle grip.

### Frames
- Free foot on their hip or biceps keeps distance.
- The lasso leg pushes their arm outward and away, pulling their posture off-centre.

### Priorities
1. Set the lasso with a tight sleeve grip.
2. Free foot on their hip or the other arm.
3. Off-balance them with the lasso leg.
4. Sweep or take the back.

### Common mistakes
- **Loose sleeve grip:** they pull the arm out.
- **Lasso too shallow, foot on the biceps only:** they strip it.
- **Lying flat:** they stack you and pass.
