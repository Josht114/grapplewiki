---
name: Top half guard
side: top
counterpart: bottom-half-guard
---
You are on top with one leg trapped between the opponent's legs. Win the underhook and crossface, flatten them, and free the leg to pass.

## Key points

1. Crossface and underhook: flatten them on their back.
2. Keep your weight forward and your hips low.
3. Free your trapped knee step by step (knee slice or switch base).
4. Don't let them get an underhook or come up to their side.

## Principles

### Goal
- Flatten them, free your leg and pass to side control or mount.

### Base and posture
- Hips low and chest heavy on their chest.
- Free leg sprawled back for base; trapped leg's knee pointing out.
- Keep your head low and tight to theirs so they can't post up or get under you.

### Hands and arms
- Crossface and a far underhook, or a near underhook.
- Win the underhook battle: if they get it, whizzer or pummel to get it back.
- Gi: far collar or near pants grips.

### Priorities
1. Win the underhook and crossface; flatten them.
2. Kill the knee shield.
3. Free the trapped leg (knee slide, step over).
4. Settle side control or mount.

### Common mistakes
- **Giving them the underhook:** they come up or sweep.
- **Posture too high:** they get a knee shield in.
- **Freeing the leg before they're flat:** they re-guard.
