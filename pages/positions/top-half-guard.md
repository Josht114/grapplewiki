---
name: Top half guard
side: top
counterpart: bottom-half-guard
---
You are on top with one leg trapped between the opponent's legs. Win the underhook and crossface, flatten them, and free the leg to pass.

## Key points

1. Crossface and underhook: flatten them on their back.
2. Keep your weight forward and your hips low.
3. Free your trapped knee step by step (knee slice or switch base).
4. Don't let them get an underhook or come up to their side.
