name: Bottom mount (S-mount)
side: bottom
counterpart: top-mount-s
---
Opponent is in S-mount with an arm isolated. The arm bar is coming, so protect the elbow and escape before they swing the leg over.

## Key points

1. Clasp your hands and keep the elbows tight.
2. Bridge toward the knee by your head.
3. Turn into them and push that knee down.
4. Escape the hips and recover half guard.

## Principles

### Goal
- Protect the elbow and escape before they swing the leg over.

### Base and posture
- Turn into them, onto the side of the trapped arm; flat on your back is where the armbar finishes.
- Feet on the mat, ready to bridge.

### Hands and arms
- Clasp your hands (or grab your own lapel in the gi) so the trapped arm can't be straightened.
- Keep the trapped elbow tight and pointing down toward the mat, not up at them.
- Free arm pushes the knee by your head.

### Frames
- Free hand or forearm on the knee by your head, pushing it past your face as you bridge.

### Priorities
1. Clasp your hands, elbows tight.
2. Bridge toward the knee by your head.
3. Push that knee down past your head and turn into them.
4. Escape the hips and recover half guard.

### Common mistakes
- **Letting go of your hands:** they straighten the arm.
- **Pushing their knee with the trapped arm:** you extend the elbow for them.
- **Bridging straight up:** they ride it and swing the leg over.
