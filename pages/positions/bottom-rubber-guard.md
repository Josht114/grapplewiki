High closed guard with a leg over the shoulder, held by the arm. Controls posture for omoplatas, triangles and gogoplatas.

## Principles

### Goal
- Lock their posture down with your high leg, then attack the omoplata, triangle or gogoplata.

### Base and posture
- Closed guard pulled high on their back first; you can't climb from a low guard.
- Hips up and angled out to the side of the high leg.
- Needs flexible hips; don't force the leg if it hurts your knee.

### Hands and arms
- Hold your shin or ankle over their back with the opposite arm.
- Other hand controls their wrist on that side so they can't post or posture.
- Gi or no-gi: the grips are on your own leg, so it works the same in both.

### Frames
- Your shin across their upper back is the frame: it keeps their head down and their posture broken.

### Priorities
1. Break their posture in closed guard.
2. Climb a leg over their shoulder and catch your shin.
3. Control their free arm.
4. Attack: omoplata, triangle or gogoplata.

### Common mistakes
- **Climbing before posture is broken:** they stand up and stack you.
- **Letting go of the shin:** the leg slips off and they posture.
- **Forcing it with stiff hips:** knee injury. Work flexibility first.
