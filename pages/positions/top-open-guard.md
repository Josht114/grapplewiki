You are passing an open guard, either standing or on your knees. Standing favours speed passes like the toreando; kneeling favours pressure passes like the knee cut, over-under and smash pass.

## Key points

1. Neutralise the feet and grips before you try to pass.
2. Standing: control the knees or pants and use speed and angles.
3. Kneeling: get your hips heavy and pin their hips and head.
4. Finish every pass chest to chest in side control.
