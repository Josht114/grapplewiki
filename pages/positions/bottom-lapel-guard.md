---
name: Bottom lapel guard (worm)
side: bottom
counterpart: top-lapel-guard
---
Gi guards that use the opponent's own lapel, often wrapped around their lead leg (worm guard). Strong control of distance and the lead leg.

## Key points

1. Pull out the opponent's lapel.
2. Feed it around their lead leg and grip it.
3. Use the lapel grip to anchor and steer the leg.
4. Sweep or take the back when they lose balance.
