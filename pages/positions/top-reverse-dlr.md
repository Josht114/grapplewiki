---
name: Top reverse De La Riva guard
side: top
counterpart: bottom-reverse-dlr
---
Opponent's hook is on the inside of your lead leg. Watch for berimbolos and kiss-of-the-dragon back takes.

## Principles

### Goal
- Kill the hook and pass before they berimbolo or take your back.

### Base and posture
- Hips back, knees bent, stance wide, weight on the hooked leg.
- Stay square to them; turning your back toward them invites the spin under you.
- Drop the hooked knee toward the mat to flatten the hook.

### Hands and arms
- Break the ankle grip on your lead leg.
- Control their far knee or hips so they can't invert.
- Gi: a belt or collar grip stops the berimbolo and kiss-of-the-dragon spin.

### Priorities
1. Grips: break their ankle grip and get your own on the belt, collar or hips.
2. Kill the hook.
3. Pass to the side away from the hook.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaning forward over them:** they berimbolo under you.
- **Turning your back to them:** kiss of the dragon to the back.
- **Passing with the hook still in:** they follow you and re-guard or sweep.
