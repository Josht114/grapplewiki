---
name: Bottom saddle (inside sankaku)
side: bottom
counterpart: top-saddle
---
You have your legs triangled around the opponent's thigh from the inside. The main heel hook control position.

## Key points

1. Bring your legs inside and around their leg.
2. Triangle your legs across their hip.
3. Squeeze your knees to control the knee line.
4. Clamp the heel and attack.

## Principles

### Goal
- Control their knee line and finish a heel hook.

### Base and posture
- Legs triangled across their hip from the inside, your hips tight to their knee.
- Knees pinched together so their knee can't slide out past your hips.
- Hips turned toward their trapped leg.

### Hands and arms
- Clamp the heel in the crook of your elbow, the other hand locked on your wrist.
- Before the grip, keep their free leg away with a hand or foot so they can't kick your legs off.
- IBJJF gi rules ban heel hooks: in the gi use the position for straight ankle locks or sweeps.

### Frames
- Free foot or hand on their free leg stops them turning to face you.

### Priorities
1. Legs in and triangled; knees pinched.
2. Control their free leg.
3. Hand-fight for the heel.
4. Clamp the heel and finish with a hip turn.

### Common mistakes
- **Loose knees:** their knee slides out and the position is gone.
- **Hips too far from their knee:** no control.
- **Grabbing the heel without controlling the free leg:** they kick you off.
