---
name: Top half guard (knee shield / Z guard)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-half-guard-knee-shield
---
Opponent has a knee across your chest as a shield. Flatten the knee and get your chest to theirs to pass.

## Key points

1. Grab their collar or head to stop them sitting up.
2. Drive the shield knee down to the mat.
3. Win the crossface and underhook.
4. Free your leg and pass to side control.
