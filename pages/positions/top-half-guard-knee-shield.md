---
name: Top half guard (knee shield / Z guard)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-half-guard-knee-shield
---
Opponent has a knee across your chest as a shield. Flatten the knee and get your chest to theirs to pass.

## Key points

1. Grab their collar or head to stop them sitting up.
2. Drive the shield knee down to the mat.
3. Win the crossface and underhook.
4. Free your leg and pass to side control.

## Principles

### Goal
- Flatten the knee shield and get chest to chest to pass.

### Base and posture
- Hips low, chest heavy, weight leaning into the shield.
- Keep your trapped knee pointing out to stop them going deep.

### Hands and arms
- Grab their collar or head (gi or no-gi) to stop them sitting up.
- Then crossface and underhook.

### Priorities
1. Grab their collar or head.
2. Drive the shield knee down to the mat.
3. Win the crossface and underhook.
4. Free your leg and pass to side control.

### Common mistakes
- **Posture too high:** they keep the shield and the distance.
- **Ignoring the frame on your neck:** they keep you away.
- **Passing before the knee is down:** they re-guard.
