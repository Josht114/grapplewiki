name: Top mount (S-mount)

High mount with one knee up by the opponent's head and the other shin under their shoulder, forming an S. Isolates an arm for the arm bar.

## Key points

1. From high mount, step one foot up beside their shoulder.
2. Slide the other shin under their head or shoulder.
3. Lean your weight onto their chest.
4. Isolate the near arm and swing into the arm bar.

## Principles

### Goal
- Keep the near arm isolated and finish the armbar, or follow them to the back if they turn away.

### Base and posture
- One foot up beside their head, knee high; the other shin under their shoulder with that knee tight to their side.
- Hips heavy on their chest: lean forward onto them, not back.
- Knees pinched together around their arm so they can't turn into you.

### Hands and arms
- Hug the isolated arm to your chest, their elbow pointing up.
- Free hand posts on the mat by their head when they bridge, or pushes their head away.
- Gi: a collar grip on the far side stops them turning in and sets up a choke.

### Priorities
1. Climb from high mount: foot up by their shoulder, shin under the other shoulder.
2. Settle your weight on their chest.
3. Isolate the near arm and hug it.
4. Swing the leg over the face and fall back for the armbar.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaning back before the arm is trapped:** they bridge and turn in.
- **Loose grip on the arm:** they pull the elbow back to their ribs.
- **Knees apart:** they turn into you and escape their hips.
