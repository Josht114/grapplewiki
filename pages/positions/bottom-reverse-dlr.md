Hook on the inside of the opponent's lead leg. Good for retention, berimbolo and kiss of the dragon.

## Principles

### Goal
- Retain guard and set up the berimbolo, kiss of the dragon or a back take.

### Base and posture
- On your back or side, turned toward their lead leg.
- Hook on the inside of their lead leg, wrapped behind the knee.
- Free foot on their far hip or knee.

### Hands and arms
- Grip their ankle or pants.
- Other hand on a sleeve, collar or belt.

### Frames
- The hook and free foot keep them from stepping across to pass.

### Priorities
1. Set the hook and the ankle grip.
2. Upper-body grip.
3. Off-balance them, then berimbolo, kiss of the dragon or sweep.

### Common mistakes
- **Hook too shallow:** they step out.
- **No grips:** they pass easily.
- **Flat with no angle:** you can't follow their movement.
