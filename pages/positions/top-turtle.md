name: Top turtle
side: top
counterpart: bottom-turtle
---
Opponent is on hands and knees. Control the hips and head, then attack the back, chokes or a roll.

## Key points

1. Keep your hips on their hips, chest on their back.
2. Control a wrist or the near arm so they can't post.
3. Keep your knees out of reach so they can't grab a leg.
4. Attack the back, break them down flat, or go for the neck.
