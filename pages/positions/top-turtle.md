name: Top turtle
side: top
counterpart: bottom-turtle
---
Opponent is on hands and knees. Control the hips and head, then attack the back, chokes or a roll.

## Key points

1. Keep your hips on their hips, chest on their back.
2. Control a wrist or the near arm so they can't post.
3. Keep your knees out of reach so they can't grab a leg.
4. Attack the back, break them down flat, or go for the neck.

## Principles

### Goal
- Break them down and take the back, or attack the neck, crucifix or front headlock.

### Base and posture
- Chest heavy on their back, hips low; stay on one side of them.
- Knees beside their hips, not directly behind or on top of them, so a roll doesn't take you over.
- Keep your weight on them, not on your own hands.

### Hands and arms
- Seatbelt, or one hand on the far hip and the other on the far wrist (wrist ride).
- Chop the near arm or pull the far arm to flatten them.
- Gi: belt grip and collar grips (clock choke).

### Priorities
1. Stay heavy and controlled on one side.
2. Break them down: pull an arm out, flatten them.
3. Insert hooks to take the back, or choke, crucifix or roll.
4. If they come up toward you, front headlock.

### Common mistakes
- **Hooks in too early:** they roll and you end up underneath.
- **Sitting high on top of them:** they sit out or granby roll away.
- **Reaching too far under them:** they trap the arm and roll you.
