---
name: Top back take (standing)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-back-take-standing
---
You are behind a standing opponent, usually with a seatbelt or body lock. The goal is to bring them down while keeping their back.

## Principles

### Goal
- Bring them down while you keep their back, then settle hooks or a body triangle on the mat.

### Base and posture
- Chest glued to their back, hips low and tight behind theirs.
- Feet wide and knees bent so they can't sit down or throw you over a shoulder.
- Head tight to their back or beside their head, never out in front.

### Hands and arms
- Seatbelt or a body lock (hands locked around the waist, low).
- Keep your hands away from their hands: they will peel your fingers first.
- Gi: belt or collar grips add control.

### Priorities
1. Lock the seatbelt or body lock and stay tight.
2. Break their stance: mat return, trip the back of a leg or sit them down.
3. Follow them down and insert a hook as you land.
4. If they turn in, keep a body lock and take them down from the front.

### Common mistakes
- **Space between your chest and their back:** they turn in.
- **Standing tall:** they drop their weight and break the grip.
- **Jumping to the back with no control:** they fall back on you.
