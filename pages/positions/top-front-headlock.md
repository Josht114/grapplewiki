---
name: Top front headlock
side: top
counterpart: bottom-front-headlock
---
You control the opponent's head and one arm from the front while they are bent over or on their knees. A hub for the darce, anaconda, guillotine and go-behinds.

## Key points

1. Chin on the back of their head or shoulder.
2. One arm around the head, the other on the far triceps.
3. Keep your hips back so they can't shoot under you.
4. Snap them down, then circle behind or attack the neck.

## Principles

### Goal
- Keep their head below their hips, then go behind to the back or finish a choke (guillotine, darce, anaconda).

### Base and posture
- Hips back and heavy, out of reach of their arms and legs; sprawl if they drive in.
- Chest or shoulder on the back of their head and neck so their head stays down.
- Stay square or slightly to the side of the arm you control, not in front of their legs.

### Hands and arms
- One arm makes a chin strap: hand cupping the chin, forearm across the face or neck.
- The other hand controls the near arm at the elbow or triceps so they can't post or reach your legs.
- Gi: a deep collar grip can replace the chin strap.

### Priorities
1. Snap the head down and sprawl so they can't shoot or stand.
2. Lock the chin strap and the arm.
3. Go behind when they push into you, or choke when their arm or neck opens.
4. If their head comes up past your chest, let go and reset your stance.

### Common mistakes
- **Hips too close:** they grab your legs and shoot or stand up.
- **Loose chin strap:** they lift their head and square up.
- **Head only, no arm:** they post, circle away or roll through.
