---
name: Top front headlock
side: top
counterpart: bottom-front-headlock
---
You control the opponent's head and one arm from the front while they are bent over or on their knees. A hub for the darce, anaconda, guillotine and go-behinds.

## Key points

1. Chin on the back of their head or shoulder.
2. One arm around the head, the other on the far triceps.
3. Keep your hips back so they can't shoot under you.
4. Snap them down, then circle behind or attack the neck.
