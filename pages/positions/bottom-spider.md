Sleeve grips with feet on the biceps. Controls distance and sets up triangles, omoplatas and sweeps.

## Principles

### Goal
- Control distance with your feet on their biceps, then triangle, omoplata or sweep.

### Base and posture
- On your back or hip, angled to one side.
- Both feet on their biceps, or one on the biceps and the other lasso or on the hip.
- Keep your knees bent and pushing.

### Hands and arms
- Sleeve grips, pulling their arms toward you while your feet push.
- Gi only: this guard needs sleeves.

### Frames
- Feet on the biceps are the frames; pull on the sleeves as you push with the feet to make tension.

### Priorities
1. Sleeve grips and feet on the biceps.
2. Push and pull to off-balance them.
3. Triangle, omoplata or sweep.

### Common mistakes
- **Straight legs:** they collapse them.
- **Loose sleeve grips:** they strip them.
- **Flat with no angle:** they pass.
