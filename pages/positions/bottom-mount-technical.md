You turned to your side under mount and their knee is now behind your back. Turning further gives up your back, so turn back in and escape.

## Key points

1. Stop turning away: that gives them your back.
2. Turn back flat toward their posted leg.
3. Frame on the posted knee.
4. Elbow-knee escape to recover half guard.
