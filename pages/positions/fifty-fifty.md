---
name: 50/50
side: neutral
---
Symmetrical leg entanglement where both athletes have the same attacks. Heel exposure and sweeps on both sides.

## Principles

### Goal
- Protect your heel and attack theirs, or come up on top for the sweep.

### Base and posture
- Sit up toward them; lying back exposes your heel.
- Hide your heel: toes pointed, foot tucked against their body.
- Keep your hips close to their knee.

### Hands and arms
- Grip their heel or foot, controlling the ankle.
- Hand-fight their grips on your heel.
- IBJJF gi rules ban heel hooks: in the gi, 50/50 is used for sweeps and straight ankle locks.

### Priorities
1. Hide your heel.
2. Grip their foot.
3. Attack (heel hook in no-gi), or come up on top for the sweep.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaving your heel exposed:** heel hook.
- **Lying flat:** they attack first.
- **Turning the wrong way while they hold your heel:** knee injury.
