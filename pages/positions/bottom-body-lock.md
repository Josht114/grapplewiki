---
name: Bottom body lock (standing)
side: bottom
counterpart: top-body-lock
---
Opponent has their hands locked around your waist while standing. Break the grip and get your hips away before they trip or lift you.

## Key points

1. Get your hips back and widen your base.
2. Frame on their head or neck to create space.
3. Peel the grip apart at the wrists.
4. Win an underhook or whizzer and turn to face them.

## Principles

### Goal
- Break their grip and get your hips away before they trip or lift you.

### Base and posture
- Hips back, base wide, knees bent.
- Weight low and centred.

### Hands and arms
- Peel their grip apart at the wrists.
- Win an underhook or a whizzer to turn into them.

### Frames
- A frame on their head or neck makes space between your hips and theirs.

### Priorities
1. Widen your base and get your hips back.
2. Frame on their head or neck.
3. Break the grip at the wrists.
4. Underhook or whizzer and turn to face them.

### Common mistakes
- **Standing tall:** they lift you.
- **Grabbing their head only:** you leave the waist lock alone.
- **Hips close:** they trip you easily.
