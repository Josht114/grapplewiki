---
name: Bottom body lock (standing)
side: bottom
counterpart: top-body-lock
---
Opponent has their hands locked around your waist while standing. Break the grip and get your hips away before they trip or lift you.

## Key points

1. Get your hips back and widen your base.
2. Frame on their head or neck to create space.
3. Peel the grip apart at the wrists.
4. Win an underhook or whizzer and turn to face them.
