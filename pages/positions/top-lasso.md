---
name: Top lasso guard
side: top
counterpart: bottom-lasso
---
Opponent's leg is wrapped around your arm. Free the arm or get to the outside before you pass.

## Principles

### Goal
- Free the lassoed arm or get to the outside of the lasso, then pass.

### Base and posture
- Posture up, hips back.
- Keep your weight on the lasso side to pin their lasso leg down.

### Hands and arms
- Break the sleeve grip at their wrist.
- Pull the lassoed elbow back to your hip to free the arm.

### Priorities
1. Break the sleeve grip.
2. Free the arm, or step to the outside of the lasso leg.
3. Control the legs and pass.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaning forward:** they off-balance you with the lasso.
- **Passing to the lasso side:** you walk into the sweep.
- **Leaving the arm trapped:** you can't defend their sweeps.
