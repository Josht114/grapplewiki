name: Bottom open guard
side: bottom
counterpart: top-open-guard
---
You are on your back with your legs between you and the opponent, who is either standing or kneeling. Manage distance with your feet and grips, and sweep when their posture breaks.

## Key points

1. Keep your feet and knees between you and them.
2. Against a standing passer: control distance with feet on hips, biceps or knees.
3. Against a kneeling passer: stay on your side and frame on the head and shoulders.
4. Sweep or attack when they lean or post a hand.
