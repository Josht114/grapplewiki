Opponent is underneath you with an X hook on your leg. Your base is compromised, so keep weight centred and look to step out.

## Principles

### Goal
- Keep your weight centred over the trapped leg and step out to pass.

### Base and posture
- Weight low and centred over the trapped leg; standing tall on it means they can lift you.
- Free leg posted wide for base.
- Head up, hips low.

### Hands and arms
- Grip their collar or head to stop them getting further under you.
- Push their hip or the hooking foot off your hip.
- Gi: a collar or belt grip helps you keep their shoulders down.

### Priorities
1. Weight low and centred.
2. Stop them getting under you.
3. Push the hooks off and step the trapped leg out.
4. Pass.

### Common mistakes
- **Standing tall:** they elevate you.
- **Leaning away:** they sweep you to that side.
- **Leaving the trapped leg in while you pass:** they come up into a single leg.
