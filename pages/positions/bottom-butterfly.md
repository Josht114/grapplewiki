---
name: Bottom butterfly guard
side: bottom
counterpart: top-butterfly
---
Seated with both feet hooked inside the opponent's thighs. Excellent for elevation sweeps and arm drags.

## Key points

1. Sit up: your head should be higher than theirs.
2. Get an underhook and hug their upper body close.
3. Keep both hooks inside their thighs.
4. Elevate as they lean in, or drag the arm to take the back.

## Principles

### Goal
- Elevate and sweep, arm drag or take the back.

### Base and posture
- Sit up, chest forward, head on their chest or shoulder and higher than theirs.
- Hooks under their thighs; heels in, knees out.
- Never fall flat on your back.

### Hands and arms
- Underhook on one side and an overhook or arm drag on the other.
- Gi: belt grip or sleeve.

### Frames
- If they flatten you, frame on the shoulder or forehead and get back up to sitting.

### Priorities
1. Sit up and win inside position.
2. Underhook or arm drag.
3. Elevate to sweep, or take the back.
4. If they flatten you, switch to X guard or SLX.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaning back:** they flatten you and pass.
- **Hooks too shallow:** no lift.
- **Reaching without upper body control:** they post and kill the sweep.
