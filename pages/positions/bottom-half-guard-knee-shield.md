name: Bottom half guard (knee shield / Z guard)
side: bottom
counterpart: top-half-guard-knee-shield
---
Half guard with your top knee across the opponent's chest as a frame. Keeps distance and blocks the crossface.

## Key points

1. Stay on your side, not flat.
2. Put your top knee across their chest or hip.
3. Frame on their collar or shoulder with your top hand.
4. Fight for the underhook or switch to an open guard.

## Principles

### Goal
- Keep distance and block the crossface, then sweep, come up or switch to an open guard.

### Base and posture
- On your side, not flat.
- Top knee across their chest or hip, shin angled toward them.
- Hips back from their hips; the shield only works with space behind it.

### Hands and arms
- Top hand frames on their collar or shoulder.
- Bottom hand fights for the underhook or a wrist.
- Gi: a collar grip on the frame side and a sleeve on the other.

### Frames
- Knee shield and forearm frame together keep their weight off you.

### Priorities
1. Stay on your side.
2. Knee across their chest or hip.
3. Frame on their collar or shoulder.
4. Underhook to come up or sweep, or switch to an open guard.

### Common mistakes
- **Lying flat:** they crush the shield.
- **Knee too low on the belly:** they pass over it.
- **No frame:** they crossface and flatten you.
