---
name: Top body lock (standing)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-body-lock
---
Hands locked around the opponent's waist while standing. Controls their posture and sets up trips and mat returns.

## Key points

1. Keep your head tight to their chest or under their chin.
2. Lock your hands low around the waist (gable or S-grip).
3. Keep your hips close so they can't sprawl or frame.
4. Pull their hips into you to break their base.

## Principles

### Goal
- Break their base and take them down: a trip, a lift or a mat return.

### Base and posture
- Head tight to their chest or under their chin.
- Hips close to theirs so they can't sprawl or frame.
- Knees bent, feet under you for lifting.

### Hands and arms
- Hands locked low around the waist: gable grip or S-grip.
- Elbows tight to their sides.
- Gi: a belt grip gives the same lock.

### Priorities
1. Head position and a tight lock.
2. Pull their hips into yours.
3. Trip, lift or mat return.
4. Land in side control or mount, not inside their guard.

### Common mistakes
- **Head out of position:** they guillotine you or frame on your head.
- **Lock too high:** they whizzer and turn.
- **Hips away:** they sprawl and break the grip.
