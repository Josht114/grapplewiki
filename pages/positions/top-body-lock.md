---
name: Top body lock (standing)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-body-lock
---
Hands locked around the opponent's waist while standing. Controls their posture and sets up trips and mat returns.

## Key points

1. Keep your head tight to their chest or under their chin.
2. Lock your hands low around the waist (gable or S-grip).
3. Keep your hips close so they can't sprawl or frame.
4. Pull their hips into you to break their base.
