name: Top mount (technical)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-mount-technical
---
Mount variation where the opponent has turned onto their side: one knee behind their back, the other leg posted in front. Follow their turn to take the back or arm bar.

## Key points

1. As they turn, slide your knee tight behind their back.
2. Post the other leg up in front of their face.
3. Keep the near arm trapped.
4. Take the back if they keep turning, or arm bar if they turn back.

## Principles

### Goal
- Follow their turn: take the back if they keep turning away, armbar if they turn back.

### Base and posture
- Knee on the mat tight behind their back, at hip height.
- Other foot posted in front of their face, near their head.
- Hips low, sitting on their hip, not up high.

### Hands and arms
- Trap the top arm: hug it or pin it to their face so it can't frame.
- Free hand posts or grips the collar (gi: sliding collar choke).

### Priorities
1. Slide your knee tight behind their back as they turn.
2. Post the front leg by their head.
3. Trap the top arm.
4. If they keep turning, slide the knee in as a hook and take the seatbelt; if they turn back, armbar the trapped arm.

### Common mistakes
- **Loose knee behind their back:** they roll flat and elbow-knee escape.
- **Sitting high:** they bridge and you fall off.
- **Waiting in technical mount:** it's a transition; take the back or the arm.
