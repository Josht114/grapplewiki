---
name: Bottom De La Riva guard
side: bottom
counterpart: top-dlr
---
Outside hook on the opponent's lead leg with heel or sleeve grips. Gateway to berimbolo, back takes and sweeps.

## Principles

### Goal
- Use the outside hook and your grips to off-balance them, then sweep, take the back or berimbolo.

### Base and posture
- On your back or your hip, turned toward the hooked leg.
- Your hips close to the hooked leg's heel; the hook is weak if you are far away.
- Free foot active: on their far hip, far knee or biceps.

### Hands and arms
- Hand on the same side as the hook grips their ankle or pants at the heel.
- Other hand on a sleeve or collar (gi), or a wrist or collar tie (no-gi).

### Frames
- Hook wrapped round their lead leg from outside, ankle behind their knee.
- Free foot on their far hip keeps distance and lets you pull or push their weight.

### Priorities
1. Set the hook and the ankle grip.
2. Upper-body grip (sleeve, collar or wrist).
3. Off-balance them, usually away from the hook.
4. Sweep, take the back or berimbolo when they step to rebalance.

### Common mistakes
- **Hook too shallow:** your foot slides off and they pass.
- **No ankle grip:** they step out of the hook.
- **Flat on your back:** your hips can't follow them.
