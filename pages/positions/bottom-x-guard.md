name: Bottom X guard
side: bottom
counterpart: top-x-guard

Under the opponent with an X hook on one leg. Powerful elevation for sweeps.

## Principles

### Goal
- Elevate them off balance and sweep.

### Base and posture
- Under them, shoulders under their hips.
- Your legs form an X on their lead leg: one foot behind their knee, the other on their hip or front of the thigh.
- Hips close to their leg; the further away, the weaker the lift.

### Hands and arms
- Hug their leg with one arm.
- Other hand grips their far ankle, sleeve or belt.

### Frames
- Your legs under their leg are the frame; extend them to lift.

### Priorities
1. Get under them.
2. Set the X hooks.
3. Extend your legs to elevate them.
4. Sweep: kick their leg out and pull their far ankle, or come up into a single leg.

### Common mistakes
- **Too far from their leg:** no control, no lift.
- **No grip on the far ankle or sleeve:** they step out.
- **Hooks not tight:** they flatten you and pass.
