You hold a cross collar and a cuff or bicep grip. Use the collar to off-balance and connect to sweeps and back takes.

## Principles

### Goal
- Use the cross collar to off-balance them and connect to sweeps and back takes.

### Base and posture
- On your hip, facing them, with your feet active on their hips or biceps.
- Pull them toward you to break their posture.

### Hands and arms
- Cross-collar grip deep, close to the back of the neck.
- Other hand on the cuff or biceps of the arm on the same side as the collar grip.
- Gi only: this guard needs a collar.

### Frames
- Feet on the hips or biceps; keep the knee of the collar-grip side in front of their chest.

### Priorities
1. Get the cross collar deep and a sleeve or biceps grip.
2. Off-balance them forward or sideways.
3. Sweep or take the back.

### Common mistakes
- **Shallow collar grip:** they break it easily.
- **Flat on your back:** no angle.
- **Pulling with only the arms:** use your legs to steer.
