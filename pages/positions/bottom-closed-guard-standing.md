---
name: Bottom closed guard (standing)
side: bottom
counterpart: top-closed-guard-standing
---
Opponent has stood up inside your closed guard. Break their posture or switch to an open guard sweep before they open your legs.

## Key points

1. Keep your ankles locked while you have grips.
2. Pull a sleeve or collar to break their posture.
3. Sweep from the ankles or hips while they stand.
4. If the guard opens, switch to an open guard.
