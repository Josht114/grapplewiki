Opponent is seated with butterfly hooks. Flatten them or keep your hips heavy to stop the elevation.

## Key points

1. Keep your hips heavy and back so the hooks can't lift you.
2. Control their head or shoulders to flatten them.
3. Kill a hook by pinning the leg or stepping it out.
4. Pass to the side where a hook is dead.

## Principles

### Goal
- Kill the hooks and flatten them before they elevate you.

### Base and posture
- Hips heavy and back so the hooks can't get under them; chest and head forward onto them.
- Knees wide; don't sit up straight where they can lift you.
- Keep your head higher than theirs and pushed into them.

### Hands and arms
- Win the underhook battle: overhook their underhook or get a double underhook.
- Gi: grip the pants at the knees to pin the hooks down.

### Priorities
1. Head position and stop their underhook.
2. Kill one hook (push the knee down or stagger your legs).
3. Flatten them onto their back.
4. Pass.

### Common mistakes
- **Upright posture:** arm drag or elevation sweep.
- **Knees together:** easy to lift.
- **Staying square:** they get both hooks and choose the side.
