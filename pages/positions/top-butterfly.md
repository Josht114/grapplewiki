Opponent is seated with butterfly hooks. Flatten them or keep your hips heavy to stop the elevation.

## Key points

1. Keep your hips heavy and back so the hooks can't lift you.
2. Control their head or shoulders to flatten them.
3. Kill a hook by pinning the leg or stepping it out.
4. Pass to the side where a hook is dead.
