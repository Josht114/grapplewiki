Opponent is underneath at an angle, threatening to enter the legs from K guard. Keep your knees safe and your hips back.

## Principles

### Goal
- Keep your knees safe and your hips back, then pass.

### Base and posture
- Hips back, knees bent, stance wide.
- Keep your lead knee pointed out, not in, to stop them entering.
- Weight centred.

### Hands and arms
- Grip their legs at the knees or pants and pin them to the mat.
- Push their inside knee down so it can't come across your thigh.
- Keep a hand ready to block their ankle grip on your lead leg.

### Priorities
1. Hips back and knees safe: don't let them under your centre of weight.
2. Pin their inside leg.
3. Step away from the angle and pass to the side of the pinned leg.
4. If they enter the legs, go straight to your leg-lock defence (hide the heel).

### Common mistakes
- **Leaning forward:** they invert under you and enter the legs.
- **Standing still in front of them:** they take the angle and the back.
- **Stepping over their legs:** you step right into an entanglement.
