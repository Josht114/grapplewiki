You have stood up inside the opponent's closed guard, but their ankles are still locked. Keep posture and pressure the legs open before you pass.

## Key points

1. Stand with feet wide and hips forward.
2. Keep a straight back and grips on their belt or chest.
3. Push one knee down or step back to break the ankles.
4. Control the legs as soon as the guard opens.

## Principles

### Goal
- Break the ankles open and control the legs the moment the guard opens.

### Base and posture
- Feet wide, knees bent, hips forward and back straight.
- Stand up in one move with your posture set; standing while bent over gets you pulled down.

### Hands and arms
- Grips on their belt, lapels at the sternum (gi) or hips, pinning their hips down.
- Elbows tight; keep your arms out of their sleeve and wrist grips.

### Priorities
1. Stand with posture and a wide base.
2. Push one knee down or step a leg back to break the ankles.
3. Control the legs as soon as the guard opens.
4. Pass before they get their feet back on your hips.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaning forward:** they pull you back down into the guard.
- **Narrow base:** a sweep from the ankles takes you over.
- **Letting go of their hips:** they reset and attack.
