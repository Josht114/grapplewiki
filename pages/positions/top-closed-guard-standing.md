name: Top closed guard (standing)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-closed-guard-standing
---
You have stood up inside the opponent's closed guard, but their ankles are still locked. Keep posture and pressure the legs open before you pass.

## Key points

1. Stand with feet wide and hips forward.
2. Keep a straight back and grips on their belt or chest.
3. Push one knee down or step back to break the ankles.
4. Control the legs as soon as the guard opens.
