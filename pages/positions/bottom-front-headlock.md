---
name: Bottom front headlock
side: bottom
counterpart: top-front-headlock
---
Your head is trapped in front of the opponent, often after a failed shot. Protect the neck first, then square up and lift your head.

## Key points

1. Hand-fight the arm around your neck before anything else.
2. Square your hips up underneath you.
3. Drive your head up into their chest.
4. Circle out or stand up once the head is free.

## Principles

### Goal
- Protect your neck, get your head out and get back to standing or to guard.

### Base and posture
- Get your feet or knees under your hips; the further your hips are behind your head, the worse it gets.
- Chin down while they hold the strap; look up and lift your head once the strap is off.
- Square up to them: don't let them get to your side.

### Hands and arms
- Both hands fight the choking arm: pull the wrist down off your chin.
- Keep the elbow by your head tight to your ear. Their darce and anaconda thread past it.
- Gi: grip their sleeve or wrist on your collar grip and strip it.

### Frames
- When the choke is handled, post a hand on their hip or thigh to push their hips back while you lift your head.

### Priorities
1. Hand-fight the choking arm, chin tucked.
2. Get your hips back under you.
3. Pop the head out on the side of their arm and square up or stand.
4. If they go behind, sit to guard or defend from turtle.

### Common mistakes
- **Shooting with your head trapped:** you feed them the choke.
- **Reaching for a leg with the trapped-side arm:** that arm is what they need for the darce or anaconda.
- **Staying flat on your knees:** they spin behind and take the back.
