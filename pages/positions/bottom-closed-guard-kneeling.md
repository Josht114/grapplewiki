---
Your legs are locked around the opponent's waist. Break their posture, then attack sweeps and submissions.

## Key points

1. Break their posture: pull them down with your legs and grips.
2. Control a sleeve or wrist to keep one arm from posting.
3. Angle off to the side; don't stay square under them.
4. Use your hips: shrimp, sit up or bump to create attacks.
