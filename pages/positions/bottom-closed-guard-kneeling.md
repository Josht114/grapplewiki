name: Bottom closed guard (kneeling)

Your legs are locked around the opponent's waist. Break their posture, then attack sweeps and submissions.

## Key points

1. Break their posture: pull them down with your legs and grips.
2. Control a sleeve or wrist to keep one arm from posting.
3. Angle off to the side; don't stay square under them.
4. Use your hips: shrimp, sit up or bump to create attacks.

## Principles

### Goal
- Break their posture and attack with sweeps and submissions.

### Base and posture
- Legs locked high on their back; pull your knees toward your chest.
- Hips mobile: shrimp to angle off rather than lying square under them.
- Head off the mat.

### Hands and arms
- One hand controls posture (collar, head or collar tie); the other controls an arm (sleeve, wrist or overhook).
- Gi: cross collar and sleeve. No-gi: collar tie and wrist or two-on-one.

### Frames
- If they posture up or stand, put your feet on their hips and open on your terms rather than letting them pry your legs open.

### Priorities
1. Break their posture.
2. Control an arm.
3. Angle off.
4. Attack combinations: armbar, triangle, kimura, hip bump sweep.

### Common mistakes
- **Lying flat holding the guard closed:** they posture and open it.
- **Legs low on their hips:** no control of posture.
- **Attacking before posture is broken:** easy to defend.
