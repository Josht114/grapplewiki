Opponent is controlling your posture with a high leg. Keep posture and pin the leg down before it locks in.

## Principles

### Goal
- Keep your posture and pin the high leg down before it locks in.

### Base and posture
- Posture up as soon as their leg starts to climb; head up, back straight.
- Hips forward and heavy on their hips.

### Hands and arms
- Pin their hips or the high knee down with your hands.
- Keep both arms in front of you; don't post a hand on the mat.
- Free your wrist from their grip by circling it out.

### Priorities
1. Posture before it's set.
2. Pin the high leg down off your shoulder.
3. Free your arm.
4. Stand up and open the guard.

### Common mistakes
- **Letting your head drop:** they lock the shin over your back.
- **Posting a hand:** omoplata or triangle.
- **Pulling your arm straight out:** they triangle the arm they let go of.
