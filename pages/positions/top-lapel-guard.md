name: Top lapel guard (worm)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-lapel-guard
---
Opponent has wrapped your own lapel around your leg. Break the lapel grip before you try to pass.

## Key points

1. Keep your lapel tucked into your belt to deny it.
2. Pull your lead leg back and kill the wrap.
3. Strip the lapel grip at the wrist.
4. Pass once the leg is free.

## Principles

### Goal
- Break the lapel grip and free your lead leg before you try to pass.

### Base and posture
- Posture up, hips back, weight centred.
- Pull your lead leg back to kill the wrap's angle.

### Hands and arms
- Keep your lapel tucked into your belt so they can't pull it out.
- Strip the lapel grip at the wrist: two hands on their gripping hand.
- Gi only.

### Priorities
1. Deny the lapel: keep it tucked.
2. If they have it, pull your lead leg back and strip the grip at the wrist.
3. Control their legs and pass once the leg is free.

### Common mistakes
- **Passing with the lapel still wrapped:** they sweep you or take your back.
- **Leaning forward:** they off-balance you with the lapel.
- **Leaving your lapel loose:** gives them the grip in the first place.
