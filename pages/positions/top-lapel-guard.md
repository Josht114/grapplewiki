---
name: Top lapel guard (worm)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-lapel-guard
---
Opponent has wrapped your own lapel around your leg. Break the lapel grip before you try to pass.

## Key points

1. Keep your lapel tucked into your belt to deny it.
2. Pull your lead leg back and kill the wrap.
3. Strip the lapel grip at the wrist.
4. Pass once the leg is free.
