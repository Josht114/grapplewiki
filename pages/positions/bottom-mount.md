Opponent is mounted on you. Keep elbows tight, protect the neck, and escape with bridges and elbow-knee movement.

## Key points

1. Keep your elbows tight to your ribs.
2. Protect your neck with your hands and chin.
3. Bridge first, then shrimp; one sets up the other.
4. Never turn your back to escape.
