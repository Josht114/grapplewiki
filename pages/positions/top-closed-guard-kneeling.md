You are inside the opponent's closed guard on your knees. Posture up and open the guard before you try to pass.

## Key points

1. Posture first: back straight, head up.
2. Grips on their hips, belt or chest, not on the mat.
3. Keep your elbows inside your knees to avoid arm attacks.
4. Open the guard before you try to pass.

## Principles

### Goal
- Hold good posture, open the guard and pass.

### Base and posture
- Knees wide, hips forward, back straight, head up.
- Sit your weight back toward your heels, not forward into them.

### Hands and arms
- Gi: both hands on their lapels at the sternum or one on the belt, pinning them down.
- No-gi: hands on the hips or biceps.
- Elbows tight to your body; don't post hands on the mat.
- Keep both arms in or both out; one in, one out is a triangle.

### Priorities
1. Posture and grips first.
2. Break their grips and keep your arms safe.
3. Open the guard (knee in the tailbone or stand up).
4. Pass.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaning forward:** they break your posture and attack.
- **One arm in, one out:** triangle.
- **Opening the guard before your posture is set:** they sweep you as you move.
