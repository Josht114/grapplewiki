name: Top closed guard (kneeling)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-closed-guard-kneeling
---
You are inside the opponent's closed guard on your knees. Posture up and open the guard before you try to pass.

## Key points

1. Posture first: back straight, head up.
2. Grips on their hips, belt or chest, not on the mat.
3. Keep your elbows inside your knees to avoid arm attacks.
4. Open the guard before you try to pass.
