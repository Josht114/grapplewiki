Both arms are trapped and your neck is open. Keep your chin tucked and free the arm trapped by their legs before they settle.

## Key points

1. Tuck your chin and keep your free hand near your neck.
2. Turn toward them, putting the leg-trapped shoulder to the mat.
3. Walk your hips away to pull the arm out.
4. Recover turtle or guard.

## Principles

### Goal
- Protect your neck and free the arm trapped by their legs before they settle.

### Base and posture
- Chin tucked hard.
- Turn toward them, putting the shoulder of the leg-trapped arm to the mat.
- Keep your hips under you and moving; don't let them pull you flat on your back.

### Hands and arms
- Free hand near your neck.
- Pull the leg-trapped arm out as your hips walk away from them, elbow first.

### Frames
- None while trapped; once the arm is free, frame on their hip or leg and get back to turtle or guard.

### Priorities
1. Chin tucked, free hand protecting your neck.
2. Turn toward them.
3. Walk your hips away to pull the arm out.
4. Recover turtle or guard.

### Common mistakes
- **Turning away from them:** they lock the arm tighter and you end up flat.
- **Leaving your neck open:** they choke you.
- **Waiting:** escape before they settle the second arm.
