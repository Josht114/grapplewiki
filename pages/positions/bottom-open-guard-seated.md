Sitting up facing the opponent with your feet and hands active. Good for wrestling up, collar drags and leg entries.

## Key points

1. Sit up with one hand posted behind you.
2. Keep your feet in front as frames.
3. Grip a collar, sleeve or ankle.
4. Wrestle up, drag, or enter the legs when they reach.

## Principles

### Goal
- Stay mobile and connected: wrestle up, drag, or enter the legs when they reach.

### Base and posture
- Sit up with one hand posted behind you and your chest toward them.
- Feet in front as frames; knees bent and ready to move.
- Head up and forward, not leaning back.

### Hands and arms
- Free hand grips a collar, sleeve, wrist or ankle.
- Posting hand is ready to lift your hips to come up.
- Gi: collar and sleeve for the collar drag. No-gi: wrist, ankle or collar tie.

### Frames
- Feet on their shins, knees or hips keep them at distance.
- If they push your head down, frame on their shoulder and come back to sitting.

### Priorities
1. Sit up, post a hand, feet in front.
2. Get a grip: collar, sleeve, wrist or ankle.
3. Wrestle up, collar drag, or enter the legs when they reach.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaning back:** they push your head to the mat and pass.
- **Feet idle:** they step past them.
- **Waiting with no grips:** they set up the pass.
