Opponent is sitting up facing you. Control their head and knees so they can't wrestle up or drag you, then pass.

## Key points

1. Keep your hips back out of reach of their ankle grips.
2. Push their head or shoulder back to the mat.
3. Sprawl if they shoot under you.
4. Grip the knees and pass.

## Principles

### Goal
- Control their head and knees so they can't wrestle up or drag you, then pass.

### Base and posture
- Hips back out of reach of their ankle grips.
- Staggered stance, knees bent, ready to sprawl.

### Hands and arms
- One hand on their forehead or shoulder, pushing them back toward the mat.
- Other hand blocks their grip hand or grips the near knee.
- Gi: a collar grip with a stiff arm keeps them on their back; break their collar grip before they drag.

### Priorities
1. Hips back, out of reach of their ankle grips.
2. Push their head or shoulder back to the mat.
3. Sprawl if they shoot under you.
4. Grip the knees and pass.

### Common mistakes
- **Reaching in from far away:** they grab your arm and collar drag you.
- **Leaning over their legs:** they come up under you into a single leg.
- **Passing before their head is down:** they come up with you.
