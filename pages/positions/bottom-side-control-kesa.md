---
name: Bottom side control (kesa gatame)
side: bottom
counterpart: top-side-control-kesa
---
Opponent is sitting beside you in a scarf hold with your head and near arm wrapped. Free the arm, then bridge them over or take their back.

## Key points

1. Pull your trapped elbow down toward your hip.
2. Frame your far arm on their head or hip.
3. Bridge high into them and turn.
4. Roll them over your chest, or slide behind them to take the back.

## Principles

### Goal
- Free the trapped arm, then bridge them over or take their back.

### Base and posture
- Turn into them, onto your side.
- Hips moving: walk them under their hips.

### Hands and arms
- Pull your trapped elbow down toward your hip.
- Far arm frames on their head or hip.

### Frames
- Far forearm on their jaw or hip keeps their weight off your chest.

### Priorities
1. Free the trapped elbow.
2. Frame on their head or hip.
3. Bridge high into them and turn.
4. Roll them over or slide behind to take the back.

### Common mistakes
- **Pushing with a straight arm:** they armlock it.
- **Lying flat:** you can't move the hips.
- **Bridging without first freeing the arm:** they stay.
