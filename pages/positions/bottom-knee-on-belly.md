---
name: Bottom knee on belly
side: bottom
counterpart: top-knee-on-belly
---
Opponent's knee is driving into your stomach. Frame on the knee, turn in and recover guard before they mount.

## Key points

1. Push the knee down toward your hips with both hands.
2. Shrimp away from the knee to make space.
3. Turn in and bring your own knee back inside.
4. Recover guard or come up on a single leg.
