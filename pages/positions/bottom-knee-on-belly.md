name: Bottom knee on belly
side: bottom
counterpart: top-knee-on-belly
---
Opponent's knee is driving into your stomach. Frame on the knee, turn in and recover guard before they mount.

## Key points

1. Push the knee down toward your hips with both hands.
2. Shrimp away from the knee to make space.
3. Turn in and bring your own knee back inside.
4. Recover guard or come up on a single leg.

## Principles

### Goal
- Get the knee off your belly and recover guard before they climb to mount.

### Base and posture
- Turn onto your side toward them; flat on your back takes the whole knee.
- Feet active, ready to shrimp your hips away from the knee.

### Hands and arms
- Both hands on their knee or shin, pushing it down toward the mat and past your hip.
- Far arm stays tight; reaching up to push their chest gives the far-side armbar.

### Frames
- Hands and forearms on the knee are the main frame; the shrimp makes the space.
- Get your near elbow inside their knee line before you bring your own knee in.

### Priorities
1. Turn in and grab the knee.
2. Shrimp away while you push the knee down past your hip.
3. Bring your knee or foot in and recover half or full guard.

### Common mistakes
- **Pushing their chest with a straight arm:** that's the far-side armbar.
- **Turning away:** they step to mount or the back.
- **Staying flat and waiting:** their weight only gets worse.
