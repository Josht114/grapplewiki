---
name: Top saddle (inside sankaku)
side: top
counterpart: bottom-saddle
---
Your leg is trapped in the opponent's saddle. Protect your heel and knee before anything else.

## Key points

1. Hide your heel by pointing your toes.
2. Hand-fight their grips on your foot.
3. Work your knee out past their hips.
4. Free the leg or counter-attack the legs.

## Principles

### Goal
- Protect your heel and knee, then free your leg or counter-attack the legs.

### Base and posture
- Sit up toward them; don't lie back.
- Point your toes and keep your foot hidden from their arms.
- Don't roll away from their grip in a panic; that's how knees get hurt.

### Hands and arms
- Hand-fight their grips on your foot; peel their hands off the heel.
- Free leg pushes their top knee down or their hip away.

### Priorities
1. Hide your heel.
2. Hand-fight their grips.
3. Work your knee out past their hips.
4. Free the leg or counter-attack.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaving the heel exposed:** heel hook.
- **Rolling blindly:** you twist your own knee.
- **Pulling the trapped leg straight back while they hold the heel:** it tears the knee.
