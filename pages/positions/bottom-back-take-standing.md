Your opponent is behind you while standing. Priorities: protect the neck, break the grip lock, and turn to face them.

## Principles

### Goal
- Protect your neck, break their grip and turn to face them.

### Base and posture
- Feet wide, knees bent, hips low so they can't lift you.
- Chin down and shoulders up.
- Turn into them as soon as their grip is broken.

### Hands and arms
- First both hands on the arm near your neck.
- Then peel their hands apart at the wrists, starting with the fingers.
- Gi: guard your collars.

### Frames
- Once you've turned, a hand on their head or hip keeps your back from being taken again.

### Priorities
1. Neck first: hands on the choking arm.
2. Widen your base so they can't lift or trip you.
3. Break the grip and turn to face them.
4. If they take you down, land on your side, not flat on your face.

### Common mistakes
- **Reaching back for their head:** they keep the grip and take you down.
- **Standing tall:** easy to lift and drop.
- **Ignoring the hooks:** if a leg wraps you, it's back mount on your feet.
