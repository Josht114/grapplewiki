---
name: Bottom single leg X (ashi garami)
side: bottom
counterpart: top-slx
---
You entangle one leg with the opponent standing or kneeling. Sweeps and straight foot lock attacks.

## Principles

### Goal
- Off-balance them from single leg X and sweep, or attack the straight foot lock.

### Base and posture
- On your side under them, hugging one leg.
- Outside foot on their hip; inside leg hooked behind their knee from between their legs.
- Hips close to their leg.

### Hands and arms
- Hug their foot to your chest or armpit.
- Free hand grips the heel or ankle.

### Frames
- Foot on the hip keeps them from stepping over you.

### Priorities
1. Hug the leg and set the hooks.
2. Push their hip and kick the back of the knee to off-balance them.
3. Sweep, or attack the straight foot lock.

### Common mistakes
- **Hips too far from their leg:** no control.
- **Loose hug:** they pull the leg out.
- **No foot on the hip:** they step over and pass.
