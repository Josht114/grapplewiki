---
name: Bottom half guard
side: bottom
counterpart: top-half-guard
---
One of the opponent's legs is trapped between yours. Stay on your side, win the underhook or a knee shield, and avoid getting flattened.

## Key points

1. Stay on your side, never flat on your back.
2. Win the underhook on the trapped-leg side.
3. Use a knee shield to keep distance when you can't underhook.
4. Come up to your knees or take the back when they lean forward.

## Principles

### Goal
- Get the underhook to sweep, take the back or come up, or recover full guard.

### Base and posture
- On your side facing them, never flat on your back.
- Head off the mat and close to their chest, so the crossface has nowhere to go.

### Hands and arms
- Near underhook: pummel it in and keep it.
- Or a knee shield with a forearm frame on their neck.
- Gi: belt or pants grips. No-gi: underhook and wrist.

### Frames
- Knee shield across their belly or chest keeps their weight off.
- Forearm frame on their neck or collarbone stops the crossface.

### Priorities
1. Get on your side and block the crossface.
2. Win the underhook or set a knee shield.
3. Sweep, take the back or come up to your knees; or recover full guard.

### Common mistakes
- **Lying flat:** they crossface and pass.
- **Losing the underhook:** they flatten you.
- **Loose legs on the trapped leg:** they free it and pass.
