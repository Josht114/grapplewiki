---
name: Bottom half guard
side: bottom
counterpart: top-half-guard
---
One of the opponent's legs is trapped between yours. Stay on your side, win the underhook or a knee shield, and avoid getting flattened.

## Key points

1. Stay on your side, never flat on your back.
2. Win the underhook on the trapped-leg side.
3. Use a knee shield to keep distance when you can't underhook.
4. Come up to your knees or take the back when they lean forward.
