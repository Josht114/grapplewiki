Inverted-angle guard for entering leg entanglements and back takes from under the opponent.

## Principles

### Goal
- Enter the legs or take the back from an inverted angle.

### Base and posture
- On your back or shoulder, turned toward their lead leg.
- Inside leg's knee across their thigh, outside foot on their hip.
- Ready to invert under them; stay close to their leg.

### Hands and arms
- Grip their ankle or leg.
- Other hand on their far knee or a collar tie.

### Frames
- Your legs between you and them.
- Outside foot on the hip keeps them from stepping over.

### Priorities
1. Get the angle under them.
2. Grip the leg.
3. Enter the legs (backside 50/50, SLX) or take the back.

### Common mistakes
- **Flat on your back square to them:** no angle to enter from, and they pass.
- **No grip on the leg:** they step away from the entry.
- **Late entry:** they sit back, pin your legs and pass.
