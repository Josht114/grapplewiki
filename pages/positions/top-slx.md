Your leg is entangled in single leg X. Protect the heel, stay upright and step out to pass, or go to 50/50.

## Principles

### Goal
- Protect your heel, stay upright and step out to pass, or go to 50/50.

### Base and posture
- Stay upright with your hips over the trapped leg; falling back puts you in their game.
- Trapped knee bent and pointed down toward their chest, toes pointed to hide the heel.
- Free leg posted wide behind you for base.

### Hands and arms
- Grip the foot on your hip and push it off, or pin it to the mat.
- Hand-fight their grips on your heel or ankle.
- Gi: a collar or sleeve grip on them keeps them from coming up under you.

### Priorities
1. Hide the heel and keep the knee bent.
2. Stay upright and centred over the trapped leg.
3. Clear the foot on your hip, then step the trapped leg out and back to pass.
4. If you can't step out, go to 50/50 rather than fall over.

### Common mistakes
- **Falling back or sitting down:** you end up in their leg locks.
- **Straight trapped leg:** gives them the straight ankle lock.
- **Ignoring the foot on your hip:** that foot is what sweeps you.
