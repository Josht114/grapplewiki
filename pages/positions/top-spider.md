name: Top spider guard
side: top
counterpart: bottom-spider
---
Opponent controls your sleeves with feet on your biceps. Break the sleeve grips or circle to strip a foot.

## Principles

### Goal
- Break the sleeve grips or strip a foot off your biceps, then pass.

### Base and posture
- Posture up, hips back, stance wide and staggered.
- Weight centred; don't lean into their feet.

### Hands and arms
- Break the sleeve grips: circle your hand out and around their foot, or pull your elbow back to your hip.
- Elbows in, close to your body; straight arms are what spider guard needs.
- Once a foot is stripped, pin that leg to the mat.

### Priorities
1. Break one sleeve grip.
2. Strip that foot off your biceps and pin the leg.
3. Pass to that side before they rebuild the grip.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaning forward:** triangle or sweep.
- **Pulling straight back against the feet:** their legs only get stronger.
- **Passing with both grips still on:** they sweep you as you move.
