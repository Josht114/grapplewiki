name: Top De La Riva guard
side: top
counterpart: bottom-dlr

Opponent has a De La Riva hook around your lead leg. Kill the hook and the far grip before attacking the pass.

## Principles

### Goal
- Kill the hook and the far grip, then pass before they rebuild it.

### Base and posture
- Hips back, knees bent, stance wide; weight on the hooked leg.
- Posture up; don't lean on them.
- Turn the hooked knee outward to loosen the hook.

### Hands and arms
- Break the far grip on your sleeve or collar first.
- Push their hook knee down, or grip the pants at the knee to pin it.
- Gi: a grip on their collar or belt stops the berimbolo spin.

### Priorities
1. Break the far grip.
2. Kill the hook: knee out, or pin their knee.
3. Control both legs and pass to the side away from the hook.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaning forward:** berimbolo or back take.
- **Standing on one leg:** they sweep you easily.
- **Passing before the grips are broken:** they re-guard and sweep.
