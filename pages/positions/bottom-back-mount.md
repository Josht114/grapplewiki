---
name: Bottom back mount
side: bottom
counterpart: top-back-mount
---
Opponent has your back. Defend the neck first, clear a hook, and slide your back to the mat on the side of the bottom hook.

## Key points

1. Hand-fight the choke first; chin down.
2. Slide your shoulders toward the side of their bottom hook.
3. Clear the top hook and get your back to the mat.
4. Turn into them to finish the escape.

## Principles

### Goal
- Protect your neck, get your back to the mat and turn into them.

### Base and posture
- Chin down, shoulders shrugged up.
- Scoot your hips away to slide your back to the mat on the side of the bottom hook, shoulders below theirs.
- Once your shoulders are on the mat, turn toward them, not toward the mat.

### Hands and arms
- Two-on-one the choking arm: both hands on the top arm's wrist, pulling it down.
- Keep that grip while your hips do the escaping.
- Gi: grip the hand on your collar and hold it there so it can't feed deeper.

### Priorities
1. Both hands on the choking arm, chin tucked.
2. Clear the bottom hook and slide your back to the mat.
3. Get your head to the mat next to theirs, then turn in to half guard or top.
4. Re-hook defence: keep their top leg from coming back over your hip.

### Common mistakes
- **Letting go of the choking arm to fight hooks:** they choke you.
- **Falling to the top-hook side:** they keep the hooks or mount.
- **Turning toward the mat:** you end up flattened with them still on your back.
