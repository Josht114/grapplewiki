---
name: Bottom back mount
side: bottom
counterpart: top-back-mount
---
Opponent has your back. Defend the neck first, clear a hook, and slide your back to the mat on the side of the bottom hook.

## Key points

1. Hand-fight the choke first; chin down.
2. Slide your shoulders toward the side of their bottom hook.
3. Clear the top hook and get your back to the mat.
4. Turn into them to finish the escape.
