---
name: Bottom half guard (deep half)
side: bottom
counterpart: top-half-guard-deep
---
You are underneath the opponent, your body under their hips and both legs around one of their legs. Strong for sweeps like the waiter.

## Key points

1. Get your head and shoulders under their hips.
2. Hug their trapped leg with both arms.
3. Keep your legs locked around that leg.
4. Off-balance them forward or sideways for the sweep.

## Principles

### Goal
- Get deep under their hips and sweep them using their trapped leg.

### Base and posture
- Head and shoulders under their hips, your body square to their trapped leg.
- Legs locked around their trapped leg.
- Keep your head tight to their hip so they can't push it away.

### Hands and arms
- Both arms hug their trapped leg; the near arm under the thigh.
- Watch for the whizzer: keep your far arm out of their overhook.

### Frames
- Your body under their hips is the frame; while it's there they can't drop weight on you.

### Priorities
1. Get your head under their hips.
2. Hug their trapped leg.
3. Off-balance them forward or sideways for the sweep (waiter, or tip them over their trapped leg).

### Common mistakes
- **Head out from under their hips:** they flatten you.
- **Loose leg lock:** they free the trapped leg.
- **Waiting too long:** they whizzer and back-step.
