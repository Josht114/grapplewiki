Opponent is pinning you head-to-hips. Protect the neck, frame on the hips, and turn in to recover guard.

## Key points

1. Protect your neck: keep your near arm inside.
2. Frame on their hips to make space.
3. Turn in toward their legs as they move.
4. Recover guard or come up to your knees.

## Principles

### Goal
- Protect your neck, make space and turn in to recover guard.

### Base and posture
- Turn onto your side; flat is where they want you.
- Hips moving: walk your feet and shrimp to turn under them.

### Hands and arms
- Keep a hand or forearm in front of your neck against the choke.
- Elbows tight; don't reach around their body (kimura).

### Frames
- Hands or forearms on their hips, pushing them toward your feet.
- Elbows inside, so you can turn and bring a knee in.

### Priorities
1. Neck first: a hand between their arm and your neck.
2. Frame on the hips and turn onto your side.
3. Bring your knee in and recover guard, or come up to your knees.

### Common mistakes
- **Reaching around their back:** they catch a kimura.
- **Lying flat:** they settle the chest and choke.
- **Only pushing with your arms:** the hips make the space, not the arms.
