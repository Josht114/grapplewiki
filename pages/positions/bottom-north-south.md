Opponent is pinning you head-to-hips. Protect the neck, frame on the hips, and turn in to recover guard.

## Key points

1. Protect your neck: keep your near arm inside.
2. Frame on their hips to make space.
3. Turn in toward their legs as they move.
4. Recover guard or come up to your knees.
