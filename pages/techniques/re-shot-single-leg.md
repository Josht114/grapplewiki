From a front headlock, shoot again for a single leg as the opponent leans on you.

## Key steps

1. Hand-fight the arm on your neck.
2. Wait for them to lean their weight on you.
3. Drop and grab a leg.
4. Drive through to finish the takedown.

## Defense

Sprawl your hips back and keep their head down.

1. Kick your legs back.
2. Drop your hips on their shoulders.
3. Keep the front headlock.
