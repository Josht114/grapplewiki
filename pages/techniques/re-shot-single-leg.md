From a front headlock, shoot again for a single leg as the opponent leans on you.

## Key steps

1. Hand-fight the arm on your neck.
2. Wait for them to lean their weight on you.
3. Drop and grab a leg.
4. Drive through to finish the takedown.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom of the front headlock
- They're over you in a front headlock, chest on your head.

### 1. Hand-fight the arm on your neck
- Control the wrist of the arm around your neck so they can't lock a choke.

### 2. Wait for them to lean their weight on you
- Let them put their weight forward onto your head; that commits their legs close to you.

### 3. Drop and grab a leg
- Drop your level and grab one of their legs, head on the inside, behind their knee.

### 4. Drive through to finish the takedown
- Drive forward through the leg and turn the corner, coming up on top.

### Common mistakes
- **Shooting with your neck exposed:** clear the arm first, or the re-shot walks into a guillotine.

## Defense

Sprawl your hips back and keep their head down.

1. Kick your legs back.
2. Drop your hips on their shoulders.
3. Keep the front headlock.
