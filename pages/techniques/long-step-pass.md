From a pressure position, take a long step back and around to clear the legs.

## Key steps

1. From a pressure position, keep your chest heavy on them.
2. Take a long step back with your leg, away from their legs.
3. Swing around to the side, keeping your weight on them.
4. Drop into side control.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: in their half or open guard
- You're in a heavy pressure position: chest on them, maybe an underhook or crossface.

### 1. From a pressure position, keep your chest heavy on them
- Stay heavy on their chest; they have to carry your weight, which keeps them flat.

### 2. Take a long step back with your leg, away from their legs
- Step one leg back and out, past their legs, toward the side you're passing to. The step is long, so your hips end up away from their legs.

### 3. Swing around to the side, keeping your weight on them
- Pivot your hips around to that side, your chest staying on them the whole time.

### 4. Drop into side control
- Bring your hips down and settle into side control, crossface and underhook.

### Common mistakes
- **Lifting your chest to step:** they get space to recover guard.
- **A short step:** their legs catch yours again.

## Defense

Stopping pressure passes by keeping your knees between you and the opponent and refusing to be flattened.

1. Stay on your side, never flat.
2. Frame on the head and shoulders.
3. Keep your knees connected to your elbows.
4. Pummel your legs back inside when they clear one.
