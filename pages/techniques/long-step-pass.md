From a pressure position, take a long step back and around to clear the legs.

## Key steps

1. From a pressure position, keep your chest heavy on them.
2. Take a long step back with your leg, away from their legs.
3. Swing around to the side, keeping your weight on them.
4. Drop into side control.

## Defense

Stopping pressure passes by keeping your knees between you and the opponent and refusing to be flattened.

1. Stay on your side, never flat.
2. Frame on the head and shoulders.
3. Keep your knees connected to your elbows.
4. Pummel your legs back inside when they clear one.
