Make space and turn to your knees, keeping elbows tight.

## Key steps

1. Frame and bridge.
2. Shrimp away.
3. Turn onto your knees.
4. Elbows to knees, chin down.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom side control
- They're on top across you.

### 1. Frame and bridge
- Frame on their neck and hip and bridge to lift them.

### 2. Shrimp away
- Shrimp your hips out to make space.

### 3. Turn onto your knees
- Turn away from them onto your knees, tucking your knees under you.

### 4. Elbows to knees, chin down
- Elbows tight to your knees, chin tucked, so they can't get hooks or a choke.

### Common mistakes
- **Turning with your elbows out:** they take your back. Elbows tight.

## Defense

Follow and take the back or break them down.

1. Stay on their hips.
2. Get the seatbelt.
3. Insert hooks.
