Make space and turn to your knees, keeping elbows tight.

## Key steps

1. Frame and bridge.
2. Shrimp away.
3. Turn onto your knees.
4. Elbows to knees, chin down.

## Defense

Follow and take the back or break them down.

1. Stay on their hips.
2. Get the seatbelt.
3. Insert hooks.
