Make space with frames and slide your knee in to recover half guard.

## Key steps

1. Frame on the neck and hip.
2. Bridge into them.
3. Shrimp away and slide your knee into the gap.
4. Trap their leg to recover half guard.

## Defense

Block the near hip with your knee and keep your weight heavy.

1. Keep your knee against their hip.
2. Crossface to stop them turning.
3. Switch base if they get a knee in.
