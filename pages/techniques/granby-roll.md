Roll over your shoulder from turtle to put your legs back in front.

## Key steps

1. Tuck your head to one side.
2. Roll over your shoulder, not your neck.
3. Bring your legs over and in front.
4. Face them in open guard.

## Defense

Stay on their hips and follow the roll to the back.

1. Keep your chest on their back.
2. Follow their hips as they roll.
3. Insert hooks or settle side control.
