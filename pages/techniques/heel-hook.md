Rotational leg lock that twists the heel to attack the knee. Very dangerous: apply slowly and tap early.

## Key steps

1. Control the knee line from the [[Bottom saddle (inside sankaku)]].
2. Trap the heel in the crook of your elbow.
3. Lock your hands together.
4. Rotate your shoulders and body slowly.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom saddle (inside sankaku)
- Your legs are triangled around one of their legs, with their knee trapped between your thighs. No-gi only; often banned for lower belts.

### 1. Control the knee line from the [[Bottom saddle (inside sankaku)]]
- Their knee must be past your hips and trapped; if their knee is free, the heel hook can't be finished safely and they'll escape.

### 2. Trap the heel in the crook of your elbow
- Wrap their foot so the heel sits in the crook of your elbow, the toes under your armpit.

### 3. Lock your hands together
- Lock your hands with a gable or figure-four grip so the heel can't slip out.

### 4. Rotate your shoulders and body slowly
- Turn your shoulders and hips together, slowly, rotating the heel. The pressure goes to the knee, so give them time to tap.

### Common mistakes
- **Cranking fast:** heel hooks damage the knee before it hurts. Always slowly.
- **Knee line free:** they spin out and the rotation goes nowhere.

## Defense

Hide the heel, clear the knee line, and never spin against the lock.

1. Point your toes and hide the heel from their grip.
2. Pull your knee out past their hips (clear the knee line).
3. Fight their grip with your hands.
4. Turn only with the direction of the rotation, never against it.
