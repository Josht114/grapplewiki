Head and arm choke from top half guard, often finished by sliding into mount or side control.

## Key steps

1. Push their arm across their face.
2. Lock your hands behind their neck.
3. Free your leg if possible.
4. Drop your weight and squeeze.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: top half guard
- You're on top in their half guard with a crossface.

### 1. Push their arm across their face
- When they push your head or neck, push that arm across their face and pin it with your head.

### 2. Lock your hands behind their neck
- Wrap your arm behind their neck and lock palm to palm, or grab your other biceps.

### 3. Free your leg if possible
- If you can, free your trapped leg and step out to the side opposite their trapped arm; the choke gets much tighter outside the half guard.

### 4. Drop your weight and squeeze
- Drop your chest toward the mat on the far side, squeeze your elbows and drive your shoulder into the neck.

### Common mistakes
- **Finishing while still stuck in half guard:** you can't get the angle. Free the leg first if you can.
- **Their arm sliding back across:** keep your head tight on it.

## Defense

Keep your elbow down and don't let the arm cross.

1. Keep your underhook arm low.
2. Grab your own head.
3. Turn into them.
