Arm bar that comes from high mount or S-mount when the opponent pushes up on your chest.

## Key steps

1. Climb to high mount and isolate one arm.
2. Step to [[Top mount (S-mount)]] on the side of the arm.
3. Swing your leg over their head and sit close to the shoulder.
4. Pinch the knees, thumb up, and lift the hips.

## Defense

Hitchhiker escape: rotate your arm thumb-out, like a hitchhiker, and turn your body with it so the elbow slips free.

1. Clasp your hands and keep the elbow bent as long as possible.
2. Turn your thumb outward, like a hitchhiker, to rotate the elbow out of line.
3. Walk your feet around toward their head.
4. Pull the elbow out and come up on top.
