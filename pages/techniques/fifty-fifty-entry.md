Entering 50/50 from the top of single leg X.

## Key steps

1. From the top of single leg X, sit down next to their trapped leg.
2. Swing your outside leg over their leg and across their hip.
3. Triangle your legs around their leg.
4. Hug the foot and settle in 50/50.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: top of single leg X
- They're under you in single leg X, holding your trapped leg.

### 1. From the top of single leg X, sit down next to their trapped leg
- Sit down beside their legs, so you're at the same level, facing them.

### 2. Swing your outside leg over their leg and across their hip
- Swing your outside leg over their leg and across their hip.

### 3. Triangle your legs around their leg
- Lock your legs in a triangle around their leg, mirroring theirs.

### 4. Hug the foot and settle in 50/50
- Hug their foot to your chest and settle into 50/50, both of you with a leg entangled.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaving your heel exposed as you enter:** they heel hook first. Hide your heel.

## Defense

Keep your leg out of their legs, or match their entry so you're not caught underneath.

1. Keep your weight on top and your knee pointing away.
2. Pummel your leg free as they sit.
3. If they enter, hide your heel and grip their foot to even it up.
