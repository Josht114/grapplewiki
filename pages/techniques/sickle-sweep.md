Follow-up to the tripod: hold the ankle and chop behind the far heel.

## Key steps

1. Grip the near ankle.
2. Hook your foot behind their far heel.
3. Chop the heel while pulling the ankle.
4. Come up on top.

## Defense

Step the chopped leg back and keep your weight on the front foot.

1. Free your heel.
2. Keep your weight forward.
3. Step back into stance.
