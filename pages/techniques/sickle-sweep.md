Follow-up to the tripod: hold the ankle and chop behind the far heel.

## Key steps

1. Grip the near ankle.
2. Hook your foot behind their far heel.
3. Chop the heel while pulling the ankle.
4. Come up on top.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom open guard
- They're standing in front of you; you're sitting or on your back.

### 1. Grip the near ankle
- Grab their nearest ankle from the outside and pull it toward you, so that foot can't step.

### 2. Hook your foot behind their far heel
- Hook the instep of your other foot behind their far heel.

### 3. Chop the heel while pulling the ankle
- Chop through the heel with your hooking foot, like a sickle, while you keep pulling the near ankle.
- With both feet taken, they fall backwards.

### 4. Come up on top
- Sit up and come up to your knees or feet as they land, into a passing position.

### Common mistakes
- **Not pulling the near ankle:** they step back and stay up.
- **Kicking the calf instead of the heel:** chop low, at the heel.

## Defense

Step the chopped leg back and keep your weight on the front foot.

1. Free your heel.
2. Keep your weight forward.
3. Step back into stance.
