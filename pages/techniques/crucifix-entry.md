From top turtle, trap the near arm with your legs and roll to the crucifix.

## Key steps

1. Hook their near arm with your inside leg.
2. Trap it with your other leg too.
3. Roll or slide onto your side.
4. Control their other arm with your arm.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: top of turtle
- They're turtled. You're at their side with your chest on their back.

### 1. Hook their near arm with your inside leg
- Step your inside leg (the one nearest their head) in under their near arm, between their elbow and knee.

### 2. Trap it with your other leg too
- Bring your other leg over and lock your legs together, figure-four or crossed, with their arm caught between them.
- That arm is now out of the fight.

### 3. Roll or slide onto your side
- Roll over their back toward their far side, or slide onto your side behind them, pulling them with you.
- They end up on their back on top of you, or on their side facing away.

### 4. Control their other arm with your arm
- Wrap their free arm with your arm, so both their arms are trapped: one by your legs, one by your arms.
- From here you can choke the open neck or arm lock the arm in your legs.

### Common mistakes
- **Rolling before the leg trap is locked:** the arm slips out mid-roll.
- **Letting their far arm stay free:** they frame and turn into you.

## Defense

Keep your elbows glued to your knees so an arm can't be hooked.

1. Elbows tight to knees.
2. Pull the arm out as they hook it.
3. Turn into them as they roll.
