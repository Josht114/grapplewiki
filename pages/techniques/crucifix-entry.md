From top turtle, trap the near arm with your legs and roll to the crucifix.

## Key steps

1. Hook their near arm with your inside leg.
2. Trap it with your other leg too.
3. Roll or slide onto your side.
4. Control their other arm with your arm.

## Defense

Keep your elbows glued to your knees so an arm can't be hooked.

1. Elbows tight to knees.
2. Pull the arm out as they hook it.
3. Turn into them as they roll.
