Blood choke from the back: your arm wraps the neck and is locked behind their head with the other arm.

## Key steps

1. From the seatbelt, slide the choking arm under their chin so the elbow lines up with the chin.
2. Grab your own biceps with the choking hand.
3. Put the other hand behind their head.
4. Squeeze your elbows together and expand your chest.

## Defense

Hand-fight the choking arm with both hands and tuck your chin.

1. Chin down, shoulders up.
2. Two-on-one the choking wrist and pull it down.
3. Keep the arm off your neck while you escape.
4. Move to the [[Back escape]] once the choke is clear.

## Notes

Hand fighting is the battle. Win the [[Grip fighting]] first, and keep your hooks or body triangle so they can't turn in.
