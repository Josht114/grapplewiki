Freeing the arm from the lasso and passing.

## Key steps

1. Grip their lasso foot or knee.
2. Turn your arm inward to loosen the lasso.
3. Pull your arm free and step back.
4. Pin the leg and step around to side control.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: top of lasso guard
- Their leg wraps around your arm (the lasso) and they grip your sleeve. Gi only.

### 1. Grip their lasso foot or knee
- Grab the foot or knee of the lassoing leg so you can control it.

### 2. Turn your arm inward to loosen the lasso
- Turn your elbow inward and your hand toward your body, loosening the wrap.

### 3. Pull your arm free and step back
- Pull your arm out of the lasso and step back to make distance.

### 4. Pin the leg and step around to side control
- Pin their leg to the mat and step around into side control.

### Common mistakes
- **Pulling your arm straight out:** the lasso tightens. Turn it first.

## Defense

Keep the lasso deep and your sleeve grip tight so they can't free their arm.

1. Keep your foot deep over their arm.
2. Keep the sleeve grip on the lassoed arm.
3. Pull them forward and off balance when they try to free it.
