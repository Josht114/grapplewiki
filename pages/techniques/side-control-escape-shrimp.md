Frame on the opponent, shrimp away, and put your knee back in to recover guard, or half guard if you only make room for one knee.

## Key steps

1. Frame one forearm on the neck, the other hand on the hip.
2. Bridge into them to lift their weight.
3. Shrimp away to create space.
4. Slide your knee into the gap and recover guard.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom side control
- They're on top across your chest, crossface and underhook.

### 1. Frame one forearm on the neck, the other hand on the hip
- Put your top forearm against their neck or collarbone and your bottom hand on their hip. Frames don't push; they hold space.

### 2. Bridge into them to lift their weight
- Bridge your hips up into them so their weight lifts for a moment.

### 3. Shrimp away to create space
- As you come down, shrimp your hips away from them, making a gap between your hips and theirs.

### 4. Slide your knee into the gap and recover guard
- Slide your bottom knee into the gap in front of their hips. Get it high in the gap, or they step over it.
- **Two outcomes:**
- **Enough space:** bring both legs in front of their hips and recover open guard.
- **Only room for one knee** (they're heavy or follow your shrimp): hook your knee under their near leg and lock your legs around it to recover [[Bottom half guard]].

### Common mistakes
- **Pushing with straight arms:** they lean on you and you tire. Frame, don't push.
- **Shrimping without bridging first:** their weight pins your hips.
- **Knee too low:** they step over it. Get it high in the gap.

## Defense

Crossface and underhook, and keep your weight heavy on their hips.

1. Crossface to turn their head away.
2. Block the near hip with your knee.
3. Follow their shrimp.
4. Switch base if they get a knee in.
