Close your legs around the opponent's waist from open guard.

## Key steps

1. Pull them in with your grips.
2. Wrap both legs around their waist.
3. Lock your ankles.
4. Break their posture.

## Defense

Keep your hips back and don't let them pull you in.

1. Keep your posture.
2. Hips back.
3. Control their knees and pass.
