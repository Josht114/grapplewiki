Shoulder lock from open guard, swinging your leg over the opponent's shoulder.

## Key steps

1. Control their wrist and angle off.
2. Swing your leg over their shoulder from the outside.
3. Sit up and face the same way as them.
4. Control their hips and lean forward.

## Defense

Roll forward before the lock is set, or posture up and grab their pants.

1. Keep your elbow tight.
2. Grab their pants or belt as the leg comes over.
3. Roll forward over the trapped shoulder.
