Shoulder lock from open guard, swinging your leg over the opponent's shoulder.

## Key steps

1. Control their wrist and angle off.
2. Swing your leg over their shoulder from the outside.
3. Sit up and face the same way as them.
4. Control their hips and lean forward.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom open guard
- You have a grip on one of their wrists or sleeves.

### 1. Control their wrist and angle off
- Grip the wrist and swing your hips out to the same side, so you're at an angle to them.

### 2. Swing your leg over their shoulder from the outside
- Bring the leg on that side up and over their shoulder from the outside, hooking behind their armpit, so their arm is trapped between your legs and your body.

### 3. Sit up and face the same way as them
- Turn and sit up beside them, facing the same direction they do, their arm locked by your legs.

### 4. Control their hips and lean forward
- Wrap their far hip so they can't roll out, then lean forward and slightly away; the shoulder rotates and they tap.

### Common mistakes
- **Not controlling the hips:** they forward-roll out of it.
- **Lying on your back after the leg goes over:** sit up, or there's no pressure on the shoulder.

## Defense

Roll forward before the lock is set, or posture up and grab their pants.

1. Keep your elbow tight.
2. Grab their pants or belt as the leg comes over.
3. Roll forward over the trapped shoulder.
