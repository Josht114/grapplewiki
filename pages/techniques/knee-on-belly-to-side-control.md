Drop your chest back to side control when the opponent turns into you or makes space.

## Key steps

1. Feel them turn into you.
2. Slide your knee off and down to the mat.
3. Drop your chest onto theirs and settle with the crossface and underhook.

## Defense

Recover guard while they drop back, before the chest connects.

1. Shrimp as their knee comes off.
2. Bring your knee into the space.
3. Recover guard.
