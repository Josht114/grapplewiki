Drop your chest back to side control when the opponent turns into you or makes space.

## Key steps

1. Feel them turn into you.
2. Slide your knee off and down to the mat.
3. Drop your chest onto theirs and settle with the crossface and underhook.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: knee on belly
- They turn into you to recover guard.

### 1. Feel them turn into you
- When they turn their hips toward you, the knee on belly is about to be lost.

### 2. Slide your knee off and down to the mat
- Drop the knee to the mat right against their hip, blocking their knees from coming in.

### 3. Drop your chest onto theirs and settle with the crossface and underhook
- Fall chest to chest, crossface on their head and underhook their far arm.

### Common mistakes
- **Staying up while they turn:** they get a knee in front of you and recover guard.

## Defense

Recover guard while they drop back, before the chest connects.

1. Shrimp as their knee comes off.
2. Bring your knee into the space.
3. Recover guard.
