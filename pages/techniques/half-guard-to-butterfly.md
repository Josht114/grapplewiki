Free your outside leg and insert butterfly hooks from half guard.

## Key steps

1. Frame on their hips.
2. Pull your outside leg through.
3. Insert both feet as butterfly hooks.
4. Sit up and get an underhook.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom half guard
- They're on top in your half guard, not yet flattening you.

### 1. Frame on their hips
- Put your hands or forearms on their hips and push to create space between you.

### 2. Pull your outside leg through
- Pull your outside (top) knee in between you, so your shin is in front of them.

### 3. Insert both feet as butterfly hooks
- Unlock your legs and slide both insteps inside their thighs.

### 4. Sit up and get an underhook
- Sit up, pummel an arm under their armpit, and come chest to chest with your head high.

### Common mistakes
- **Not framing first:** without space you can't get the knee through.
- **Staying flat:** sit up or they push you back down.

## Defense

Keep your hips heavy and flatten them before they sit up.

1. Crossface.
2. Keep weight forward.
3. Pin their legs.
