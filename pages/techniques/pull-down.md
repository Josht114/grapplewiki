Bringing a standing opponent to the mat from behind while keeping back control.

## Key steps

1. Lock your hands around their waist from behind.
2. Step to one side and sit your hips down.
3. Pull them down onto your lap.
4. Insert your hooks as they land.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: behind a standing opponent
- You're behind them with your hands locked around their waist.

### 1. Lock your hands around their waist from behind
- Lock your hands with a gable or wrist grip around their waist, chest on their back.

### 2. Step to one side and sit your hips down
- Step to one side and sit down, staying tight to them.

### 3. Pull them down onto your lap
- Pull them down with you so they sit on your lap.

### 4. Insert your hooks as they land
- As they land, put your hooks in and switch to a seatbelt.

### Common mistakes
- **Sitting too far behind:** they stand up out of it.

## Defense

Keep your weight forward and break the grip before they can sit you down.

1. Stay low with your weight forward.
2. Peel their hands apart at the wrists.
3. Turn into them as they sit.
