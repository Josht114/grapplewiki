Arm triangle: their own shoulder and your arm close off the neck.

## Key steps

1. Push their arm across their face and drop your head on it.
2. Wrap your arm behind their neck and lock your hands.
3. Dismount to the side of your choking arm.
4. Squeeze, driving your shoulder into theirs.

## Defense

Keep your arm from being pushed across your face, or turn into them before they dismount.

1. Keep your elbow down and tight.
2. If the arm crosses, grab your own collar or head with that hand.
3. Turn into them and make space with your shoulder.
4. Keep them from dismounting to the side.
