Close the distance from standing and lock your hands around the opponent's waist.

## Key steps

1. Pummel for underhooks.
2. Step in close with your head tight to their chest.
3. Lock your hands low around their waist.
4. Pull their hips into you.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: standing
- You're in an upper-body tie-up, hand fighting.

### 1. Pummel for underhooks
- Swim your arms inside theirs so your arms end up under their armpits.

### 2. Step in close with your head tight to their chest
- Step your hips in close, head on their chest or the side of their head, so they can't lever your head down.

### 3. Lock your hands low around their waist
- Lock your hands behind their lower back, low near the hips (gable or wrist grip).

### 4. Pull their hips into you
- Pull their hips tight to yours; from here you can lift, trip or take them down.

### Common mistakes
- **Lock too high on the ribs:** they can move their hips away. Lock low.
- **Head away from them:** they push on it and break your posture.

## Defense

Win the underhooks and keep your hips back.

1. Pummel your arms inside.
2. Frame on their head.
3. Step your hips back.
