Figure-four shoulder lock that rotates the arm behind the opponent's back.

## Key steps

1. Grip the opponent's wrist with your near-side hand.
2. Thread your other arm under their triceps and grab your own wrist.
3. Keep their elbow tight to your chest and bend the arm to 90°.
4. Rotate the hand toward their head and behind their back for the finish.

## Defense

Grab your own belt, pants or gi to stop the arm from rotating, then reposition.

1. Grab your belt or pants with the attacked hand.
2. Keep the elbow tight to your body.
3. Turn into them to take the angle away.
4. Free the arm and recover guard.
