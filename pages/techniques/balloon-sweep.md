Roll back with both feet on the opponent's hips and launch them over your head, following them into mount.

## Key steps

1. Grip their collar and sleeve (or both sleeves).
2. Put both feet on their hips.
3. Pull them forward as you roll back.
4. Extend your legs to send them overhead and follow into mount.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom open guard
- They're standing or leaning over you. You have grips on their collar and sleeve, or both sleeves.

### 1. Grip their collar and sleeve (or both sleeves)
- Strong grips you can pull with; they're what keep them on your feet.

### 2. Put both feet on their hips
- Place both feet on their hip bones, knees bent, your hips close under them.

### 3. Pull them forward as you roll back
- Pull your grips and roll back onto your shoulders, so their weight comes forward onto your feet.

### 4. Extend your legs to send them overhead and follow into mount
- Straighten your legs up and over your head to send them flying over you, keeping the grips.
- Do a backward roll over your shoulder to follow them and land in mount.

### Common mistakes
- **Feet too low on their thighs:** you push them away instead of lifting.
- **Letting go of the grips:** they land and get away, or land in your guard.

## Defense

Keep your weight back and stay off their feet.

1. Don't lean over them.
2. Step back off the feet on your hips.
3. Base out with your hands.
