Mount escape: trap one arm and the foot on the same side, then bridge over that shoulder to roll the opponent.

## Key steps

1. Trap their arm by hugging it to your chest.
2. Trap their foot on the same side with your foot.
3. Bridge high over the trapped shoulder.
4. Land in their closed guard with good posture.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom mount
- They're mounted, posting a hand or leaning forward.

### 1. Trap their arm by hugging it to your chest
- Hug one of their arms to your chest (wrap it at the elbow, or grab the wrist), so they can't post with it.

### 2. Trap their foot on the same side with your foot
- Put your foot on the outside of their foot on the same side, so they can't step out.

### 3. Bridge high over the trapped shoulder
- Bridge your hips up as high as you can and turn over your shoulder toward the trapped side.

### 4. Land in their closed guard with good posture
- They roll over; land on top, in their guard, and posture up straight away.

### Common mistakes
- **Bridging straight up:** they just ride it. Bridge over your shoulder to the trapped side.
- **Missing the foot:** they step out and post.

## Defense

Post your hand and foot wide on the side they bridge toward.

1. Keep your hands off their chest.
2. Post wide when they bridge.
3. Climb back to low mount.
