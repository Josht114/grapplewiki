Mount escape: trap one arm and the foot on the same side, then bridge over that shoulder to roll the opponent.

## Key steps

1. Trap their arm by hugging it to your chest.
2. Trap their foot on the same side with your foot.
3. Bridge high over the trapped shoulder.
4. Land in their closed guard with good posture.

## Defense

Post your hand and foot wide on the side they bridge toward.

1. Keep your hands off their chest.
2. Post wide when they bridge.
3. Climb back to low mount.

## Notes

Not to be confused with the [[Arm roll (turtle)]].
