When the opponent turns to their knees to escape side control, follow them and take the back.

## Key steps

1. Feel them turn away toward their knees.
2. Get a seatbelt grip as they turn.
3. Insert your near hook as they come up.
4. Roll to your side and insert the second hook.

## Defense

Turn into them, not away, so you never show your back.

1. Turn toward them as you escape.
2. If you do turn away, keep elbows tight to your knees.
3. Hand-fight the seatbelt before the hooks go in.
