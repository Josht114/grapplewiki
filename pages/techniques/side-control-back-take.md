When the opponent turns to their knees to escape side control, follow them and take the back.

## Key steps

1. Feel them turn away toward their knees.
2. Get a seatbelt grip as they turn.
3. Insert your near hook as they come up.
4. Roll to your side and insert the second hook.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: side control
- You're on top; they're trying to escape by turning away toward their knees.

### 1. Feel them turn away toward their knees
- Let them turn; don't fight it. Their back is now coming toward you.

### 2. Get a seatbelt grip as they turn
- Wrap one arm over their shoulder and one under their armpit and lock your hands on their chest.

### 3. Insert your near hook as they come up
- As their hips rise, slip your near foot in front of their hip as the first hook.

### 4. Roll to your side and insert the second hook
- Fall to your side (the side of your underhook arm) and slide the second hook in. You're now in back control.

### Common mistakes
- **Chasing hooks before the seatbelt:** they spin back in or stand up.
- **Rolling to the wrong side:** you end up under them with the hooks gone.

## Defense

Turn into them, not away, so you never show your back.

1. Turn toward them as you escape.
2. If you do turn away, keep elbows tight to your knees.
3. Hand-fight the seatbelt before the hooks go in.
