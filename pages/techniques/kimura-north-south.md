Figure-four shoulder lock on the near arm while you're in north south.

## Key steps

1. Trap their near arm off the mat.
2. Grip their wrist and thread your other arm under to grab your wrist.
3. Sit your hips back to keep their shoulder down.
4. Rotate the arm up toward their head.

## Defense

Grab your own belt or pants to stop the rotation, then turn into them.

1. Keep your near arm tight to your body.
2. Grab your belt, pants or gi.
3. Turn toward their legs to take away the angle.
