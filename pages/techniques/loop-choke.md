Gi choke from a collar grip that loops around the opponent's neck when they shoot in or drop their head.

## Key steps

1. Get a deep collar grip, palm down, while their head is low.
2. Push their head under your arm.
3. Loop the arm over the back of the neck.
4. Roll to the side and drive their head down.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: seated open guard
- They're on top or kneeling in front of you, often trying to drop their head or shoot. Gi only.

### 1. Get a deep collar grip, palm down, while their head is low
- Grip their collar deep and palm down, close to the neck.

### 2. Push their head under your arm
- Push their head down under your gripping arm.

### 3. Loop the arm over the back of the neck
- Bring your gripping arm over the back of their neck, making a loop.

### 4. Roll to the side and drive their head down
- Roll to the side of your gripping arm and push their head down with your other hand or forearm; the collar loop chokes them.

### Common mistakes
- **Shallow grip:** no loop. Go deep and close to the neck.

## Defense

Keep your head up and don't dive in while they hold a deep collar grip.

1. Keep your head up and posture strong.
2. Strip the collar grip.
3. Move your head out of the loop.
4. Pass toward the side of the grip.
