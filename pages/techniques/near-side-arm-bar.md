Trap the near arm when the opponent pushes your knee, then spin around their head into an arm bar.

## Key steps

1. Wait for them to push the knee with their near arm.
2. Trap that arm against your body.
3. Step around their head, keeping the knee heavy.
4. Sit back, pinch your knees and lift your hips.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: knee on belly
- They're pushing your knee with the near arm.

### 1. Wait for them to push the knee with their near arm
- The near arm straightens to push your knee.

### 2. Trap that arm against your body
- Hug the arm to your chest or trap it under your armpit.

### 3. Step around their head, keeping the knee heavy
- Walk around their head while your knee keeps weight on them.

### 4. Sit back, pinch your knees and lift your hips
- Sit close to their shoulder, pinch your knees, thumb up, lift your hips.

### Common mistakes
- **Losing the knee pressure as you step:** they sit up and stack you.

## Defense

Push the knee with your forearm and elbow tight, not a straight arm.

1. Frame with a bent arm.
2. Pull the arm in as soon as they trap it.
3. Turn into them as they spin.
