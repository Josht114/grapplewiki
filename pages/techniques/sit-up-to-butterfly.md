Sit up from open guard and slide your feet in as butterfly hooks.

## Key steps

1. Post on your hand and sit up.
2. Bring your knees in.
3. Slide both feet inside their thighs.
4. Get an underhook.

## Defense

Push their head or shoulder back down as they sit up.

1. Control their head.
2. Keep your hips back.
3. Pass as they sit up.
