Hyperextends the elbow from closed guard after breaking the opponent's posture.

## Key steps

1. Break their posture and control one arm across your chest.
2. Put a foot on their hip and pivot your body perpendicular to theirs.
3. Swing your leg over their face.
4. Pinch your knees, thumb up, and lift your hips.

## Defense

Hide the elbow, stack the opponent and pull the arm out before the hips lift.

1. Keep your elbows inside your knees.
2. Grip your own wrist or lapel.
3. Stack them by standing and driving forward.
4. Rotate the thumb down and pull out.

## Notes

Chains well with the [[Triangle choke]] and [[Omoplata]].
