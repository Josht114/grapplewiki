Sit through from turtle to turn into the opponent and come up.

## Key steps

1. Post on your near hand.
2. Thread your outside leg through underneath you.
3. Turn to face them.
4. Come up to your feet.

## Defense

Follow their hips and keep your chest on their back.

1. Stay on their hips.
2. Block the threading leg.
3. Take the back as they turn.
