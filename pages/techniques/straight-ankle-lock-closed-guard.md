After standing to open the closed guard, trap a leg and fall back for a straight ankle lock.

## Key steps

1. Stand and break the closed guard.
2. Trap one leg under your arm as it opens.
3. Blade your forearm under the Achilles.
4. Fall back with your knees pinched and arch.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: inside their closed guard
- You've postured up and are standing to open their guard.

### 1. Stand and break the closed guard
- Stand up with good posture and step a leg back, or drop a knee, so their ankles unlock.

### 2. Trap one leg under your arm as it opens
- As their legs open, wrap one leg under your armpit on the same side, their foot pointing behind you.

### 3. Blade your forearm under the Achilles
- Slide your forearm under their leg so the bony edge of your wrist sits on the Achilles tendon, just above the heel. Grip your other forearm or their shin.

### 4. Fall back with your knees pinched and arch
- Sit and fall back to the side, pinch your knees together on their leg, then arch your back and push your hips forward so the wrist drives up into the tendon.

### Common mistakes
- **Forearm high on the calf:** no pressure. It has to be on the Achilles.
- **Knees apart:** they pull the leg out or kick you off.

## Defense

Re-guard or sit up as soon as your legs open, keeping your feet away from their armpit.

1. Flex your foot.
2. Sit up into them and grab their collar.
3. Pull your foot out as they fall.
