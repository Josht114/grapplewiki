After standing to open the closed guard, trap a leg and fall back for a straight ankle lock.

## Key steps

1. Stand and break the closed guard.
2. Trap one leg under your arm as it opens.
3. Blade your forearm under the Achilles.
4. Fall back with your knees pinched and arch.

## Defense

Re-guard or sit up as soon as your legs open, keeping your feet away from their armpit.

1. Flex your foot.
2. Sit up into them and grab their collar.
3. Pull your foot out as they fall.
