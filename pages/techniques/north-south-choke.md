Choke from north south using the side of your chest and arm against the neck.

## Key steps

1. Thread an arm under their neck and grip near their armpit.
2. Line up the side of your chest with their neck.
3. Drop your hips and sprawl to close the choke.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: north south
- Your chest is on theirs, head toward their hips.

### 1. Thread an arm under their neck and grip near their armpit
- Slide one arm under their neck; grip their far side near the armpit (or your other hand, or the mat).

### 2. Line up the side of your chest with their neck
- Shift your body sideways so the side of your chest (around your armpit and lats) sits on the side of their neck.
- The choke comes from your chest pressing one side of the neck while your arm presses the other.

### 3. Drop your hips and sprawl to close the choke
- Sprawl your legs out and drop your hips toward the mat; your chest presses down into the neck.
- Don't squeeze with the arm; drop the weight.

### Common mistakes
- **Chest on their face instead of the neck:** it's just uncomfortable. Shift the angle.
- **Hips up in the air:** no pressure. Drop them.

## Defense

Keep your near arm inside and turn toward the opponent so their chest can't seal your neck.

1. Keep your near arm tight, elbow by your ribs.
2. Frame on their hips.
3. Turn into them to take the angle away.
4. Recover guard as the pressure eases.
