Trap the opponent's arm and bridge them over you to reverse side control.

## Key steps

1. Trap their near arm to your chest.
2. Bring your feet close to your hips.
3. Bridge high toward the trapped side.
4. Roll them over and come up on top.

## Defense

Keep your weight low and post when they bridge.

1. Free your arm.
2. Post wide.
3. Stay heavy on their chest.
