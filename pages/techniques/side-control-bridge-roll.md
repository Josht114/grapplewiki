Trap the opponent's arm and bridge them over you to reverse side control.

## Key steps

1. Trap their near arm to your chest.
2. Bring your feet close to your hips.
3. Bridge high toward the trapped side.
4. Roll them over and come up on top.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom side control
- They're across your chest.

### 1. Trap their near arm to your chest
- Hug their near arm (the one by your hips or head) tight to your chest so they can't post with it.

### 2. Bring your feet close to your hips
- Walk your heels close to your butt so you can bridge hard.

### 3. Bridge high toward the trapped side
- Bridge your hips up and over your shoulder toward the side of the trapped arm.

### 4. Roll them over and come up on top
- They can't post, so they roll over; come up on top in their guard or side control.

### Common mistakes
- **Bridging straight up:** bridge over your shoulder toward the trapped arm.
- **Arm not trapped:** they just post and stay on top.

## Defense

Keep your weight low and post when they bridge.

1. Free your arm.
2. Post wide.
3. Stay heavy on their chest.
