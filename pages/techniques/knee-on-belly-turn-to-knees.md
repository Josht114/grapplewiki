Push the knee off and turn to your knees.

## Key steps

1. Push the knee down.
2. Turn onto your side.
3. Come up to your knees.
4. Elbows tight.

## Defense

Follow and take the back.

1. Stay close.
2. Seatbelt.
3. Hooks.
