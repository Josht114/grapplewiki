Push the opponent flat and step over a hook so you land in top half guard.

## Key steps

1. Push their shoulders flat to the mat.
2. Pin one hook with your knee.
3. Step over that leg.
4. Settle into top half guard.

## Defense

Keep sitting up; don't let them push you flat.

1. Keep your head higher than theirs.
2. Get an underhook.
3. Re-hook as they step.
