Step your leg over the opponent's head from side control and sit back for an arm bar on the near arm.

## Key steps

1. Isolate their near arm against your chest.
2. Post on your hand and step your leg over their head.
3. Sit back close to their shoulder, keeping the arm tight.
4. Pinch your knees, thumb up, and lift your hips.

## Defense

Keep your near elbow tight to your ribs and turn into them as they step over.

1. Don't leave your near arm hanging up.
2. Clasp your hands together.
3. Turn into them and stack them as they sit back.
