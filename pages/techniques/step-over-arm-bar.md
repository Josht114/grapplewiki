Step your leg over the opponent's head from side control and sit back for an arm bar on the near arm.

## Key steps

1. Isolate their near arm against your chest.
2. Post on your hand and step your leg over their head.
3. Sit back close to their shoulder, keeping the arm tight.
4. Pinch your knees, thumb up, and lift your hips.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: side control
- You're on top with their near arm loose or reaching up.

### 1. Isolate their near arm against your chest
- Trap the arm to your chest, with their elbow pointing away from you.

### 2. Post on your hand and step your leg over their head
- Post a hand on the mat by their head and step your far leg over their head.

### 3. Sit back close to their shoulder, keeping the arm tight
- Sit your butt down right next to their shoulder, arm hugged to your chest.

### 4. Pinch your knees, thumb up, and lift your hips
- Pinch your knees around the arm, turn their thumb to the ceiling, and lift your hips slowly.

### Common mistakes
- **Sitting far from the shoulder:** the arm slips out. Sit close.
- **Thumb pointing sideways:** the elbow isn't aligned; turn the thumb up.

## Defense

Keep your near elbow tight to your ribs and turn into them as they step over.

1. Don't leave your near arm hanging up.
2. Clasp your hands together.
3. Turn into them and stack them as they sit back.
