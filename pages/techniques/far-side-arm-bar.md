When the opponent pushes your knee with their far arm, step over their head and arm bar it.

## Key steps

1. Wait for them to push on your knee or hip with the far arm.
2. Grab that arm and keep it to your chest.
3. Step your far leg over their head.
4. Sit back, pinch your knees and lift your hips.

## Defense

Don't push with a straight arm; frame with bent arms and keep your elbows tight.

1. Push the knee with your forearm, not a straight arm.
2. If the leg comes over your head, turn toward your trapped arm.
3. Stack them or pull the elbow out as they sit back.
