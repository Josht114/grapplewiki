Shoulder lock from mount on an arm pinned beside the opponent's head.

## Key steps

1. Pin their wrist to the mat with their elbow bent at 90°.
2. Thread your other arm under their elbow and grab your own wrist.
3. Keep your chest low over their arm.
4. Slide the wrist toward their hip and lift the elbow.

## Defense

Keep your elbows tight to your body so your arm can't be pinned beside your head.

1. Hide your elbows by your ribs.
2. If the wrist is pinned, straighten the arm.
3. Bridge to break their base.
