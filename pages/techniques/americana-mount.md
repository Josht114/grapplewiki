Shoulder lock from mount on an arm pinned beside the opponent's head.

## Key steps

1. Pin their wrist to the mat with their elbow bent at 90°.
2. Thread your other arm under their elbow and grab your own wrist.
3. Keep your chest low over their arm.
4. Slide the wrist toward their hip and lift the elbow.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: mount
- You're mounted with your hips low and they push up at you with their arms.

### 1. Pin their wrist to the mat with their elbow bent at 90°
- When an arm comes up, push it to the mat beside their head, elbow bent like an L.
- Put your weight through your hand onto their wrist.

### 2. Thread your other arm under their elbow and grab your own wrist
- Slide your other hand under their upper arm and grab your own wrist: the figure-four.

### 3. Keep your chest low over their arm
- Drop your chest close to the arm so they can't bridge it free or turn into you.

### 4. Slide the wrist toward their hip and lift the elbow
- Paint their hand along the mat toward their hip, then lift the elbow a little. Go slowly.

### Common mistakes
- **Sitting upright:** you lose the angle and they bridge you off.
- **Wrist leaving the mat:** keep it down the whole time.

## Defense

Keep your elbows tight to your body so your arm can't be pinned beside your head.

1. Hide your elbows by your ribs.
2. If the wrist is pinned, straighten the arm.
3. Bridge to break their base.
