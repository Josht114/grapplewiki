From deep half, lift the opponent's far leg like a waiter's tray and tip them over.

## Key steps

1. Establish deep half guard.
2. Put a hand under their far leg.
3. Lift the leg as you push into the trapped leg.
4. Come up on top.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom deep half guard
- You're under their hips, hugging their leg with both arms, your legs locked around it.

### 1. Establish deep half guard
- Get your head and shoulders under their hips and hug the trapped leg tight to your chest.

### 2. Put a hand under their far leg
- Reach one hand under their free leg, behind the knee or ankle, like holding a waiter's tray.

### 3. Lift the leg as you push into the trapped leg
- Lift the free leg up while you push with your legs into the trapped leg; with both legs taken, they tip over to the side.

### 4. Come up on top
- Follow them as they fall, come up on top, and settle into half guard or pass.

### Common mistakes
- **Lifting before you're deep under them:** they sprawl their hips onto you.
- **Not pushing the trapped leg:** they just stand on it.

## Defense

Keep your weight forward and your free leg posted wide, so they can't lift it from deep half.

1. Keep your weight forward over their head.
2. Post your free leg wide and out of reach.
3. Work to free your trapped leg or switch your hips to face them.
