Arm-in choke from the front headlock, finished by stepping a leg over the opponent's back and sitting to the side.

## Key steps

1. Lock an arm-in grip (head and one arm) from the front headlock.
2. Step your leg over their back on the side of their head.
3. Sit down to the side, keeping the grip.
4. Drive your legs down onto their back to finish.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: the front headlock
- They're on their knees or all fours, head under your armpit, one arm trapped with the head.

### 1. Lock an arm-in grip (head and one arm) from the front headlock
- Wrap their head and one arm, and gable or biceps grip like an arm-in guillotine.
- Keep the grip tight to your chest.

### 2. Step your leg over their back on the side of their head
- Step the leg on the side of their head up and over their back, so your thigh lies across their shoulders.

### 3. Sit down to the side, keeping the grip
- Sit to your hip on the side of their head, keeping their head tight under your arm.
- Bring your second leg over their back too.

### 4. Drive your legs down onto their back to finish
- With both legs across their back, drive them down toward the mat.
- The pressure through their back folds them over your arm and closes the choke.

### Common mistakes
- **Loose grip:** if the head slips out as you sit, you end up underneath them.
- **Sitting too far away:** sit right beside their head so your legs land high on their back.

## Defense

Get your trapped arm out and your head up before they sit and swing their legs over.

1. Pull your trapped arm out and down.
2. Square your hips and drive your head up.
3. Turn into them as they sit so they can't get their legs on your back.
