Push the knee off, shrimp away and recover open guard.

## Key steps

1. Push the knee down toward your hips.
2. Shrimp away from the knee.
3. Turn in toward them.
4. Bring your feet back in front.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom knee on belly
- Their knee is driving into your belly, far leg posted wide.

### 1. Push the knee down toward your hips
- Put both hands (or a hand and forearm) on their knee and push it down toward your hips. Pushing it up toward your chest only adds weight.

### 2. Shrimp away from the knee
- As the knee slides down, shrimp your hips away from it, making space between you.

### 3. Turn in toward them
- Turn onto your side facing them, so your knees can come between you.

### 4. Bring your feet back in front
- Bring your knees in and put your feet on their hips or legs: open guard again.

### Common mistakes
- **Turning away from them:** you give up your back.
- **Pushing with straight arms on their belt:** they just lean in. Move the knee and your hips.

## Defense

Ride their movement and step to mount or back to side control.

1. Keep the knee heavy.
2. Follow their hips.
3. Step to mount as they push.
