Push the knee off, shrimp away and recover open guard.

## Key steps

1. Push the knee down toward your hips.
2. Shrimp away from the knee.
3. Turn in toward them.
4. Bring your feet back in front.

## Defense

Ride their movement and step to mount or back to side control.

1. Keep the knee heavy.
2. Follow their hips.
3. Step to mount as they push.
