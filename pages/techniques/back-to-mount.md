When the opponent escapes their back to the mat, follow over the top into mount.

## Key steps

1. Feel them slide toward your bottom hook.
2. Keep the top hook and swing your hips over.
3. Land on their chest as their back hits the mat.
4. Settle into mount.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: back control
- They're escaping by sliding their shoulders to the mat on your bottom-hook side.

### 1. Feel them slide toward your bottom hook
- Their back is about to hit the mat; stop fighting it.

### 2. Keep the top hook and swing your hips over
- Use your top leg to stay connected and swing your hips over them as they flatten.

### 3. Land on their chest as their back hits the mat
- Come down chest to chest on top.

### 4. Settle into mount
- Knees tight to their sides, hips low, posture up.

### Common mistakes
- **Holding on to the back too long:** they escape to guard. Switch to mount.

## Defense

Turn into them as you land so they can't step over into mount.

1. Clear the top hook first.
2. Turn your chest toward them as your back hits the mat.
3. Put your knee between you to recover half guard.
