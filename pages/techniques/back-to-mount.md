When the opponent escapes their back to the mat, follow over the top into mount.

## Key steps

1. Feel them slide toward your bottom hook.
2. Keep the top hook and swing your hips over.
3. Land on their chest as their back hits the mat.
4. Settle into mount.

## Defense

Turn into them as you land so they can't step over into mount.

1. Clear the top hook first.
2. Turn your chest toward them as your back hits the mat.
3. Put your knee between you to recover half guard.
