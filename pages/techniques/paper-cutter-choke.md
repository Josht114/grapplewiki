Gi choke from side control, using a deep collar grip and a forearm that cuts across the neck.

## Key steps

1. Feed your near hand deep into the far collar.
2. Drop your other forearm across their throat.
3. Drive your elbow toward the mat like closing a paper cutter.

## Defense

Block the deep collar grip and keep the forearm off your neck.

1. Keep your far collar covered.
2. Turn your chin toward the grip.
3. Frame on the forearm.
4. Escape the hips.
