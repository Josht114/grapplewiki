Pull the opponent's head down sharply to their knees and land in the front headlock.

## Key steps

1. Collar tie and triceps control.
2. Push into them so they push back.
3. Snap the head down with your body weight.
4. Sprawl into the [[Top front headlock]].

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: standing
- You're in a collar tie (hand behind their neck), the other hand on their triceps or wrist.

### 1. Collar tie and triceps control
- Collar tie with one hand; the other controls their arm at the triceps, so they can't post on you.

### 2. Push into them so they push back
- Push your head and the collar tie into them. They push back to stay upright; now their weight is coming forward.

### 3. Snap the head down with your body weight
- Step back and drop your weight sharply, pulling their head down with the collar tie, using your body, not just your arm.
- Their hands go to the mat to catch themselves.

### 4. Sprawl into the [[Top front headlock]]
- Sprawl your hips back and land with your chest on the back of their head and shoulders.

### Common mistakes
- **Snapping with only your arm:** they just pull their head back up.
- **Snapping when they're pulling away:** wait until they lean into you.

## Defense

Keep your head up and your posture back.

1. Resist the pull with your neck.
2. Step back with your hips.
3. Hand-fight the collar tie.
