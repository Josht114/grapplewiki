Elbow-knee escape on both sides to recover full guard.

## Key steps

1. Frame on the hip.
2. Shrimp and bring one knee through.
3. Shrimp the other way and bring the other knee through.
4. Close your guard.

## Defense

Grapevine and stay heavy; pull the knee back out.

1. Grapevine.
2. Keep knees tight to their hips.
3. Pull the knee out.
