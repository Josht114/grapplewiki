When the guard player hooks a foot in your hip, fall back with the leg trapped for a straight ankle lock.

## Key steps

1. Trap their foot in your armpit as they hook in.
2. Blade your forearm under their Achilles.
3. Fall back to the side, keeping your knees pinched on their leg.
4. Arch back to finish.

## Defense

Flex your foot and sit up into them before they fall back.

1. Flex your foot (toes to shin).
2. Sit up and grab their collar or arm.
3. Push the forearm down off your Achilles and pull the foot out.
