Escape the back and turn into the opponent to recover guard.

## Key steps

1. Hand-fight the choke.
2. Slide your shoulders to the mat.
3. Clear the top hook.
4. Turn in and recover guard.

## Defense

Follow their hips and keep the top hook.

1. Body triangle.
2. Follow the hips.
3. Mount as they turn.
