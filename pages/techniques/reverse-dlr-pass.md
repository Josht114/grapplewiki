Passing the reverse De La Riva guard.

## Key steps

1. Grip their reverse DLR foot at the ankle.
2. Push the hook off the inside of your leg.
3. Turn your lead knee in and drop it to the mat.
4. Pin their far leg and step around to side control.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: top of reverse De La Riva
- Their leg is hooked on the inside of your lead leg, and they hold your far leg or sleeve. They're looking to invert for the back or sweep you.

### 1. Grip their reverse DLR foot at the ankle
- Grab the hooking foot at the ankle or heel with the hand on that side, so you control where it goes.

### 2. Push the hook off the inside of your leg
- Push the foot out and down, away from the inside of your knee, so the hook comes off.
- Keep your hips back while you do it; leaning forward gives them the invert.

### 3. Turn your lead knee in and drop it to the mat
- Turn your freed knee inward and drop it to the mat between their legs, so they can't re-hook.

### 4. Pin their far leg and step around to side control
- Push their other leg down with your hand, step around their legs, and drop your chest into side control.

### Common mistakes
- **Leaving your lead leg in the hook while you try to pass:** they invert under you to the back (the kiss of the dragon).
- **Standing tall and square:** they off-balance you backward. Keep your base low.

## Defense

Keep the reverse De La Riva hook connected and your grip on their far leg so they can't clear the hook and step around.

1. Keep the hook deep on the inside of their lead leg.
2. Keep your grip on their far leg.
3. Use the hook to off-balance them when they push on it.
4. Re-hook or invert as they step.
