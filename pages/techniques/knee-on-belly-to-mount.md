Step over into mount when the opponent pushes your knee or turns away.

## Key steps

1. Feel them push the knee down or turn.
2. Slide the knee across their belly.
3. Step your far leg over.
4. Settle into mount.

## Defense

Push the knee toward your hips and turn in, so there's no room to step over.

1. Frame on the knee and hip.
2. Turn into them, not away.
3. Bring your knee in as they move.
