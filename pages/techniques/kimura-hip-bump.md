Sit up, post on a hand and bump the opponent over with your hips. Chains to the kimura if they post.

## Key steps

1. Sit up onto your elbow, then your hand.
2. Reach your other arm over their shoulder.
3. Bump your hips up and into them to roll them over.
4. If they post their hand, switch to the kimura.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom closed guard
- They're kneeling upright in your guard.

### 1. Sit up onto your elbow, then your hand
- Open your guard and sit up, posting first on your elbow, then on your hand.

### 2. Reach your other arm over their shoulder
- Reach your free arm high over their shoulder on the same side as your post.

### 3. Bump your hips up and into them to roll them over
- Drive your hips up into their chest and roll them backward over the side of your posted hand. Land in mount.

### 4. If they post their hand, switch to the kimura
- If they block the sweep by posting a hand, grab that wrist, figure-four and finish the kimura.

### Common mistakes
- **Sitting up halfway:** you have to come up high for the bump to work.

## Defense

Post your free hand wide and keep your posture, then pull the arm free if they switch to the kimura.

1. Keep your posture as they sit up.
2. Post wide when they bump.
3. Grab your belt if they lock the kimura.
