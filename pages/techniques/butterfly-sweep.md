Underhook and sleeve control, fall to one side and elevate with a butterfly hook.

## Key steps

1. Underhook on one side, sleeve or overhook on the other.
2. Pull them forward so their weight is on you.
3. Fall to the overhook side.
4. Elevate with the hook on the underhook side and follow them over.

## Defense

Base out wide and post your free hand on the side they fall to.

1. Widen your knees.
2. Post your hand on the overhook side.
3. Kill the hook by sprawling back.
