Underhook and sleeve control, fall to one side and elevate with a butterfly hook.

## Key steps

1. Underhook on one side, sleeve or overhook on the other.
2. Pull them forward so their weight is on you.
3. Fall to the overhook side.
4. Elevate with the hook on the underhook side and follow them over.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom butterfly guard
- You're sitting up with both butterfly hooks inside their thighs.

### 1. Underhook on one side, sleeve or overhook on the other
- Get an underhook on one side, and grip their sleeve or overhook their arm on the other side.

### 2. Pull them forward so their weight is on you
- Pull them forward with both arms so their weight comes onto your chest and hooks.

### 3. Fall to the overhook side
- Fall onto your side toward the overhook or sleeve side, taking them with you.

### 4. Elevate with the hook on the underhook side and follow them over
- Lift with your hook on the underhook side so they tip over, then follow them over and come up on top.

### Common mistakes
- **Lifting while their weight is back:** pull them onto you first.
- **Falling to the underhook side:** you fall under them.

## Defense

Base out wide and post your free hand on the side they fall to.

1. Widen your knees.
2. Post your hand on the overhook side.
3. Kill the hook by sprawling back.
