Invert under the opponent's legs from reverse De La Riva to take the back.

## Key steps

1. Reverse DLR hook with a grip on the far leg.
2. Invert, bringing your head toward their far heel.
3. Slide between their legs to come out behind them.
4. Seatbelt and insert hooks.
