Invert under the opponent's legs from reverse De La Riva to take the back.

## Key steps

1. Reverse DLR hook with a grip on the far leg.
2. Invert, bringing your head toward their far heel.
3. Slide between their legs to come out behind them.
4. Seatbelt and insert hooks.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom reverse De La Riva
- Your leg hooks the inside of their lead leg (reverse DLR), and you grip their far leg.

### 1. Reverse DLR hook with a grip on the far leg
- Keep the reverse DLR hook deep and grip their far leg at the ankle or pants.

### 2. Invert, bringing your head toward their far heel
- Roll onto your shoulders and bring your head toward their far heel, going between their legs.

### 3. Slide between their legs to come out behind them
- Slide your body through between their legs so you come out behind them.

### 4. Seatbelt and insert hooks
- Come up behind them, seatbelt, and insert hooks.

### Common mistakes
- **Inverting with them standing tall and balanced:** off-balance them first.

## Defense

Keep your hips back and your base wide, and kill their reverse De La Riva hook before they can invert.

1. Keep your hips back and your weight down.
2. Push their reverse DLR hook off your leg.
3. Close your knees so they can't slide between your legs.
4. Turn to face them as they come up.
