Extend your X hooks to off-balance the opponent, then stand up in base as they fall.

## Key steps

1. Hug the near leg and extend the X hooks.
2. Push their weight backward.
3. Technical stand-up as they fall.
4. Land in a passing position.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom X guard
- You're under them, hugging one leg, with your X hooks on the other: one foot behind their knee, the other on their hip.

### 1. Hug the near leg and extend the X hooks
- Hug their near leg tight and straighten your legs, pushing their hooked leg away.

### 2. Push their weight backward
- As their leg goes out, push their weight back over the heel of the leg you're hugging, so they fall back or sit.

### 3. Technical stand-up as they fall
- Post a hand, swing your leg under you and stand up in base as they hit the mat.

### 4. Land in a passing position
- Keep hold of the leg and land standing at their legs, ready to pass.

### Common mistakes
- **Extending without hugging the leg:** they step back out.
- **Staying on your back after the sweep:** they recover guard. Stand up.

## Defense

Keep your weight forward and free your leg from the X hooks before they can extend.

1. Keep your weight forward over them.
2. Turn your knee out and free the hooked leg.
3. Step back out of the hooks.
4. If they extend, sit to the side rather than falling straight back.
