Get up from the mat safely, keeping a frame and base the whole time.

## Key steps

1. Post one hand behind you and plant the opposite foot.
2. Frame forward with your free hand or foot.
3. Lift your hips and swing the posted leg back through.
4. Stand in a stance, facing them.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: on the mat
- You're sitting or on your back and want to stand up safely, with them in front of you.

### 1. Post one hand behind you and plant the opposite foot
- Post a hand on the mat behind your hip and plant the opposite foot flat near your butt.

### 2. Frame forward with your free hand or foot
- Keep your free hand (or other foot) up in front of you to keep them at a distance.

### 3. Lift your hips and swing the posted leg back through
- Push off the posted hand and foot to lift your hips, and swing your other leg back underneath you and behind.

### 4. Stand in a stance, facing them
- Stand with your feet in a fighting stance, hands up, still facing them.

### Common mistakes
- **Standing straight up face first:** you get taken down or hit.
- **Leaving your head forward:** keep it back and your frame up as you rise.

## Defense

Close the distance and push them back down as they rise.

1. Step in as they post.
2. Push their head or shoulder back.
3. Pass as they fall back.
