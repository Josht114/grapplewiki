Guillotine from bottom half guard when the opponent's head comes low.

## Key steps

1. Wrap their head as it drops.
2. Lock your grip under their chin.
3. Hip out away from the trapped leg.
4. Arch your hips and pull.

## Defense

Keep your head up and away from their armpit.

1. Don't dive your head down.
2. Clear the arm.
3. Pass to the side of your head.
