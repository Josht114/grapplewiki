Guillotine from bottom half guard when the opponent's head comes low.

## Key steps

1. Wrap their head as it drops.
2. Lock your grip under their chin.
3. Hip out away from the trapped leg.
4. Arch your hips and pull.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom half guard
- They're on top and their head drops in front of you as they try to pass or crossface.

### 1. Wrap their head as it drops
- Wrap your arm around their neck from the front as it comes down near your chest.

### 2. Lock your grip under their chin
- Slide the blade of your forearm under their chin and grip your wrist, or your biceps for an arm-in version.

### 3. Hip out away from the trapped leg
- Shrimp your hips out to the side, away from the leg you've trapped, so you're at an angle to their neck.

### 4. Arch your hips and pull
- Pull your elbow to your ribs and arch your hips into them; they can't follow because their leg is trapped.

### Common mistakes
- **Staying flat underneath:** angle out or the guillotine has no pull.
- **Forearm across the face:** get it under the chin.

## Defense

Keep your head up and away from their armpit.

1. Don't dive your head down.
2. Clear the arm.
3. Pass to the side of your head.
