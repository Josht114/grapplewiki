Kill or lift one butterfly hook and pass to the side.

## Key steps

1. Flatten them with head or shoulder control.
2. Lift or pin one hook.
3. Swing your leg free over the dead hook.
4. Drop into side control.

## Defense

Keep your hooks active and sit up into them.

1. Re-insert your hook as soon as it's lifted.
2. Sit up and get an underhook.
3. Switch to half guard if a hook is lost.
