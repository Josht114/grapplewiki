Kimura from closed guard when the opponent posts a hand on the mat.

## Key steps

1. Grip their wrist as they post.
2. Sit up and thread your arm over their shoulder.
3. Lock your own wrist.
4. Fall back, hip out, and rotate the arm behind them.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom closed guard
- They post a hand on the mat or on your body.

### 1. Grip their wrist as they post
- Grab the wrist of the posted hand with your hand on the same side.

### 2. Sit up and thread your arm over their shoulder
- Open your guard, sit up, and reach your other arm over their shoulder and under their arm.

### 3. Lock your own wrist
- Grab your own wrist: the figure-four kimura grip.

### 4. Fall back, hip out, and rotate the arm behind them
- Fall back, hip out to the side of the trapped arm, re-close or keep your legs over their back, and rotate their arm up behind them.

### Common mistakes
- **Not sitting up:** you can't reach over their shoulder.
- **Not hipping out:** the arm stays tight to their body.

## Defense

Don't post your hands on the mat; grab your belt if they lock the grip.

1. Keep your hands on their body.
2. Grab your belt or pants.
3. Drive into them and posture up.
