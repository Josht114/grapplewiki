Kimura from closed guard when the opponent posts a hand on the mat.

## Key steps

1. Grip their wrist as they post.
2. Sit up and thread your arm over their shoulder.
3. Lock your own wrist.
4. Fall back, hip out, and rotate the arm behind them.

## Defense

Don't post your hands on the mat; grab your belt if they lock the grip.

1. Keep your hands on their body.
2. Grab your belt or pants.
3. Drive into them and posture up.
