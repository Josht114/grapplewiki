Shoulder lock using your legs to rotate the opponent's arm behind their back.

## Key steps

1. Control their wrist and angle off to that side.
2. Swing your leg over their shoulder from the outside.
3. Sit up and turn so you face the same way they do.
4. Control their hips and lean forward to finish.

## Defense

Roll forward before the lock is set, or grab the belt and posture up.

1. Keep your posture and elbows tight.
2. If the leg comes over, grab their belt or pants.
3. Roll forward over your trapped shoulder.
4. Come up facing them.

## Notes

Often set up from [[Bottom rubber guard]] and [[Bottom spider guard]]. If they roll, you sweep.
