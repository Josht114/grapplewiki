Shoulder lock using your legs to rotate the opponent's arm behind their back.

## Key steps

1. Control their wrist and angle off to that side.
2. Swing your leg over their shoulder from the outside.
3. Sit up and turn so you face the same way they do.
4. Control their hips and lean forward to finish.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom closed guard
- They're in your closed guard; you have a grip on one wrist or sleeve.

### 1. Control their wrist and angle off to that side
- Hold the wrist and open your guard, swinging your hips out to the same side as that arm.

### 2. Swing your leg over their shoulder from the outside
- Bring the leg on that side over their shoulder from the outside and hook it behind their armpit, trapping the arm between your legs.

### 3. Sit up and turn so you face the same way they do
- Pull your other leg free, sit up and turn your body so you're beside them, facing the way they face, their arm locked by your legs.

### 4. Control their hips and lean forward to finish
- Wrap their far hip with your arm so they can't forward-roll out, then lean forward and slightly away. The shoulder rotates and they tap.

### Common mistakes
- **Not controlling the hips:** they roll forward and escape.
- **Staying on your back:** sit up and face their way or there's no pressure.

## Defense

Roll forward before the lock is set, or grab the belt and posture up.

1. Keep your posture and elbows tight.
2. If the leg comes over, grab their belt or pants.
3. Roll forward over your trapped shoulder.
4. Come up facing them.
