Drag the opponent's arm across from closed guard and climb to their back.

## Key steps

1. Grab their wrist and triceps.
2. Drag the arm across your body.
3. Open your guard and hip out.
4. Climb to their back and insert hooks.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom closed guard
- They're in your closed guard with an arm reaching to you or posting.

### 1. Grab their wrist and triceps
- Grip one of their wrists, and with your other hand cup the back of the same arm above the elbow.

### 2. Drag the arm across your body
- Pull the arm across your body toward your other hip, so their shoulder comes forward and their side opens up.

### 3. Open your guard and hip out
- Open your legs and shift your hips out to the side the arm went, sitting up onto your elbow.

### 4. Climb to their back and insert hooks
- Reach your arm over their back, pull yourself up behind them, and put your hooks in, finishing with a seatbelt.

### Common mistakes
- **Dragging the forearm instead of the triceps:** they pull it back.
- **Staying flat on your back:** sit up and climb, or you can't reach their back.

## Defense

Pull your elbow back and turn to face them.

1. Keep your elbows tight.
2. Pull the arm back.
3. Square up.
