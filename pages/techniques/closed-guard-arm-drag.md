Drag the opponent's arm across from closed guard and climb to their back.

## Key steps

1. Grab their wrist and triceps.
2. Drag the arm across your body.
3. Open your guard and hip out.
4. Climb to their back and insert hooks.

## Defense

Pull your elbow back and turn to face them.

1. Keep your elbows tight.
2. Pull the arm back.
3. Square up.
