When the opponent turns away from the knee, follow them and take the back.

## Key steps

1. Feel them turn away from the pressure.
2. Slide your knee behind their back as they turn.
3. Get the seatbelt grip.
4. Insert hooks and settle.

## Defense

Turn into the knee to escape, never away from it.

1. Push the knee down toward your hips.
2. Turn your chest toward them.
3. Recover guard instead of turning your back.
