Major outer reap: break balance to the rear corner and reap the supporting leg from the outside.

## Key steps

1. Grip sleeve and collar.
2. Step beside their leg, chest to chest.
3. Drive their weight onto the near heel.
4. Reap the leg back while pushing the upper body down.

## Defense

Lower your hips and step around the reaping leg.

1. Drop your hips.
2. Step your leg around theirs.
3. Push their upper body away.
