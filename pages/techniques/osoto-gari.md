Major outer reap: break balance to the rear corner and reap the supporting leg from the outside.

## Key steps

1. Grip sleeve and collar.
2. Step beside their leg, chest to chest.
3. Drive their weight onto the near heel.
4. Reap the leg back while pushing the upper body down.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: standing
- Sleeve and collar grips (gi) or wrist and collar tie.

### 1. Grip sleeve and collar
- Sleeve grip to control their arm, collar grip to control their head and shoulder.

### 2. Step beside their leg, chest to chest
- Step your supporting foot beside their foot, close, chest against theirs.

### 3. Drive their weight onto the near heel
- Push and pull with your grips so their weight goes back onto the heel of the leg you'll reap.

### 4. Reap the leg back while pushing the upper body down
- Swing your free leg through and reap their leg back from behind, calf to calf, while you push their upper body down and back.

### Common mistakes
- **Reaping before their weight is on the heel:** they just lift the leg.
- **Leaning away:** stay chest to chest.

## Defense

Lower your hips and step around the reaping leg.

1. Drop your hips.
2. Step your leg around theirs.
3. Push their upper body away.
