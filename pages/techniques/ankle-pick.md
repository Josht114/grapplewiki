Pull the opponent forward onto one leg, then pick that ankle while pushing the head back.

## Key steps

1. Collar tie and pull them forward so they step.
2. Level change and grab the heel of the lead foot.
3. Drive the head back and down.
4. Pull the heel and land in a passing position.

## Defense

Keep weight off your lead foot and sprawl your hips back.

1. Don't step forward when they pull you.
2. Pull the picked foot back.
3. Keep your head up and hips back.
