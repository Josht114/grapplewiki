Pull the opponent forward onto one leg, then pick that ankle while pushing the head back.

## Key steps

1. Collar tie and pull them forward so they step.
2. Level change and grab the heel of the lead foot.
3. Drive the head back and down.
4. Pull the heel and land in a passing position.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: standing
- You're both standing; you have a collar tie (hand behind their neck).

### 1. Collar tie and pull them forward so they step
- Pull their head down and toward you so they step forward to catch their balance. The lead foot that steps is your target.

### 2. Level change and grab the heel of the lead foot
- Drop your hips (bend your knees, not your back) and reach down to cup the heel of the foot they just stepped with.

### 3. Drive the head back and down
- At the same moment, push their head back up and away with the collar-tie hand, so their weight goes onto that heel.

### 4. Pull the heel and land in a passing position
- Pull the heel up toward you; with their head pushed back and no foot to step on, they sit down.
- Stay between their legs or step to the side, ready to pass.

### Common mistakes
- **Reaching down by bending at the waist:** your head drops and they sprawl on you. Level change with your legs.
- **Pulling the heel without driving the head:** they just hop.

## Defense

Keep weight off your lead foot and sprawl your hips back.

1. Don't step forward when they pull you.
2. Pull the picked foot back.
3. Keep your head up and hips back.
