Hook behind one ankle, push the other hip, and topple the standing opponent backward.

## Key steps

1. Grip one ankle and hook behind it with your foot.
2. Push the opposite hip with your other foot.
3. Pull the ankle as you push.
4. Come up as they fall.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom open guard
- They're standing in front of you.

### 1. Grip one ankle and hook behind it with your foot
- Grab one ankle with your hand and hook the back of that heel with the foot on the same side.

### 2. Push the opposite hip with your other foot
- Put your other foot on their opposite hip.

### 3. Pull the ankle as you push
- Pull the ankle and heel toward you while you push the hip away, so they fall backward.

### 4. Come up as they fall
- Sit up and come up to your knees or feet as they hit the mat, ready to pass.

### Common mistakes
- **Pushing without pulling the ankle:** they just step back.

## Defense

Step the hooked foot free and keep your weight forward.

1. Step out of the ankle grip.
2. Lean your weight forward.
3. Push their foot off your hip.
