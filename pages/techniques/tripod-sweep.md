Hook behind one ankle, push the other hip, and topple the standing opponent backward.

## Key steps

1. Grip one ankle and hook behind it with your foot.
2. Push the opposite hip with your other foot.
3. Pull the ankle as you push.
4. Come up as they fall.

## Defense

Step the hooked foot free and keep your weight forward.

1. Step out of the ankle grip.
2. Lean your weight forward.
3. Push their foot off your hip.
