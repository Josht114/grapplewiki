Slide your trapped knee across the opponent's thigh to free it and pass to side control.

## Key steps

1. Get the crossface and underhook.
2. Free your knee slightly so it points across their thigh.
3. Slice your knee across toward the mat.
4. Pull your trailing foot free and settle in side control.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: top half guard
- Your leg is trapped in their half guard.

### 1. Get the crossface and underhook
- Crossface their head and underhook their far arm, so they're flat and can't come up on you.

### 2. Free your knee slightly so it points across their thigh
- Wiggle your trapped knee forward so it's pointing across the top of their thigh.

### 3. Slice your knee across toward the mat
- Drive your knee across their thigh, down to the mat on the other side, staying up on the toes of your back foot.

### 4. Pull your trailing foot free and settle in side control
- Pull your back foot out of their legs and settle chest to chest.

### Common mistakes
- **No upper-body control:** they turn in and recover.

## Defense

Win the underhook and come up to your side, or switch to a knee shield.

1. Keep your knee shield or underhook.
2. Turn into them as the knee slides.
3. Recover half guard or full guard.
