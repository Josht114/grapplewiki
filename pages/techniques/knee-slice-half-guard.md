Slide your trapped knee across the opponent's thigh to free it and pass to side control.

## Key steps

1. Get the crossface and underhook.
2. Free your knee slightly so it points across their thigh.
3. Slice your knee across toward the mat.
4. Pull your trailing foot free and settle in side control.

## Defense

Win the underhook and come up to your side, or switch to a knee shield.

1. Keep your knee shield or underhook.
2. Turn into them as the knee slides.
3. Recover half guard or full guard.
