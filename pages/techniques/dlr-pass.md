Passing the De La Riva guard by killing the hook first.

## Key steps

1. Grip their DLR foot or ankle and push it off your leg.
2. Drop your knee to the mat inside their hook to kill it.
3. Pin their far leg with your hand.
4. Step around and drop into side control.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: top of De La Riva guard
- Their outside leg is hooked around your lead leg, they hold your ankle and your sleeve or collar.

### 1. Grip their DLR foot or ankle and push it off your leg
- Grip the hooking foot or ankle and push it away from your leg, loosening the hook.

### 2. Drop your knee to the mat inside their hook to kill it
- Drop your lead knee to the mat inside their hooking leg, so the hook has nothing to wrap around.

### 3. Pin their far leg with your hand
- Push their other leg down to the mat with your hand on the knee or pants.

### 4. Step around and drop into side control
- Step your free leg around their legs and drop your chest into side control.

### Common mistakes
- **Standing tall with the hook in:** they off-balance you and berimbolo or sweep.

## Defense

Keep the De La Riva hook connected and your grip on their ankle so they can't kill the hook and step around.

1. Keep the DLR hook deep, with your heel pulling on their leg.
2. Keep your ankle grip on their lead leg.
3. Put your other foot on their hip to hold distance.
4. Re-hook as they step.
