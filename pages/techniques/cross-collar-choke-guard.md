Gi choke from closed guard using two deep cross collar grips.

## Key steps

1. Break their posture with your legs.
2. Feed one hand deep into their collar.
3. Get the second grip on the other collar.
4. Pull your elbows out and down to finish.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom closed guard
- They're in your closed guard in a gi.

### 1. Break their posture with your legs
- Pull your knees to your chest so they fall forward; their collar comes within reach.

### 2. Feed one hand deep into their collar
- Open the collar and feed one hand deep, fingers inside, thumb out, as far round the back of their neck as you can.

### 3. Get the second grip on the other collar
- Your other hand crosses over and grips the opposite collar, thumb inside, so your wrists cross.

### 4. Pull your elbows out and down to finish
- Pull your elbows out to the sides and down toward the mat, pulling them in with your legs; your forearms close the neck.

### Common mistakes
- **Shallow first grip:** nothing closes. Go deep.
- **Letting them posture up between grips:** keep them broken down with your legs.

## Defense

Keep your posture and strip the first grip before the second arrives.

1. Posture up.
2. Strip the first collar grip.
3. Tuck your chin.
