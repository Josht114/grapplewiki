Elbow-knee escape: make space, shrimp and pull a knee through to recover half guard.

## Key steps

1. Frame your forearm on their hip.
2. Bridge to make space.
3. Shrimp away and slide your elbow to your knee.
4. Pull your knee through and trap their leg in half guard.

## Defense

Stay low and heavy, and block their knee from sliding through.

1. Grapevine their legs to stop the shrimp.
2. Keep your knees tight to their hips.
3. Follow their hips and pull the knee back out before half guard locks.
