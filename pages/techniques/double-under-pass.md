Both arms under the legs to stack the opponent, then pass around one side.

## Key steps

1. Scoop both arms under their thighs.
2. Lock your hands or grip the belt/collar.
3. Stack their hips up over their shoulders.
4. Walk around one side and drop into side control.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: in their open guard
- You're on your knees between their legs, their feet on your hips or legs around you.

### 1. Scoop both arms under their thighs
- Drop your hips and slide both arms under their thighs, hands near their hips.

### 2. Lock your hands or grip the belt/collar
- Lock your hands behind their back, or grip their belt or collar, so they're trapped in your arms.

### 3. Stack their hips up over their shoulders
- Drive forward and up so their hips go over their shoulders and their weight is on their neck.

### 4. Walk around one side and drop into side control
- Keep them stacked and walk your knees around one side, then drop into side control.

### Common mistakes
- **Not stacking enough:** they triangle you. Get their hips high.

## Defense

Stopping pressure passes by keeping your knees between you and the opponent and refusing to be flattened.

1. Stay on your side, never flat.
2. Frame on the head and shoulders.
3. Keep your knees connected to your elbows.
4. Pummel your legs back inside when they clear one.
