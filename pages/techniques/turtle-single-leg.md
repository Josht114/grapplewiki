Grab the opponent's leg from turtle and drive through to reverse the position.

## Key steps

1. Reach and grab their near leg.
2. Pull it to your chest.
3. Come up to your knees.
4. Drive through and come up on top.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom turtle
- You're turtled and their leg is near your head or arm.

### 1. Reach and grab their near leg
- Reach for their near leg at the knee or ankle.

### 2. Pull it to your chest
- Hug it tight to your chest, head on the inside.

### 3. Come up to your knees
- Come up onto your knees, holding the leg.

### 4. Drive through and come up on top
- Drive into them with your shoulder and finish the single leg, coming up on top.

### Common mistakes
- **Reaching with your head down:** they front headlock you.

## Defense

Keep your legs back and away from their hands.

1. Sprawl the leg back.
2. Crossface.
3. Stay on their back.
