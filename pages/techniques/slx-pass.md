Freeing the trapped leg from single leg X and passing.

## Key steps

1. Turn your trapped knee out and away from them.
2. Push their foot off your hip.
3. Pull your trapped leg free and step back.
4. Step around their legs and drop into side control.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: top of single leg X
- They're on their back under you, hugging one of your legs, with one foot on your hip and the other leg behind your knee.

### 1. Turn your trapped knee out and away from them
- Point your trapped knee outward, away from their body. This protects your heel and knee from leg locks and weakens their hooks.

### 2. Push their foot off your hip
- Push the foot that's on your hip down and off, with your hand on their ankle or shin.

### 3. Pull your trapped leg free and step back
- Pull your leg out from between theirs and step back, keeping your weight on the leg you've freed their foot from.

### 4. Step around their legs and drop into side control
- Pin their legs with your hands, step around to the side, and drop chest to chest.

### Common mistakes
- **Knee pointing toward them:** your heel is exposed for a heel hook.
- **Standing upright with your weight back:** they extend the hooks and sweep you.

## Defense

Keep your single leg X hooks tight and keep extending to keep them off balance, so they never get set to free the leg.

1. Keep your foot on their hip and your other leg hooked behind their knee.
2. Hug their trapped leg tight to your chest.
3. Extend your legs to off-balance them when they try to step out.
4. Switch to an ankle lock or 50/50 if they pull the leg free.
