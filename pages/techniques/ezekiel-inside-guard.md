Sleeve choke you can apply from inside the opponent's closed guard when they pull your head down.

## Key steps

1. Feed one arm behind their neck and grab inside your own sleeve.
2. Bring the free hand across their throat.
3. Drop your weight and drive the blade of the hand down.

## Defense

Don't pull them into you with your head exposed; block the free hand.

1. Tuck your chin.
2. Block the hand crossing your throat.
3. Push their elbow away and re-posture.
