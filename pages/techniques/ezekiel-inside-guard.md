Sleeve choke you can apply from inside the opponent's closed guard when they pull your head down.

## Key steps

1. Feed one arm behind their neck and grab inside your own sleeve.
2. Bring the free hand across their throat.
3. Drop your weight and drive the blade of the hand down.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: inside their closed guard
- You're in a gi, they've pulled you down or you're close.

### 1. Feed one arm behind their neck and grab inside your own sleeve
- Slide one arm behind their neck and grab inside the sleeve of your other arm.

### 2. Bring the free hand across their throat
- The other hand goes across their throat, blade first.

### 3. Drop your weight and drive the blade of the hand down
- Drop your chest and push the blade of your hand down while the sleeve grip pulls.

### Common mistakes
- **Doing it from a weak, flattened position:** they can sweep or armbar. Keep your base wide.

## Defense

Don't pull them into you with your head exposed; block the free hand.

1. Tuck your chin.
2. Block the hand crossing your throat.
3. Push their elbow away and re-posture.
