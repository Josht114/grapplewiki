Guillotine on the butterfly guard player when they sit in with their head low.

## Key steps

1. Wrap their head as it drops.
2. Lock your grip.
3. Sprawl your hips back.
4. Lift your elbow and arch.

## Defense

Keep your head up and higher than theirs when you sit up.

1. Head up.
2. Clear the choking arm.
3. Back off and re-sit.
