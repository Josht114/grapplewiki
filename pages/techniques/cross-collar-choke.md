Gi choke using both collar grips, crossing the wrists to close the sides of the neck.

## Key steps

1. Feed the first hand deep into the collar, fingers in.
2. Place the second grip on the opposite collar.
3. Drop your elbows toward the mat and pull them apart as you bring your head down.

## Defense

Stop the second grip, then use the opponent's forward posture to escape.

1. Block the second hand before it reaches your collar.
2. Turn your chin toward the first grip.
3. Push their elbow across your face.
4. Use their forward weight to bridge and roll.
