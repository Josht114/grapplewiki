Gi choke using both collar grips, crossing the wrists to close the sides of the neck.

## Key steps

1. Feed the first hand deep into the collar, fingers in.
2. Place the second grip on the opposite collar.
3. Drop your elbows toward the mat and pull them apart as you bring your head down.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: mount
- You're mounted on someone in a gi, hips low and posture solid.

### 1. Feed the first hand deep into the collar, fingers in
- Open the collar with your other hand and slide your first hand in deep, fingers inside, thumb out, as close to the back of the neck as you can.

### 2. Place the second grip on the opposite collar
- Your second hand crosses over the first and grips the other collar, thumb inside or fingers in. Your wrists form an X.

### 3. Drop your elbows toward the mat and pull them apart as you bring your head down
- Drop your elbows wide, pulling them apart, and bring your head down toward the mat beside theirs.
- Your forearms close the sides of the neck like scissors.

### Common mistakes
- **Shallow first grip:** the choke never closes. Get it deep before the second hand.
- **Pulling up instead of down:** drop the elbows and the head.

## Defense

Stop the second grip, then use the opponent's forward posture to escape.

1. Block the second hand before it reaches your collar.
2. Turn your chin toward the first grip.
3. Push their elbow across your face.
4. Use their forward weight to bridge and roll.
