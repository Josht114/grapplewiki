Leg choke that traps the opponent's head and one arm inside a figure-four of your legs.

## Key steps

1. Control one arm and push it to their belly while pulling the other arm.
2. Shoot your leg over their shoulder and across the back of the neck.
3. Angle off and lock the figure-four behind your knee.
4. Pull the head down, squeeze the knees and lift the hips.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom closed guard
- They're in your closed guard; you have control of their arms.

### 1. Control one arm and push it to their belly while pulling the other arm
- Push one arm in to their belly (or between your legs) and pull the other arm out wide. One arm in, one out.

### 2. Shoot your leg over their shoulder and across the back of the neck
- Open your guard and kick the leg on the side of the arm that's out up over their shoulder, behind their neck.

### 3. Angle off and lock the figure-four behind your knee
- Swing your hips out to an angle and hook that foot behind the knee of your other leg.

### 4. Pull the head down, squeeze the knees and lift the hips
- Pull their head down, squeeze your knees together and lift your hips; the trapped arm and your thigh close the neck.

### Common mistakes
- **Square to them:** angle off or there's no squeeze.
- **Leg across the back instead of the neck:** pull their head down so the leg sits on the neck.

## Defense

Keep posture, keep both arms on the same side, and stack the opponent before the figure-four locks.

1. Never leave one arm in and one arm out.
2. Posture up straight and drive your trapped arm across.
3. Stack them by stepping up and driving forward.
4. Pass toward the side of the trapped arm.
