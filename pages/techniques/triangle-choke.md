Leg choke that traps the opponent's head and one arm inside a figure-four of your legs.

## Key steps

1. Control one arm and push it to their belly while pulling the other arm.
2. Shoot your leg over their shoulder and across the back of the neck.
3. Angle off and lock the figure-four behind your knee.
4. Pull the head down, squeeze the knees and lift the hips.

## Defense

Keep posture, keep both arms on the same side, and stack the opponent before the figure-four locks.

1. Never leave one arm in and one arm out.
2. Posture up straight and drive your trapped arm across.
3. Stack them by stepping up and driving forward.
4. Pass toward the side of the trapped arm.

## Notes

Also available from [[Bottom spider guard]] and [[Bottom rubber guard]].
