Arm-in choke from the front headlock, finished by rolling under the opponent.

## Key steps

1. Shoot your arm under their neck and through their far armpit.
2. Lock the arm-in grip on your biceps.
3. Roll toward the trapped arm, under them.
4. Walk your feet toward their hips and squeeze.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: the front headlock
- They're on their knees in front of you, your chest on their head and shoulders, hips sprawled back.
- Their head is under one of your armpits; the arm next to it (on the middle of your body) is the arm you'll trap.

### 1. Shoot your arm under their neck and through their far armpit
- Your choking arm goes under their chin first, then out through the armpit of the trapped arm: the opposite path to the darce.
- Thread deep so your biceps, not your wrist, ends up against the side of their neck.

### 2. Lock the arm-in grip on your biceps
- Grab your other biceps with the choking hand, and put the other hand on their back or behind your head.
- Their head and one arm are now inside the loop, like a figure-four around their neck and shoulder.

### 3. Roll toward the trapped arm, under them
- Drop your shoulder on the side of the trapped arm and roll underneath them, taking them with you.
- Roll toward the trapped arm, never away from it; going the wrong way lets the arm slip out.
- You land on your back or side with them on their back across you.

### 4. Walk your feet toward their hips and squeeze
- After the roll, walk your feet toward their hips so your body curls around them.
- Squeeze your elbows together and pull your shoulder into the trapped arm.
- Some finish by stepping a leg over their body to stop them turning in.

### Common mistakes
- **Rolling too early:** lock the grip and pin the arm first, or the roll just loosens everything.
- **Shallow arm:** a wrist across the throat is a crank, not a choke.
- **Rolling the wrong way:** always toward the trapped arm.

## Defense

Get your trapped arm and head free before the grip locks, and never roll for them.

1. Pull your trapped arm out and down.
2. Turn your head away from the choking arm.
3. Square your hips and drive into them.
4. Don't roll: that tightens the anaconda.
