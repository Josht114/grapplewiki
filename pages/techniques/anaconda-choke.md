Arm-in choke from the front headlock, finished by rolling under the opponent.

## Key steps

1. Shoot your arm under their neck and through their far armpit.
2. Lock the arm-in grip on your biceps.
3. Roll toward the trapped arm, under them.
4. Walk your feet toward their hips and squeeze.

## Defense

Get your trapped arm and head free before the grip locks, and never roll for them.

1. Pull your trapped arm out and down.
2. Turn your head away from the choking arm.
3. Square your hips and drive into them.
4. Don't roll: that tightens the anaconda.
