Figure-four foot lock that twists the foot inward against the ankle.

## Key steps

1. Grip the outside of the foot with your same-side hand.
2. Thread the other arm behind the ankle and grab your wrist.
3. Pull the foot toward their hip and rotate inward.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: 50/50
- Your legs are entangled in 50/50, their foot near your chest.

### 1. Grip the outside of the foot with your same-side hand
- Grab the outside edge of their foot, around the little toes, with the hand on the same side.

### 2. Thread the other arm behind the ankle and grab your wrist
- Slide your other arm behind their ankle and grab your own wrist: a figure-four on the foot.

### 3. Pull the foot toward their hip and rotate inward
- Bend their foot in toward their own hip and rotate it inward, slowly, so the ankle twists.

### Common mistakes
- **Rotating fast:** the ankle tears before it hurts. Go slowly and let them tap.
- **Grip on the toes only:** it slips. Grip the whole outside edge of the foot.

## Defense

Point the toes and turn with the twist, then grab their grip.

1. Point your toes and keep the foot straight.
2. Rotate with the twist, not against it.
3. Grab their wrist to break the figure-four.
4. Pull the foot out.
