Pendulum sweep: control an arm, scoop a leg and swing your leg to roll the opponent over.

## Key steps

1. Control their arm on one side.
2. Hook under their opposite leg.
3. Swing your free leg up and over like a pendulum.
4. Use the momentum to roll them and come up on top.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom closed guard
- They're in your closed guard.

### 1. Control their arm on one side
- Grip their sleeve or wrist on one side and pull it across, so they can't post on that side. That's the side they'll fall to.

### 2. Hook under their opposite leg
- With your free arm, reach under their leg on the other side, hooking behind the knee.

### 3. Swing your free leg up and over like a pendulum
- Open your guard, then swing the leg on the arm side down and up hard, like a pendulum.

### 4. Use the momentum to roll them and come up on top
- At the same time, lift the hooked leg; they roll over toward the trapped arm, and you follow them into mount.

### Common mistakes
- **Not taking the arm:** they post on it and stop the sweep.
- **A slow, small swing:** use a big pendulum to build the momentum.

## Defense

Post your free hand and keep your weight back.

1. Free the trapped arm.
2. Post wide with your free hand.
3. Keep your hips back.
