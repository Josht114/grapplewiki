Pendulum sweep: control an arm, scoop a leg and swing your leg to roll the opponent over.

## Key steps

1. Control their arm on one side.
2. Hook under their opposite leg.
3. Swing your free leg up and over like a pendulum.
4. Use the momentum to roll them and come up on top.

## Defense

Post your free hand and keep your weight back.

1. Free the trapped arm.
2. Post wide with your free hand.
3. Keep your hips back.
