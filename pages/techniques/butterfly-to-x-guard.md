Elevate the opponent with a butterfly hook, slide underneath and set X hooks.

## Key steps

1. Lift them with a butterfly hook.
2. Slide under their hips.
3. Hug one leg.
4. Set your X hooks.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom butterfly guard
- You're sitting up with butterfly hooks and they're upright or standing.

### 1. Lift them with a butterfly hook
- Use a hook to lift one of their legs, making them light on that side.

### 2. Slide under their hips
- Slide your body underneath them, your back to the mat, head under their hips.

### 3. Hug one leg
- Wrap one of their legs with your arms.

### 4. Set your X hooks
- Put one foot behind their knee and the other on their hip, crossing your legs in an X. You're now in X guard.

### Common mistakes
- **Staying too far away:** get right under their hips or the X hooks won't reach.

## Defense

Keep your hips heavy and step out of the hooks.

1. Sit your hips back.
2. Kill the hook.
3. Step out and pass.
