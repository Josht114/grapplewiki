Pull the opponent's arm across your body to take the angle and get behind them.

## Key steps

1. Grab their wrist with your same-side hand.
2. Reach the other hand to the back of their triceps.
3. Pull the arm across while stepping to the outside.
4. Circle behind to the [[Top back take (standing)]].

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: standing
- You're facing them; they reach for you or have a grip.

### 1. Grab their wrist with your same-side hand
- Your right hand grabs their right wrist (or left to left), from the outside.

### 2. Reach the other hand to the back of their triceps
- Your other hand reaches across and cups the back of their upper arm, just above the elbow.

### 3. Pull the arm across while stepping to the outside
- Pull the arm across your body, like opening a door, and step your foot to the outside of theirs at the same time.
- Their arm goes past you and their side opens up.

### 4. Circle behind to the [[Top back take (standing)]]
- Keep moving round to their back, hug their waist and lock your hands.

### Common mistakes
- **Pulling without stepping:** you move them a little and end up still in front. Move yourself too.
- **Grabbing the forearm instead of the triceps:** they pull the arm back out.

## Defense

Pull your elbow back to your ribs and turn to face them.

1. Keep your elbows tight.
2. Pull the arm back as they drag.
3. Turn to face them.
