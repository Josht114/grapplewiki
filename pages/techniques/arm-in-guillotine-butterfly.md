Guillotine with their arm trapped inside, finished from butterfly guard.

## Key steps

1. Wrap their head, trapping their near arm inside your arm.
2. Lock your hands (gable grip).
3. Hip out to the side of the trapped arm.
4. Squeeze and pull up with your elbow.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom butterfly guard
- You're sitting up with butterfly hooks in, they lean on you with their head low.

### 1. Wrap their head, trapping their near arm inside your arm
- Wrap your arm around their head and one of their arms together, with their arm inside your loop.

### 2. Lock your hands (gable grip)
- Lock your hands palm to palm under their chin, your forearm blade on the side of their neck.

### 3. Hip out to the side of the trapped arm
- Shrimp your hips out toward the side of the trapped arm, so you're at an angle.

### 4. Squeeze and pull up with your elbow
- Squeeze your elbows together and pull your choking elbow up and back, using your hooks to keep them close.

### Common mistakes
- **Staying square:** the arm-in guillotine needs the angle.
- **Lying flat:** stay on your side, high on your elbow.

## Defense

Keep your posture and pass toward the side of the trapped arm.

1. Keep your head up when they sit up.
2. Clear the choking arm.
3. Pass to the side your head is on.
