Guillotine with their arm trapped inside, finished from butterfly guard.

## Key steps

1. Wrap their head with their near arm inside.
2. Lock your hands (gable grip).
3. Hip out to the side of the trapped arm.
4. Squeeze and pull up with your elbow.

## Defense

Keep your posture and pass toward the side of the trapped arm.

1. Keep your head up when they sit up.
2. Clear the choking arm.
3. Pass to the side your head is on.
