Front choke with your forearm under the opponent's chin while their head is under your armpit.

## Key steps

1. Catch the head as they shoot or bend forward.
2. Wrap the arm under their chin, blade of the wrist into the throat.
3. Grip your wrist or use an arm-in grip.
4. Pull your elbow to your ribs, then close guard or sprawl and lift.

## Defense

Clear the choking hand from your throat and move to the side where your head is.

1. Two-on-one the choking wrist and pull it down.
2. Turn your chin toward their ribs.
3. Step or pass to the side your head is on.
4. Shoulder pressure into their jaw to relieve the choke.

## Notes

Also from closed guard and [[Top front headlock]].
