Choke from the back using the opponent's own lapel as the strangling grip.

## Key steps

1. Pull out their far lapel and pass it to your choking hand.
2. Feed the lapel across their neck.
3. Grab behind their head or their far arm with your other hand.
4. Pull the lapel back while driving their head forward.

## Defense

Strip the collar grip and stop the opponent getting the angle for the bow and arrow or lapel choke.

1. Grab the choking hand at the wrist and pull it down.
2. Keep your leg hidden from the pants grip.
3. Turn toward the choking hand's side.
4. Bring your back to the mat.
