Invert under the opponent from De La Riva to off-balance them and roll to their back.

## Key steps

1. DLR hook with a belt or pants grip on the far side.
2. Off-balance them so they sit.
3. Invert, using the DLR hook to steer their hips.
4. Come up behind them and take the back.
