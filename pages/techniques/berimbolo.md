Invert under the opponent from De La Riva to off-balance them and roll to their back.

## Key steps

1. DLR hook with a belt or pants grip on the far side.
2. Off-balance them so they sit.
3. Invert, using the DLR hook to steer their hips.
4. Come up behind them and take the back.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom De La Riva guard
- Your outside leg is hooked around their lead leg (the De La Riva hook), the other foot on their hip or far leg, and you hold their ankle and their belt or far pants.

### 1. DLR hook with a belt or pants grip on the far side
- Keep the De La Riva hook deep around their lead leg and get a grip on their belt or the far pant leg, reaching across behind their knee.
- The far-side grip is what steers them later.

### 2. Off-balance them so they sit
- Pull with your grips and kick with the hook so their weight goes back and they sit down or fall onto their butt.

### 3. Invert, using the DLR hook to steer their hips
- Roll onto your shoulders, swinging your hips over and under them, keeping the DLR hook in to turn their hips away from you.
- Keep your chin tucked and roll across your shoulders, not your neck.

### 4. Come up behind them and take the back
- As your hips come through, your legs reach behind them; come up behind them, hooks in, and seatbelt.

### Common mistakes
- **Inverting before they sit:** a standing opponent just steps away. Off-balance them first.
- **Letting go of the far grip:** you lose the steering and end up under them.

## Defense

Keep your hips square and your weight back so they can't tip you, and clear the De La Riva hook before they invert.

1. Keep your weight back and your base wide when they grip your belt or pants.
2. Kill the De La Riva hook by pushing the knee down or stepping back out of it.
3. If they invert, sit back toward them and block their hips with your hands.
4. Turn to face them as they come up so they can't reach your back.
