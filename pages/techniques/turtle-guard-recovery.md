Roll or sit out from turtle to put your legs back between you and the opponent.

## Key steps

1. Keep elbows tight to knees and the neck protected.
2. Post on one foot toward the side they're on.
3. Roll over your shoulder or sit through.
4. Bring your knees in and recover guard.

## Defense

Stay on their hips and don't reach under their arms.

1. Keep your chest on their back.
2. Block the leg they post on.
3. Follow the roll and stay on top.
