Come up on your knees with the underhook from half guard and circle to the opponent's back.

## Key steps

1. Win the underhook and come up to your knees.
2. Keep your head tight to their chest.
3. Circle behind them, using the underhook to lift.
4. Seatbelt and insert hooks.

## Defense

Crossface and whizzer to stop the underhook, then flatten them.

1. Whizzer over the underhook arm.
2. Crossface to turn their head away.
3. Drive them flat on their back.
