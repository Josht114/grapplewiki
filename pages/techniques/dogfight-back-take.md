Come up on your knees with the underhook from half guard and circle to the opponent's back.

## Key steps

1. Win the underhook and come up to your knees.
2. Keep your head tight to their chest.
3. Circle behind them, using the underhook to lift.
4. Seatbelt and insert hooks.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom half guard
- You're on your side in half guard and you've won the underhook on their far side.

### 1. Win the underhook and come up to your knees
- Drive the underhook deep under their armpit and come up onto your knees beside them, your trapped-leg side still hooked: the dogfight position.

### 2. Keep your head tight to their chest
- Your head stays pressed into their chest or under their chin, higher than theirs, so they can't push it down or guillotine you.

### 3. Circle behind them, using the underhook to lift
- Lift with the underhook and walk your knees around behind them, keeping your chest tight to their side.

### 4. Seatbelt and insert hooks
- Once you're behind, lock a seatbelt, put your hooks in, and settle into back control.

### Common mistakes
- **Head below theirs:** they sprawl and guillotine or whizzer you flat.
- **Letting go of the underhook to grab:** they turn into you.

## Defense

Crossface and whizzer to stop the underhook, then flatten them.

1. Whizzer over the underhook arm.
2. Crossface to turn their head away.
3. Drive them flat on their back.
