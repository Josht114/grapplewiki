Arm-in choke threaded under the near arm and around the neck, locked with a gable or biceps grip.

## Key steps

1. Thread your arm under their near arm and across the neck.
2. Lock onto your other biceps.
3. Walk toward their head and flatten them out.
4. Squeeze, sprawling your hips to the mat.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: the front headlock
- They're on their knees or all fours in front of you.
- Sprawl your hips back and put your chest on the back of their head and shoulders, so their weight is on their hands.
- Turn their head to one side of your body, so it sits under one of your armpits (say your right).
- Their arm next to the middle of your body (their right arm in this example) is the **near arm**. That's the arm you'll trap.

### 1. Thread your arm under their near arm and across the neck
- Shoot your left arm (the one away from their head) under their near arm's armpit.
- Keep threading in front of their throat until your hand comes out by your right armpit, on the far side of their neck.
- Thread deep: your biceps should end up against their neck, not your forearm.
- Your right hand stays on their back or head, keeping the weight on them.

### 2. Lock onto your other biceps
- Grip your right biceps with your left hand.
- Put your right hand on their back, or behind your own head, like the end of a rear naked choke.
- Their head and near arm are now both trapped inside your arms. The choke comes from their own shoulder pressing one side of the neck and your arm the other.
- If you can't reach your biceps, a gable grip (palm to palm) works for now. Upgrade it once they're flat.

### 3. Walk toward their head and flatten them out
- Keep the lock and walk your feet around toward the side their head is on.
- That turns them onto their side or flattens them on their belly.
- Keep your chest heavy on their back the whole time so they can't square up or pull the arm out.

### 4. Squeeze, sprawling your hips to the mat
- Drop your hips to the mat and sprawl back, as if you're lying on them.
- Squeeze your elbows together and drive your shoulder into their trapped arm.
- From a turtle you can also finish by falling to your side, or rolling them toward their trapped arm and finishing on your side.
- Sinking your hips and closing your elbows is what finishes it, not pulling with your arms.

### Common mistakes
- **Threading too shallow:** with only your wrist across the throat it's a weak neck crank. Get your biceps to the neck before you lock.
- **Letting them square up:** if their hips turn back toward you, they can pull the arm out. Keep walking and keep your weight on them.
- **Squeezing with your arms alone:** that tires you out. Use your hips, chest and shoulder.

### If it fails
- If they pull the arm free, switch to a [[Guillotine (front headlock)]] or [[Anaconda choke]], or go behind them with the [[Go-behind (front headlock)]].

## Defense

Get your trapped arm and head free before the grip locks, and never roll for them.

1. Pull your trapped arm out and down.
2. Turn your head away from the choking arm.
3. Square your hips and drive into them.
4. Don't roll: that tightens the anaconda.
