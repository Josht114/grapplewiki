Guillotine finished from the front headlock, either high-elbow (arm out) or arm-in.

## Key steps

1. Wrap your arm under their chin from the front headlock.
2. Grip your wrist, or your own biceps for an arm-in version.
3. Sprawl your hips back to keep their weight on their knees.
4. Lift your elbow and arch to finish, or pull guard to tighten.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: the front headlock
- They're bent forward with their head under your armpit and your chest on their back.

### 1. Wrap your arm under their chin from the front headlock
- Slide your forearm under their chin from the side of their head, so the bony edge of your wrist is across the throat.
- Keep their head tight in your armpit while you do it.

### 2. Grip your wrist, or your own biceps for an arm-in version
- No arm inside: grab your choking wrist with your other hand.
- Arm-in: if one of their arms is inside with the head, grab your own biceps or make a gable grip.

### 3. Sprawl your hips back to keep their weight on their knees
- Sprawl so they can't stand up into you or drive forward.
- Their weight stays on their knees and hands, where they can't defend well.

### 4. Lift your elbow and arch to finish, or pull guard to tighten
- From standing or kneeling: lift your choking elbow up toward the ceiling and arch your back.
- Or jump or sit to guard, close your legs around their waist and stretch them away with your legs while you pull your elbow to your ribs.

### Common mistakes
- **Choking with the forearm flat:** use the blade of the wrist, high under the chin.
- **Pulling guard too far from them:** stay close or the space lets their head pop out.

## Defense

Clear the choking arm from your neck and keep your head moving toward the choking side.

1. Two-on-one the choking wrist and pull it down.
2. Turn your chin into their ribs.
3. Square up and drive your shoulder into their jaw.
