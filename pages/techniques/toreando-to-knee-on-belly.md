Finish a toreando pass straight into knee on belly instead of side control.

## Key steps

1. Push their legs aside with the toreando grips.
2. Circle around to their side.
3. Drop your knee onto their belly.
4. Post the far leg wide.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: in their open guard
- You're standing with toreando grips on their pants.

### 1. Push their legs aside with the toreando grips
- Throw their legs to one side and pin them to the mat.

### 2. Circle around to their side
- Step quickly around to the opposite side from their legs.

### 3. Drop your knee onto their belly
- Instead of dropping your chest, drop your near knee across their belly, shin angled, toes behind.

### 4. Post the far leg wide
- Post your far leg out wide for balance and grip the far collar or shoulder. You're on knee on belly.

### Common mistakes
- **Dropping the knee slowly:** they turn in before it lands. Do it as you land.
- **Far leg close to them:** they push you off balance.

## Defense

Turn into them as they circle and push the knee down.

1. Keep your feet in front.
2. Turn your hips toward them.
3. Frame on the knee.
