Finish a toreando pass straight into knee on belly instead of side control.

## Key steps

1. Push their legs aside with the toreando grips.
2. Circle around to their side.
3. Drop your knee onto their belly.
4. Post the far leg wide.

## Defense

Turn into them as they circle and push the knee down.

1. Keep your feet in front.
2. Turn your hips toward them.
3. Frame on the knee.
