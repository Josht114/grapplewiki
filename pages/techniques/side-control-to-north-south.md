Walk around the opponent's head while keeping chest-to-chest pressure.

## Key steps

1. Keep your chest on theirs and your hips low.
2. Walk your feet around past their head.
3. Keep their arms away from their head as you go.
4. Settle head-to-hips in north south.

## Defense

Turn into them as they walk around so they can't settle.

1. Follow their hips by turning toward them.
2. Frame on their hips.
3. Recover guard as soon as space opens.
