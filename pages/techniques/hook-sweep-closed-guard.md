Open the guard, insert a hook under one thigh, and elevate the opponent over (also called the elevator sweep).

## Key steps

1. Break their posture and control an arm.
2. Open your guard and slide a hook under one thigh.
3. Pull them forward onto you.
4. Lift with the hook and roll into mount.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom closed guard
- They're kneeling in your guard.

### 1. Break their posture and control an arm
- Pull them forward and control one arm, so they can't post on that side.

### 2. Open your guard and slide a hook under one thigh
- Open your legs and slide one foot (a butterfly hook) inside their thigh on the side opposite the controlled arm.

### 3. Pull them forward onto you
- Pull them forward so their weight comes onto your chest and the hook.

### 4. Lift with the hook and roll into mount
- Fall to the side of the controlled arm and lift with the hook; they roll over, and you follow them into mount.

### Common mistakes
- **Lifting while their weight is back:** pull them onto you first.

## Defense

Keep your posture and base wide when the guard opens.

1. Widen your knees.
2. Post on the free side.
3. Kill the hook.
