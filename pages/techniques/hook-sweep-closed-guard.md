Open the guard, insert a hook under one thigh, and elevate the opponent over (also called the elevator sweep).

## Key steps

1. Break their posture and control an arm.
2. Open your guard and slide a hook under one thigh.
3. Pull them forward onto you.
4. Lift with the hook and roll into mount.

## Defense

Keep your posture and base wide when the guard opens.

1. Widen your knees.
2. Post on the free side.
3. Kill the hook.
