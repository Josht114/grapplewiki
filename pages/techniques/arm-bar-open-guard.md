Arm bar from open guard, usually off sleeve control or a spider guard.

## Key steps

1. Control their sleeve or wrist.
2. Put your foot on their hip and angle off.
3. Swing your leg over their head.
4. Pinch your knees, thumb up, and lift your hips.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom open guard
- You're on your back, feet on their hips or legs, with a grip on one of their sleeves or wrists.

### 1. Control their sleeve or wrist
- Hold one arm firmly at the sleeve or wrist and pull it toward your chest so it's straight-ish and can't escape.

### 2. Put your foot on their hip and angle off
- Put the foot on the same side as the trapped arm on their hip and push, swinging your body out to that side so you're at an angle to them.

### 3. Swing your leg over their head
- Swing your other leg up and over their head, so their arm is between your legs and your calf is on their face or neck.

### 4. Pinch your knees, thumb up, and lift your hips
- Hug the arm to your chest, pinch your knees together, turn their thumb toward the ceiling and lift your hips slowly.

### Common mistakes
- **Staying square under them:** you need the angle or the leg won't reach over the head.
- **Loose knees:** they pull the elbow out or stack you.

## Defense

Free your sleeve grips and keep your elbows inside your knees.

1. Strip the sleeve grip.
2. Elbows in.
3. Stack them and pull the arm out.
