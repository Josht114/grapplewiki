Arm bar from open guard, usually off sleeve control or a spider guard.

## Key steps

1. Control their sleeve or wrist.
2. Put your foot on their hip and angle off.
3. Swing your leg over their head.
4. Pinch your knees, thumb up, and lift your hips.

## Defense

Free your sleeve grips and keep your elbows inside your knees.

1. Strip the sleeve grip.
2. Elbows in.
3. Stack them and pull the arm out.
