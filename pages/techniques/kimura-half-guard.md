Kimura on the opponent's posted arm from bottom half guard.

## Key steps

1. Grab their wrist on the side they post.
2. Sit up and thread your arm over their shoulder.
3. Lock the figure-four on your own wrist.
4. Fall back and rotate the arm behind them.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom half guard
- They post a hand on the mat beside you to keep their base.

### 1. Grab their wrist on the side they post
- Grab the posted wrist with the hand on that side.

### 2. Sit up and thread your arm over their shoulder
- Sit up and reach your other arm over their shoulder and under their arm.

### 3. Lock the figure-four on your own wrist
- Grab your own wrist: the kimura grip.

### 4. Fall back and rotate the arm behind them
- Fall back and turn their hand up behind their back to finish, keeping their elbow bent.

### Common mistakes
- **Not sitting up enough:** your arm can't get over their shoulder.

## Defense

Keep your hands off the mat near their body, and grab your belt if they lock the grip.

1. Don't post with a straight arm.
2. Grab your belt or pants.
3. Drive your weight forward to flatten them.
