Circle from the front headlock to the opponent's side and back while keeping their head down.

## Key steps

1. Keep their head down with your chest and arm.
2. Block their near arm so they can't grab your leg.
3. Walk your feet around toward their hip.
4. Land with your chest on their back.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: the front headlock
- They're on their knees or bent over in front of you, your chest heavy on their head and upper back.

### 1. Keep their head down with your chest and arm
- Keep a front headlock or snap their head down with your chest and forearm.
- Their head stays below their hips, so they can't stand or look up at you.

### 2. Block their near arm so they can't grab your leg
- Control the arm on the side you're circling to, by the wrist or elbow, or pin it with your hip.
- If that arm is free, they'll grab your leg as you go behind and drive into you.

### 3. Walk your feet around toward their hip
- Circle in small steps, keeping your chest on them and your hips back.
- Don't let your weight come off their back while you move.

### 4. Land with your chest on their back
- Finish behind them, chest on their back and an arm around their waist or seatbelt-style.
- From here, attack the back or break them down to the mat.

### Common mistakes
- **Lifting your chest off them:** they come up into you and square up.
- **Big steps:** they turn with you. Small, quick steps, always keeping contact.

## Defense

Square up and keep your head up so they can't circle behind you.

1. Hand-fight the arm on your head.
2. Follow their movement by turning to face them.
3. Get your head up and posture back.
