Circle from the front headlock to the opponent's side and back while keeping their head down.

## Key steps

1. Keep their head down with your chest and arm.
2. Block their near arm so they can't grab your leg.
3. Walk your feet around toward their hip.
4. Land with your chest on their back.

## Defense

Square up and keep your head up so they can't circle behind you.

1. Hand-fight the arm on your head.
2. Follow their movement by turning to face them.
3. Get your head up and posture back.
