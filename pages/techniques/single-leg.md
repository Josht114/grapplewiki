Takedown that controls one leg, then finishes by running the pipe, tripping or lifting.

## Key steps

1. Level change and shoot to one leg.
2. Wrap the leg with your head on the inside of their chest.
3. Stand up with the leg between your knees.
4. Finish by running the pipe, tripping or lifting.

## Defense

Sprawl: Throw your legs back and drop your hips onto the opponent to stop a leg attack.

Once the shot is stopped, follow up with a [[Snap down]] to the [[Top front headlock]].

1. Underhooks or crossface as they shoot.
2. Kick your legs back and wide.
3. Drop your hips onto their shoulders.
4. Circle to their back or the [[Top front headlock]].
