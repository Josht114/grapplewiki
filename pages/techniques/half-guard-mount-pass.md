Free your trapped leg straight up into mount instead of passing to the side.

## Key steps

1. Flatten them with crossface and underhook.
2. Walk your free knee up toward their armpit.
3. Use your free foot to push their legs off your trapped foot.
4. Slide the foot out and settle in mount.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: top half guard
- One of your legs is trapped between theirs.

### 1. Flatten them with crossface and underhook
- Crossface their head and underhook their far arm so they're flat on their back.

### 2. Walk your free knee up toward their armpit
- Walk your free knee up high, toward their armpit, so your hips are over their chest.

### 3. Use your free foot to push their legs off your trapped foot
- Put your free foot on their knee or the top of their legs and push down to loosen their lock.

### 4. Slide the foot out and settle in mount
- Pull the trapped foot out, bring it up beside their hip, and settle into mount.

### Common mistakes
- **Doing it before they're flat:** they bump you off. Crossface and underhook first.

## Defense

Keep your legs locked tight and win the underhook.

1. Lock your feet and squeeze.
2. Get your underhook.
3. Bridge into them as they free the foot.
