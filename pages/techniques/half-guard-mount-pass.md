Free your trapped leg straight up into mount instead of passing to the side.

## Key steps

1. Flatten them with crossface and underhook.
2. Walk your free knee up toward their armpit.
3. Use your free foot to push their legs off your trapped foot.
4. Slide the foot out and settle in mount.

## Defense

Keep your legs locked tight and win the underhook.

1. Lock your feet and squeeze.
2. Get your underhook.
3. Bridge into them as they free the foot.
