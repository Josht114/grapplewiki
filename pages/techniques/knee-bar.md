Arm bar for the leg: hyperextends the knee by pulling the foot against your hips.

## Key steps

1. Isolate one leg and slide your knee between their legs.
2. Roll or fall to the side, hugging the leg.
3. Get the kneecap past your hips.
4. Pinch the knees, pull the foot to your chest and extend your hips.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: top half guard
- You have their leg isolated.

### 1. Isolate one leg and slide your knee between their legs
- Wrap one of their legs and slide your knee across between their legs, at their hip.

### 2. Roll or fall to the side, hugging the leg
- Fall to your side, hugging their leg to your chest, their heel by your head.

### 3. Get the kneecap past your hips
- Slide down so their kneecap sits just past your hips; that's the pivot point.

### 4. Pinch the knees, pull the foot to your chest and extend your hips
- Pinch your knees on their thigh, pull their foot to your chest and push your hips forward to straighten the leg.

### Common mistakes
- **Knee above your hips:** no lever. Get the knee past your hips.
- **Under IBJJF rules, check it's legal at your belt.**

## Defense

Keep your knees together and stop the kneecap passing their hips.

1. Keep your knees together.
2. Turn toward them as they fall.
3. Bend the leg to keep the knee line protected.
4. Pull the leg out.
