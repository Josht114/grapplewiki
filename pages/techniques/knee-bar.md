Arm bar for the leg: hyperextends the knee by pulling the foot against your hips.

## Key steps

1. Isolate one leg and slide your knee between their legs.
2. Roll or fall to the side, hugging the leg.
3. Get the kneecap past your hips.
4. Pinch the knees, pull the foot to your chest and extend your hips.

## Defense

Keep your knees together and stop the kneecap passing their hips.

1. Keep your knees together.
2. Turn toward them as they fall.
3. Bend the leg to keep the knee line protected.
4. Pull the leg out.
