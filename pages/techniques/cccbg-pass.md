Passing against the cross collar, cuff and bicep guard.

## Key steps

1. Strip the cuff grip before anything else.
2. Posture up and free your collar grip.
3. Pin the foot on your bicep to the mat.
4. Step around to the side of the pinned leg and drop to side control.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: top of the CCCBG guard
- They hold your collar (cross collar), your sleeve cuff, and have a foot on your bicep. Gi only.

### 1. Strip the cuff grip before anything else
- Break the sleeve grip on your arm first by circling your hand and pulling it free, or gripping their wrist and pushing it off.
- With the cuff grip gone, the foot on the bicep can't control your arm.

### 2. Posture up and free your collar grip
- Stand tall or post up and break their collar grip, by pushing their wrist down or circling your hand inside.

### 3. Pin the foot on your bicep to the mat
- Push the leg that was on your bicep down and across to the mat, gripping their pants at the knee.

### 4. Step around to the side of the pinned leg and drop to side control
- Step around on that side, keep the leg pinned, and drop your chest to side control.

### Common mistakes
- **Trying to pass with the grips still on:** they pull you into a sweep or triangle.

## Defense

Keep the cuff and collar grips tight and keep your foot on their bicep so they can't posture up and step around.

1. Grip deep in the collar and keep the cuff grip.
2. Keep your foot pushing on their bicep.
3. Pull them in and off balance when they try to stand.
4. Re-pummel your foot onto the bicep if they free it.
