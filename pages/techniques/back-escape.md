Protect the neck, slide your back to the mat on the side of the bottom hook, and turn in.

## Key steps

1. Two-on-one the choking arm and keep your chin down.
2. Slide your shoulders to the mat on the side of their bottom hook.
3. Clear the top hook with your hand or leg.
4. Turn in and end on top in their half guard.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom back mount
- They have your back.

### 1. Two-on-one the choking arm and keep your chin down
- Two hands on the choking arm, chin tucked.

### 2. Slide your shoulders to the mat on the side of their bottom hook
- Scoot your shoulders to the mat on the bottom-hook side.

### 3. Clear the top hook with your hand or leg
- Push their top hook off your hip with your hand or leg.

### 4. Turn in and end on top in their half guard
- Turn in toward them, chest to chest; they're on their back with one leg of yours trapped: you're on top in their half guard.

### Common mistakes
- **Going to the wrong side:** escape on the bottom-hook side.

## Defense

Lock a body triangle and follow their hips as they slide.

1. Body triangle instead of hooks.
2. Follow their hips toward the mat.
3. Switch to mount as they turn.
