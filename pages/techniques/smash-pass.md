Fold both legs to one side and smash them with hip pressure, then pass around.

## Key steps

1. Fold both their legs to one side.
2. Drive your hip into the back of their legs.
3. Keep your chest heavy so they can't turn in.
4. Walk around and settle in side control.

## Defense

Stopping pressure passes by keeping your knees between you and the opponent and refusing to be flattened.

1. Stay on your side, never flat.
2. Frame on the head and shoulders.
3. Keep your knees connected to your elbows.
4. Pummel your legs back inside when they clear one.
