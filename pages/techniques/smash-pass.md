Fold both legs to one side and smash them with hip pressure, then pass around.

## Key steps

1. Fold both their legs to one side.
2. Drive your hip into the back of their legs.
3. Keep your chest heavy so they can't turn in.
4. Walk around and settle in side control.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: in their open guard
- You're close to them with both their knees together, often after a leg drag or a failed guard.

### 1. Fold both their legs to one side
- Push both knees over to one side, so their legs lie together on the mat and their hips turn.

### 2. Drive your hip into the back of their legs
- Drop your hip onto the back of their thighs, pinning their legs to the mat.

### 3. Keep your chest heavy so they can't turn in
- Put your chest on theirs, or a crossface, so their upper body can't turn back toward you.

### 4. Walk around and settle in side control
- Walk your legs around away from their legs and settle into side control.

### Common mistakes
- **Pinning legs but not upper body:** they turn their shoulders in and recover.
- **Leaving space at the hips:** they slip a knee back in.

## Defense

Stopping pressure passes by keeping your knees between you and the opponent and refusing to be flattened.

1. Stay on your side, never flat.
2. Frame on the head and shoulders.
3. Keep your knees connected to your elbows.
4. Pummel your legs back inside when they clear one.
