Head and arm choke from side control, using the opponent's own shoulder to close the neck.

## Key steps

1. Push their far arm across their face and put your head on it.
2. Wrap your arm behind their neck and lock your hands.
3. Walk your hips out toward their head to the far side.
4. Drop your weight and squeeze your shoulder into their neck.

## Defense

Keep your arm from being pushed across your face, or turn into them before the grip locks.

1. Keep your elbows tight and low.
2. If the arm crosses, grab your own head or collar with that hand.
3. Turn into them to make space at your neck.
