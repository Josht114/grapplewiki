Head and arm choke from side control, using the opponent's own shoulder to close the neck.

## Key steps

1. Push their far arm across their face and put your head on it.
2. Wrap your arm behind their neck and lock your hands.
3. Walk your hips out toward their head to the far side.
4. Drop your weight and squeeze your shoulder into their neck.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: side control
- Chest to chest, crossface arm under their head.

### 1. Push their far arm across their face and put your head on it
- When they push on your neck with the far arm, push that arm across their face.
- Pin it there with the side of your head, so their arm and head are trapped together.

### 2. Wrap your arm behind their neck and lock your hands
- Your crossface arm wraps behind their neck. Lock hands palm to palm, or grab your other biceps.

### 3. Walk your hips out toward their head to the far side
- Step over to the far side, or walk your hips around toward their head so you're at a sharp angle.
- Your chest drops toward the mat on their far side.

### 4. Drop your weight and squeeze your shoulder into their neck
- Squeeze your elbows together and drive your shoulder into the side of their neck.
- The pressure of their own shoulder closes the choke.

### Common mistakes
- **Staying square on their chest:** you need the angle out to the far side to finish.
- **Not sealing the arm:** if their arm slips back across, the choke is gone.

## Defense

Keep your arm from being pushed across your face, or turn into them before the grip locks.

1. Keep your elbows tight and low.
2. If the arm crosses, grab your own head or collar with that hand.
3. Turn into them to make space at your neck.
