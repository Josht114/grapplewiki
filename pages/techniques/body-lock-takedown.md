From the body lock, pull the opponent's hips in and trip or sit them down.

## Key steps

1. Lock hands tight around their waist.
2. Pull their hips into you and step to the side.
3. Block a leg with your foot (outside trip).
4. Drive them down and land in top half guard.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: body lock
- You're standing with your hands locked around their waist, chest to chest or from the side.

### 1. Lock hands tight around their waist
- Lock low, around the hips, with a gable or wrist grip. The lower the lock, the more control of their hips.

### 2. Pull their hips into you and step to the side
- Pull their hips tight into yours, arch, and step to the side so you're at an angle to them.

### 3. Block a leg with your foot (outside trip)
- Put your foot or lower leg behind their leg on the side you've stepped to, blocking it.

### 4. Drive them down and land in top half guard
- Drive your chest into them over the blocked leg so they fall back; follow them down and land on top, usually in half guard.

### Common mistakes
- **Lock high on the ribs:** they move their hips away. Lock low.
- **Driving straight forward:** they step back. Trip the leg first.

## Defense

Get your hips away and break the grip before they can pull you in and trip you.

1. Push their head or shoulders away and sprawl your hips back.
2. Get an underhook or whizzer to stop the lock tightening.
3. Break the grip by pushing down on their wrists.
4. Keep your feet wide and step away from the trip.
