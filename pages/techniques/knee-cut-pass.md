Slide your knee across the opponent's thigh while controlling the upper body.

## Key steps

1. Control the near leg's pant or knee.
2. Cut your knee across their thigh toward the mat.
3. Get an underhook or crossface.
4. Free the trailing foot and settle into side control.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: in their open guard
- You're on your knees or up on one knee between their legs, with grips on their pants or knee.

### 1. Control the near leg's pant or knee
- Grip the pant leg at the knee (or the knee itself) of the leg nearest your cutting knee and pin it to the mat.

### 2. Cut your knee across their thigh toward the mat
- Slide your knee diagonally across their thigh, just above their knee, until it touches the mat on the other side.
- Keep your hips low and heavy as you cut.

### 3. Get an underhook or crossface
- As you cut, get an underhook on their far arm or a crossface on their head, so they can't turn into you.

### 4. Free the trailing foot and settle into side control
- Pull your back leg free from between their legs and settle chest to chest.

### Common mistakes
- **No upper-body control:** they turn in, get an underhook, and come up on you.
- **Cutting with your hips high:** they get a knee shield or half guard back.

## Defense

Stopping pressure passes by keeping your knees between you and the opponent and refusing to be flattened.

1. Stay on your side, never flat.
2. Frame on the head and shoulders.
3. Keep your knees connected to your elbows.
4. Pummel your legs back inside when they clear one.
