Takedown that attacks both legs, driving through the opponent.

## Key steps

1. Set up with a level change.
2. Penetration step deep between their feet.
3. Wrap behind both knees with your head on their side.
4. Drive forward and turn the corner to land on top.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: standing
- You're facing them at a distance where you can shoot.

### 1. Set up with a level change
- Drop your hips with your back straight, often after a fake to the head, so they bring their hands up.

### 2. Penetration step deep between their feet
- Step your lead foot deep between their feet, knee toward the mat, hips under you.

### 3. Wrap behind both knees with your head on their side
- Wrap both arms behind their knees, your head on the outside of their body against their ribs.

### 4. Drive forward and turn the corner to land on top
- Drive forward with your legs, and turn toward your head's side so they fall to the mat.
- Land past their legs, ready for side control.

### Common mistakes
- **Shooting from too far:** they sprawl on your back. Close the distance first.
- **Head in the middle of their chest:** they guillotine you. Head to the side.

## Defense

Sprawl: Throw your legs back and drop your hips onto the opponent to stop a leg attack.

Once the shot is stopped, follow up with a [[Snap down]] to the [[Top front headlock]].

1. Underhooks or crossface as they shoot.
2. Kick your legs back and wide.
3. Drop your hips onto their shoulders.
4. Circle to their back or the [[Top front headlock]].
