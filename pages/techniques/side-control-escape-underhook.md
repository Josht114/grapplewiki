Bridge and win the underhook, then turn to your knees and come up into a wrestling scramble or single leg.

## Key steps

1. Frame and bridge to lift their hips.
2. Pummel your near arm into an underhook.
3. Turn in toward them and come up to your knees.
4. Drive into a single leg or stand up.

## Defense

Win the underhook battle and crossface so they can't turn to their knees.

1. Deny the underhook with a whizzer.
2. Crossface.
3. Stay heavy on their chest.
