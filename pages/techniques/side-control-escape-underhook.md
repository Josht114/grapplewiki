Bridge and win the underhook, then turn to your knees and come up into a wrestling scramble or single leg.

## Key steps

1. Frame and bridge to lift their hips.
2. Pummel your near arm into an underhook.
3. Turn in toward them and come up to your knees.
4. Drive into a single leg or stand up.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom side control
- They're on top, not yet controlling your near arm.

### 1. Frame and bridge to lift their hips
- Frame on their hip and neck and bridge to lift their weight.

### 2. Pummel your near arm into an underhook
- Swim your near arm under their arm into an underhook.

### 3. Turn in toward them and come up to your knees
- Turn onto your side toward them, then up to your knees, using the underhook to lift.

### 4. Drive into a single leg or stand up
- Drive into their leg for a single leg, or keep turning and stand up.

### Common mistakes
- **Coming up with your head low:** they snap you into a front headlock or guillotine.

## Defense

Win the underhook battle and crossface so they can't turn to their knees.

1. Deny the underhook with a whizzer.
2. Crossface.
3. Stay heavy on their chest.
