Taking the fight to the ground by sitting or jumping into your guard.

## Key steps

1. Get grips first: collar and sleeve, or a collar tie.
2. Put one foot on their hip.
3. Sit down close to them, not far away.
4. Close your guard as you land.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: standing
- You're facing them and you want to play guard.

### 1. Get grips first: collar and sleeve, or a collar tie
- Never pull guard without grips; they keep them close so they can't step away and pass.

### 2. Put one foot on their hip
- Put one foot on their hip (the other side to your sleeve grip) to control the distance.

### 3. Sit down close to them, not far away
- Sit your butt down right in front of their feet, close, still holding your grips.

### 4. Close your guard as you land
- As your back reaches the mat, wrap your legs around their waist and lock your ankles.

### Common mistakes
- **Jumping and landing far away:** they walk straight past your legs.
- **Pulling with no grips:** they stand and pass.

## Defense

Follow them down and start passing immediately.

1. Keep your posture as they sit.
2. Get grips on their knees.
3. Pass before the guard closes.
