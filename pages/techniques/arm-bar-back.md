Arm bar when the opponent defends the choke by bringing their arms high.

## Key steps

1. Clasp their wrist as they defend the choke with their arms high.
2. Swing your leg over their face, keeping a hook in.
3. Slide out to the side so you're perpendicular to them.
4. Pinch your knees, thumb up, and lift your hips.

## Defense

Keep your arms tight to your body when defending chokes so they can't be isolated.

1. Clasp your hands together.
2. Keep your elbows on your ribs.
3. Turn into the attacking side.
4. Pull the elbow out as they fall back.
