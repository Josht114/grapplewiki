Walk back around to side control, keeping chest pressure.

## Key steps

1. Keep your chest heavy on theirs.
2. Walk your feet around toward their side, keeping their arms controlled.
3. Settle with crossface and underhook.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: north south
- Chest on chest, head toward their hips.

### 1. Keep your chest heavy on theirs
- Keep all your weight on their chest as you start to move.

### 2. Walk your feet around toward their side, keeping their arms controlled
- Walk round toward one side, keeping their arms from framing between you.
- Pivot on your chest rather than stepping big.

### 3. Settle with crossface and underhook
- Once you're across their body, crossface their head and underhook their far arm, hips low.

### Common mistakes
- **Lifting up to walk:** they turn in and get a knee in front of you.

## Defense

Turn into them as they move to recover guard.

1. Frame on their hips.
2. Turn your hips toward them.
3. Bring your knee in.
