Collar choke against the turtle, finished by walking your legs around the opponent's head like a clock hand.

## Key steps

1. Feed a deep collar grip under their neck.
2. Block their arm with your other hand.
3. Sprawl your weight onto their back.
4. Walk your legs around toward their head to tighten.

## Defense

Block the collar grip, and turn or sit out toward the choke before they walk around.

1. Keep your elbows tight and your collar covered.
2. Grab the choking wrist.
3. Turn toward the choking side as they walk.
4. Sit out or roll to guard.
