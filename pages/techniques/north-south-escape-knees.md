Turn onto your knees when the opponent's weight comes off your chest.

## Key steps

1. Frame on their hips.
2. Turn onto your side.
3. Come up to your knees.
4. Keep elbows tight in turtle.

## Defense

Stay heavy and follow them to the back or side control.

1. Keep your chest on theirs.
2. Follow as they turn.
3. Take the back or break them down.
