Shoulder throw: turn in under the opponent, load them onto your back and throw them over your shoulder.

## Key steps

1. Grip their sleeve and lapel, or an underarm grip.
2. Step in and turn your back to them, knees bent.
3. Pull them onto your back.
4. Straighten your legs and throw them over your shoulder.

## Defense

Keep your hips back and lower than theirs so they can't load you.

1. Drop your hips as they turn in.
2. Push their hips away with your hand.
3. Step around their hip.
