Shoulder throw: turn in under the opponent, load them onto your back and throw them over your shoulder.

## Key steps

1. Grip their sleeve and lapel, or an underarm grip.
2. Step in and turn your back to them, knees bent.
3. Pull them onto your back.
4. Straighten your legs and throw them over your shoulder.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: standing
- You have a sleeve grip and a lapel grip (gi), or an underarm grip.

### 1. Grip their sleeve and lapel, or an underarm grip
- The sleeve hand will pull them forward. The other hand holds the lapel, or that arm goes under their armpit (ippon seoi nage).

### 2. Step in and turn your back to them, knees bent
- Step your front foot across between their feet, then pivot so your back is against their chest.
- Bend your knees so your hips are lower than theirs. This is the most important part.

### 3. Pull them onto your back
- Pull the sleeve forward and around, so their chest lands on your back and their weight comes onto their toes.

### 4. Straighten your legs and throw them over your shoulder
- Straighten your legs and bend forward, turning your head toward the sleeve side, so they go over your shoulder.
- Keep hold of the sleeve and follow them down to side control.

### Common mistakes
- **Hips higher than theirs:** you can't lift them; they just stand on you. Get low.
- **Turning in without pulling first:** their weight stays back and they block with their hips.

## Defense

Keep your hips back and lower than theirs so they can't load you.

1. Drop your hips as they turn in.
2. Push their hips away with your hand.
3. Step around their hip.
