Drag the opponent's legs across your body and pin their hips sideways.

## Key steps

1. Grip their pants at the knee.
2. Drag their legs across your body to one side.
3. Pin their hip to the mat with your knee or hip.
4. Walk around to side control.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: in their open guard
- You're standing or kneeling at their legs, gripping their pants.

### 1. Grip their pants at the knee
- Grip one pant leg at the knee (no-gi: behind the knee or at the ankle).

### 2. Drag their legs across your body to one side
- Pull that leg across your body to the other side, so their legs end up stacked together on one side of you.
- Step your near leg in behind their hips as you drag.

### 3. Pin their hip to the mat with your knee or hip
- Put your hip or knee against their hip on the mat so they can't turn back toward you.
- Their legs point away from you and their back is half turned.

### 4. Walk around to side control
- Keep the hip pinned and walk your chest up to theirs, settling into side control.

### Common mistakes
- **Dragging without pinning the hip:** their legs swing back in front of you.
- **Standing too tall:** get your weight down onto their hip.

## Defense

Stopping pressure passes by keeping your knees between you and the opponent and refusing to be flattened.

1. Stay on your side, never flat.
2. Frame on the head and shoulders.
3. Keep your knees connected to your elbows.
4. Pummel your legs back inside when they clear one.
