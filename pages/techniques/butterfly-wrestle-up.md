Post a hand and come up on a single leg from butterfly guard.

## Key steps

1. Get an underhook.
2. Post on your free hand.
3. Come up to your knees on a single leg.
4. Drive through to finish on top.

## Defense

Sprawl your hips back and crossface.

1. Kick your legs back.
2. Crossface.
3. Whizzer the underhook.
