Post a hand and come up on a single leg from butterfly guard.

## Key steps

1. Get an underhook.
2. Post on your free hand.
3. Come up to your knees on a single leg.
4. Drive through to finish on top.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: bottom butterfly guard
- You're sitting up with butterfly hooks and an underhook.

### 1. Get an underhook
- Pummel an arm under their armpit and keep your head tight to their chest.

### 2. Post on your free hand
- Post your other hand on the mat behind you.

### 3. Come up to your knees on a single leg
- Push off the post and come up onto your knees, catching one of their legs.

### 4. Drive through to finish on top
- Drive forward into them and finish the single leg, coming up on top.

### Common mistakes
- **Coming up without the underhook:** they sprawl on you.

## Defense

Sprawl your hips back and crossface.

1. Kick your legs back.
2. Crossface.
3. Whizzer the underhook.
