From turtle, overhook the arm the top player has around you and roll to that side, taking them over you so you land on top. Wrestlers call this an arm roll; if you also grab their leg it becomes a Peterson roll.

## Key steps

1. Wait for them to reach an arm around your waist or chest.
2. Overhook that arm tight to your body at the elbow.
3. Drop your shoulder on that side, tuck your head and roll toward the trapped arm.
4. They go over you; follow through and come up chest to chest on top.

## Defense

Don't reach an arm around their body without your elbow tight; keep your weight back.

1. Keep your elbow tight to their back.
2. Don't overcommit your weight.
3. Pull the arm free if it gets overhooked.

## Notes

Not the same as the mount escape [[Trap and roll (upa)]], which also uses the name "trap and roll".
