Sleeve choke: one hand inside your own sleeve, the other hand's edge across the throat.

## Key steps

1. Feed one arm behind their head and grab inside your opposite sleeve.
2. Bring the free hand across their throat.
3. Drive the blade of the hand down as you pull with the sleeve grip.

## Defense

Stop the hand from crossing your throat and escape while their arms are tied up.

1. Tuck your chin and block the free hand.
2. Push the choking elbow across.
3. Bridge while their hands are busy.
4. Elbow-knee escape.

## Notes

Works from [[Top mount]] and even from inside someone's guard. In no-gi, grip your own wrist or forearm instead of the sleeve.
