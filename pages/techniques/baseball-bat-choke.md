Gi choke using two same-direction collar grips like holding a bat, finished by rotating around the opponent's head.

## Key steps

1. Get one hand deep in the collar, thumb inside.
2. Put the other hand close by with the thumb out.
3. your arms are not crossed here
4. Bring your head down and spin toward their head.
5. Rotate until the choke closes.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: knee on belly
- You're on knee on belly with them on their back, collar within reach. Gi only.

### 1. Get one hand deep in the collar, thumb inside
- Feed one hand deep into the collar near their neck, thumb inside.

### 2. Put the other hand close by with the thumb out
- Grab the same collar a few inches away with the other hand, thumb outside. Both hands hold it like a baseball bat.

### 3. Your arms are not crossed here
- Both grips are on one side of the neck, side by side; your arms don't cross like a cross collar choke.

### 4. Bring your head down and spin toward their head
- Drop your head to the mat and step around toward their head, rotating your body.

### 5. Rotate until the choke closes
- Keep turning; the grips twist the collar into a tight loop around their neck.

### Common mistakes
- **Spinning before the grips are deep:** the collar slides and nothing tightens.
- **Lifting your weight off them:** they turn into you and escape.

## Defense

Strip one of the grips and turn with them so the choke can't close.

1. Spot the two same-direction grips early.
2. Strip the nearest hand.
3. Turn with them as they spin.
4. Recover guard.
