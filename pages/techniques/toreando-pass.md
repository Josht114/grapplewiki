Standing speed pass: control both knees, redirect the legs to one side and circle to side control.

## Key steps

1. Grip both pant legs at the knees.
2. Push the legs to one side and down to the mat.
3. Step around on the opposite side.
4. Drop your chest to side control before they recover.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: in their open guard
- You're standing in front of them, their feet on you or in front of you.

### 1. Grip both pant legs at the knees
- Grip their pants just below the knees (no-gi: grip the knees or shins). Keep your elbows in and your arms firm.

### 2. Push the legs to one side and down to the mat
- Throw both legs to one side and push them down to the mat, like a bullfighter moving the cape.

### 3. Step around on the opposite side
- At once, step quickly around the other way, past their feet, toward their head.

### 4. Drop your chest to side control before they recover
- Drop your chest onto them and settle into side control, pushing their knees away as you land.

### Common mistakes
- **Pausing after the push:** they swing their legs back. Push and step in one movement.
- **Letting them hook your legs:** keep your feet away from their hooks as you circle.

## Defense

Using frames, hip movement and leg pummeling to stop the opponent from passing your guard.

See [[Frames]], [[Pressure]] and [[Technical stand-up]].

1. Keep your knees and elbows connected (no gaps).
2. Face the opponent: turn your hips toward them as they move.
3. Frame on hips, shoulders or biceps with hands and feet.
4. Pummel your legs back inside when they clear one.
