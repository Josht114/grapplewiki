Establish the seatbelt grip on the turtle and roll or insert hooks to take the back.

## Key steps

1. Seatbelt: one arm over the shoulder, one under the armpit, hands locked.
2. Chest glued to their back.
3. Insert the near hook.
4. Roll to the side of the under-arm and insert the second hook.

## Defense

Keep your elbows tight and turn into them before the hooks go in.

1. Hand-fight the seatbelt.
2. Keep elbows to knees.
3. Turn into them and recover guard.
