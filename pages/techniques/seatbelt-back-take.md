Establish the seatbelt grip on the turtle and roll or insert hooks to take the back.

## Key steps

1. Seatbelt: one arm over the shoulder, one under the armpit, hands locked.
2. Glue your chest to their back.
3. Insert the near hook.
4. Roll to your underhook side and insert the second hook.

## Detailed breakdown

### Start: top of turtle
- They're turtled; you're at their side or behind them.

### 1. Seatbelt: one arm over the shoulder, one under the armpit, hands locked
- One arm goes over their shoulder (this becomes the choking arm), the other under their armpit on the other side.
- Lock your hands on their chest, the top hand over the bottom.

### 2. Glue your chest to their back
- Press your chest into their upper back so there's no gap. Space lets them spin or sit through.

### 3. Insert the near hook
- Slide your near foot in front of their hip on your side, heel in.

### 4. Roll to your underhook side and insert the second hook
- Fall onto your side toward your underhook arm (the one under their armpit), taking them with you.
- As they come over, put your second foot in front of their other hip. You're now in back control.

### Common mistakes
- **Rolling to the overhook side:** you land under their weight with nothing.
- **Reaching for hooks before the seatbelt:** they just stand up or turn.

## Defense

Keep your elbows tight and turn into them before the hooks go in.

1. Hand-fight the seatbelt.
2. Keep elbows to knees.
3. Turn into them and recover guard.
